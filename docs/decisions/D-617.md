## D-617 · Lab games and features change nothing players see until on for all
**Date:** 2026-11-24 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** The privacy page told every player about proposing clues in Cryptic, a game still in the lab (PR #1849).
**Decision:** A game or feature in the lab never affects any visible part of the site (texts, the privacy page, menus, Rankings, share messages) until its flag or launch makes it available to everyone. The only exceptions are the tester toggles (the «T» tab, `/labs/`). Anything a player page says about a lab item is gated on its launch, so it appears by itself when the item goes out.
**Why:** The owner's rule: players must not see or read about what they cannot play.
**Consequences:** Reviews check that a lab item leaves no trace on player pages; the privacy page draws items `{ game, t }` only when the game is in `ALL_GAMES`, not `labs` and not `oculto`.
