## D-489 · Battleship's Salvo: one volley per turn, as big as your ships afloat, entered with taps and one ¡Fuego!
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** games, battleship, labs
**Context:** issue #894 asked for the classic Salvo variant (listed as a future variant since v0.6),
chosen when creating the game, with classic as the default. The owner's concern was time: a volley
of up to five shots must not take five rounds of pick-and-confirm.
**Decision:**
- **Rule:** with `config.salvo`, each turn the shooter fires as many shots as ships they still
  have afloat (5 at the start, one fewer per ship lost; never more than the free cells). The turn
  passes once the whole volley is answered, whatever the results; extra shot and per-shot
  confirm do not apply, and their switches hide while Salvo is on.
- **Entering it:** tap the cells (tap again to take one back; a mark past the volley's size shakes
  and is refused); `🎯 ¡Fuego! n/N` enables at N and sends the whole volley in one tap.
- **Results after the volley:** the fired cells keep their 🎯 until the last answer is in; then
  the board, the sinking and one line (`Tu salva`: each cell with 🌊/💥/🔥, then the ships sunk) show
  them together. On one phone the hand-off lists the volley, one row per shot.
- **Protocol:** unchanged messages (`shot`, `reply`). The reducer accepts a shot while others of the
  same volley are unanswered; who fires and the volley's size come from `salvoTurn()` in
  `engine.js` (pure, tested). Rooms carry `salvo` in their config (a new field in the rules).
  The phone fires its whole volley from `Hunter`, marking each cell as shot so they differ.
- **Lab (D-395, D-409):** the switch shows only with `labAccess()`, in all three modes; One a Day
  stays classic. Card `#seccion-bn-salva` in `/labs/`. Tested by `tools/e2e/battleship/salvo.mjs`.
**Why:** marking N cells and confirming once costs N + 1 taps, against 2N with the confirm per shot,
and hiding the answers until the volley is complete is what makes Salvo a different game (you
commit to the whole volley before learning anything).
**Consequences:** classic games are untouched (`salvo` absent or false). Publishing for everyone
is a later decision, once the lab says the mode is worth it; until then a guest joining a Salvo
room plays it even without a key, as the host chose it.
