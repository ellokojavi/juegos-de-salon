## D-457 · Seasonal touches on the pages players move around in (Halloween first)
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** home, identity
**Context:** The owner asked for a layer of small visual nods to dates like Halloween: fun references to the day, not a redesign, including the favicon, on only for the key days. They then narrowed the basic version to the general navigation, not the games.
**Decision:** A date is a **template**: one row in `TEMPLATES` (`public/assets/season/templates.js`) with its days (`from`–`to`, `MM-DD`, the player's own calendar, both included, may cross New Year), `public`, and the touches it names, each optional: `icon` (the tab's favicon, an emoji on a two-colour square drawn in place), `hero` (emojis that take turns with the home page's 🎲), `motes` (one background particle in three becomes one of them), `flyby` (crosses the home page once per visit), `peek` (`{ emoji, chance }`, rises from the bottom edge now and then) and `corner` (drawn by the template's own css, which also sets its palette). The engine is `public/assets/js/season.js`, the shared placement and motion `public/assets/css/season.css`; how to add one is `docs/SEASONS.md`. On a template's days the page gets `season season-<id>` on `<html>`; the day after its last one it is gone with no PR. Only the home page and Rankings import it; the games look the same all year (the owner's call). Halloween (Oct 26–31) is the first: a pumpkin favicon, a pumpkin-and-purple glow instead of pink, a cobweb in the top right corner, 🎃 👻 🦇 🍬 🕸️ in the hero's cycle, 🦇 🕷️ 🪲 💀 🎃 👻 🕸️ among the background particles, a bat crossing once and, one visit in fifteen, a ghost peeking. None of it is text, takes a tap or moves with `prefers-reduced-motion`. `?season=<id>` turns a template on any day (with the rare touches always), `?season=off` turns it off, and `?test` keeps it off so scripts and screenshots do not change. A template starts with `public: false`, so it shows only to lab key holders (D-395, D-409), and is listed in `/labs/` (`#seccion-temporadas`) and in the home page's lab bar.
**Why:** A date is a cheap way to make the site feel alive, as long as it stays a wink: decorations that do not move the layout, block a button or touch the games cannot break a game night. Dates in a table switch themselves on and off, so nobody has to remember on Nov 1, and the owner asked for it to scale to future templates: a new date is data and a small stylesheet, not code.
**Consequences:** Going public is `public: true` on the template's row. `season.test.mjs` checks each template's fields, that no two share a day and that no shared game module (ui.js, i18n.js, sound.js, fin.js) loads `season.js`. The installed app's icon is not changed: only the browser tab's favicon. The favicon is drawn in place rather than a file, so the e2e scope can follow everything a template loads (D-391).
