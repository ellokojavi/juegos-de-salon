## D-453 · Toque y Fama's solo score, by deduction: tried in the lab first
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** would replace D-113's rule for 🔢 (revives the unmerged branch `tyf-puntaje-deduccion`, once D-197, then D-204) · **Topics:** cup, modes
**Context:** 🔢 (La Copa's number game, Toque y Fama's Jugar solo and the Final's number round)
scores 100 on the first guess and 10 less for each guess after it (D-113). The first guess has no
clues, so a 100 is 1 in 5040 of luck, and a flawless deducer tops out near 60, the same as a lucky
guesser. A branch from 2026-10-04 changed the rule but never merged; the owner asked to revive it.
**Decision:** The new rule: start at 100, take off 15 for each guess the earlier clues had already
ruled out (`descartado()`: against each earlier guess it would not give that guess's bulls and
cows), never below 10 if solved, 0 if not (`puntajeDescarte` in `public/cup/games/number/engine.js`).
It goes to the lab first (D-395, D-409): **everything recorded stays on D-113's rule** (rankings, Cup
totals, records, the Final), so no lab score leaks into what everyone sees. With a lab key
(`labAccess()`, card `#seccion-tyf-descarte` in `/labs/`), a finished board marks each ruled-out guess
with ⚠️, explains it, and says what the new rule would have scored ("🧪 Con la regla nueva: 85/100"),
also on the solo result under the summary. The marks never show while playing: they would tell the
player a number cannot be the secret.
**Why:** 100 should belong to whoever deduces. Always playing a possible number cracks any of the
5040 secrets in 10 guesses or fewer, so 100 is reachable without luck.
**Consequences:** "Yes, and publish" swaps `puntaje` for `puntajeDescarte` where scores are recorded
(`ui.js` `resultado`, the Final's `puntosRonda.numero`), updates the rules texts in four languages
(the old branch's wording: "Partes con 100 puntos. Cada intento que las pistas ya descartaban resta
15; el mínimo es 10.") and La Copa's breakdown lines, drops the lab line, and edits D-113 and the
canon. Unit tests in `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs`. The lab screen was checked at 320 px with a temporary look.mjs path (not added: a paths.mjs change runs every e2e script).
