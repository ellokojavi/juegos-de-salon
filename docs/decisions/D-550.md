## D-550 · Every cover starts with its rules folded
**Date:** 2026-11-10 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends C-8 ("long blocks are collapsed") · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** Most covers already folded "Cómo se juega" in a `<details>`, but a Cup game's own page (Reinas, Zip, Tango…) opened it, and Sudoku and El Camaleón showed it as an open panel. The owner asked for one rule: the instructions start minimized on every cover, so the cover shows what matters.
**Decision:** On every game's cover the instructions are a `<details class="panel">` that starts closed. The player opens them with one tap.
**Why:** The cover's job is the game's identity and its start button; open rules push the rest down and compete with it.
**Consequences:** `cup/game.js` drops `open: true` on the practice cover; Sudoku and El Camaleón (lab) fold theirs. Setup screens that explain a first game (Línea de tiempo alone) are not covers and keep their behavior.
