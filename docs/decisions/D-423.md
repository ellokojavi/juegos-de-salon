## D-423 · An ended cup is told on the home page, and its notification says to tap for the table
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-224 and D-229 (the `fin` notification) and D-351 (the splash's window) · **Topics:** cup, home
**Context:** A player who did not have La Copa's notifications on found out a cup ended only by opening it. The owner asked that the home page tell them when they come back (the final table, how places moved over the week, a link to see more and a ✕), and that the `fin` notification, which already goes to everyone with notifications on in that cup, say that tapping it shows the ranking.
**Decision:**
- **The recap window.** On the home page, when a cup this phone played (cuenta.js's "Tus copas") ended less than a week ago and its recap was not seen here, a window opens over the page: "La Copa terminó", the cup's name, who won and where this player finished (the `fin` notification's sentence), and the final table, one row per player with 🥇🥈🥉 or the place, a small line of their place after each day, the places climbed or lost from day 1 (▲2, ▼1, =), and the points; the player's row is marked. **🏆 Ver la copa** opens the cup; **Cerrar** and ✕ close it. Closing it or following its link marks it seen (`juegos-de-salon:copa:resumen-visto`, by code); with several, the most recent one.
- **Cheap for whoever has none.** Which cup is due is read from `localStorage` with no network; only then does the home page read that cup, over REST (`torneos/<code>` is public to read), and load the cup's engine. A cup with nothing to tell (it never started, nobody scored, the player is not in it) is marked seen and never fetched again; one that could not be read is tried on the next visit.
- **One window per visit.** The recap goes before the splash (D-351): on a visit that shows a recap, the splash waits for the next one. The window is the splash's (`splash.css`) with its own table (`recap.css`, `assets/js/recap.js`); `cup/recap.js` says which cup and builds its rows from the engine (`tabla`, `evolucion`).
- **In the lab first** (D-395, D-409): the window opens only for a lab key holder, listed in /labs/ as `#seccion-resumen-copa`, with `?recap=demo` showing La Copa's podium demo without recording anything. It reaches everyone once the owner validates it.
- **The notification.** The `fin` notification adds "Toca para ver la tabla final." (in the four languages) after who won and the player's place; it already opened the cup. It goes to everyone, as the owner asked: a notification cannot be held behind the lab key.
**Why:** The owner's request. The table and its lines are what the cup's own board already computes, so the window says nothing the cup would not; reading the cup only when one is due keeps the home page as light as before for everyone else.
**Consequences:** The window knows a cup ended by its planned end (what "Tus copas" remembers): a cup the admin ended early is told once its planned end passes, within the same week. A cup played on another phone is told on that one. The home page now loads `cup/recap.js`, `cup/engine.js`, `cup/cuenta.js` and `cup/demo.js` when a recap is due, declared in `REUSES` (`tools/e2e/affected.mjs`), so a change to the cup's engine also runs the home page's scripts.
