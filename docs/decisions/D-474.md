## D-474 · Rooms keep their own message counters, and the panel reads them without their messages
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-473 · **Topics:** panel, rooms
**Context:** After D-473 the panel still downloaded every live room with all its messages, and every move of every room reached it, only to show Moves, Chat and Last move (#848 step 2). Firebase cannot leave a child out of a query.
**Decision:** The shared transport (`transport/firebase.js`, `send`) bumps `rooms/<CODE>/counts/<t>` = `{ n, at }` (`increment(1)` and the server time) once each message is in. The rules take `counts/<t>` only while the room is alive, with a plain key, `n` going up by exactly one and `at` the current time. The panel lists the room codes with a shallow REST read every 30 s and listens per room only to `createdAt`, `lastAt`, `game`, `players` and `counts` (`public/panel/rooms-watch.js`); a room older than six hours stops at `createdAt`. A room with no `counts` (from before this change, or where nobody wrote yet) is read with its messages until its counters appear. `liveRooms` takes either (`porTipo` in `aggregate.js`): esJugada still decides per type which are moves, so the numbers are the same.
**Why:** Messages are most of a room's bytes and its only part that grows with play. The counters are what the panel shows, by type, so no number changes (the test compares both readings of the same room).
**Consequences:** A count is best effort: if the counter write fails, or the SDK resends it after a reconnect, the panel's number may be off by one; the game is never affected. Rooms created before this change are read as before until they expire (six hours). New rooms show in the panel up to 30 s late.
