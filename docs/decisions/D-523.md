## D-523 · Babel scores height and precision alike: a point a block, a point a perfect drop
**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-517 (Babel's score was twice its blocks) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** with two points a block, 50 blocks already gave 100: good players tied at the top and the time broke the tie, so the score could not tell a precise tower from one that barely survived. The owner asked (2026-11-01) for a score that rewards height and good drops equally, the day before the launch; the testers' old scores stay as they are.
**Decision:** Babel's score is `min(100, blocks + perfect drops)`: a cut block is worth 1 point, a perfect one 2. The longest streak does not add points; it stays in the breakdown.
**Why:** perfect drops only count while the tower grows, so precision cannot be earned without height; 100 takes 50 perfect blocks or a taller (faster) tower with fewer. The longest streak was left out because it already pays in height (from `GROW_FROM` in a row the slab grows back) and one slip in a long game would erase it.
**Consequences:** `puntaje` in `public/cup/games/stack/engine.js`, the cover's rule and the result's breakdown in three languages, the demo and the end-to-end script. Scores saved before keep their value.
