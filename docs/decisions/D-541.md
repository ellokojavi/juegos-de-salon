## D-541 · No rule without its enforcement: the rules for making rules
**Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** tests, agents
**Context:** rules were born as prose and only got a check after the owner noticed the drift (the design system got `design-lint`, English got `english.test`); where a check existed, drift stopped. The 2026-11-06 audit (#1249) found 31 of 45 U-n judged only by agents, the canons with no enforcement marks and 226 of 534 decisions amended or replaced.
**Decision:** every rule follows the ten rules in [docs/RULES.md](../RULES.md): each one is a 🤖 check, a 📈 ratchet or a 👁 judgement with a named reviewer; one registry; old debt ratcheted, never fixed in a big bang; reasons for every exception; a fix needed twice gets its check; and the rest listed there.
**Why:** a check stops drift without the owner noticing it; prose alone does not.
**Consequences:** the plan in #1249 builds the registry (`docs/rules.json`), classifies the canons and usability rules, and adds the missing checks one PR at a time. A new rule proposed without its form is incomplete.
