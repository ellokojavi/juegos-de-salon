## D-476 · A nightly job moves finished Cups and old daily tables to archive/ in the same database
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-473 (issue #848 step 3); works after D-472 · **Topics:** cup, panel, rankings
**Context:** `torneos`, `stats/*/days`, `records/*/d<day>` and `unoAlDiaInicio` only grow (#848),
and every whole-tree read (`rankings-history.mjs --podiums` every night, `weekly.mjs` every week,
`unoAlDiaInicio` under its owner read) pays for all of their past.
**Decision:**
- `tools/firebase/archive.mjs` moves, with the same path, into `archive/` of the same database:
  finished Cups ended more than 733 days ago whose podium is saved (or that never get one), with
  their `torneoIntentos`, `torneoSeats` and `torneoKeys`; stats days older than 733 days, without
  their named `rooms/` and `live/` (D-472); `records/<t>/d<day>` and `unoAlDiaInicio/<jid>/<day>`
  older than 35 days. `unoAlDia/` (sharded by month in #848 step 4) and `rooms/` are not touched.
- Dry run by default; `--apply` writes each copy, reads it back, compares it and only then deletes the
  live node. A copy that does not match stops the run with nothing deleted.
- The `firebase-archive` workflow runs it nightly (09:41 UTC, after the stats names retention), and
  only applies when the repository variable `FIREBASE_ARCHIVE_APPLY` is `true`: the owner turns it on
  after reading a dry run.
- `archive/` is closed to every client in the rules; `rankings-history.mjs` (full rebuild) reads
  `archive/torneos` alongside `torneos`.
**Why:** a cold path in Firebase rather than a JSON in the reports folder: the job runs on GitHub,
where the admin key already is and the reports folder (on the Mac mini) is not; a node nobody
listens to costs storage, not downloads, and storage is cents per GB; and copying, reading back and
deleting in one database is checkable in one place, and undoable by moving back. The cutoffs come
from the readers: the panel reads back at most a year and the year before it (D-348), plus two
days of margin; the medal table reads `torneoPodios/`, not `torneos/`; the app reads only today's
daily table and day start; `weekly.mjs` reads seven weeks. 35 days keeps a month to look at.
**Consequences:** a Cup's link stops opening two years after it ended. A new reader of an old
Cup, stats day or daily table reads `archive/` too, or the cutoff grows in the same PR. The first
dry run (2026-10-28) found nothing to move: the oldest data is younger than its cutoff.
