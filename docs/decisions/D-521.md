## D-521 · La Copa: the admin chooses its length (3 to 14 days) and its time zone
**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-113 (every new cup in Pacific time), D-418 (the final replays every game of the week), D-437 and D-438 (only 3 or 7 days); behind the `copa-crear` flag (D-498) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** A cup lasted 3 or 7 days, and every new one ran on Pacific time, where the owner lives. The owner wanted more room for the admin (a long weekend, a work week, the holidays) and noted that Chile is 5 hours from Pacific time today: a Chilean player's day opened and closed near 5 in the morning. Discussed with the owner on 2026-11-01, mockups approved.
**Decision:**
- **Length:** from 3 to 14 days (`DIAS_MIN`, `DIAS_MAX` in `engine.js`), chosen with − n days + and shortcuts (3 · long weekend, 7 · one week, 14 · two weeks), starting at seven. 14 fits the pool (16 games with Shinobi and Babel) with room for variety; with little traffic today there is no data for a lower cap.
- **The Grand Final** is a switch, offered from 5 days (`FINAL_DESDE`), on by default. A shorter cup has none (as the 3-day one, D-438).
- **The final plays 5 of the cup's own games**, drawn at random in the order they were played (`FINAL_RONDAS`, `finalRounds`), drawn alike on every phone from the cup itself. Cups created before `FINAL_FIVE_FROM` keep every game of their week (D-418).
- **The form:** when it starts first, then how long; a long cup (10 days or more) warns that whoever skips two days in a row is out (D-347); a cup longer than a week shows its days by week, with their dates.
- **The zone of the cup:** the audience's country when it names one (🇨🇱 Chile's time, 🇧🇷 Brazil's), otherwise the admin's phone; said in one line under when it starts, with **Change** (a list of zones).
- **Each player sees their own hours** when their phone is in another zone: the day card ("until 18:59, your time"), the grace day, and the board and Administrar ("For you: 19:00 to 18:59"). The reminders already say the hours left, which reads right anywhere. Each player having their own midnight was ruled out: it breaks one shared day, the hidden results and the server's windows.
- **The database rules** take 3 to 14 days, days up to 14 in `win`, `started`, `results` and the wildcard, `final` as `0`, `3` or `5` to `14`, `cal` up to 300 characters, and the "out of the cup" rule (D-347) written out to day 13.
**Why:** The owner's request: more freedom for the admin, within bounds that keep a cup playable, and a day that matches the clock of the players it is made for.
**Consequences:** The screen of before (without the flag) is unchanged: 7 days, or 3 with `?three`, on the language's zone. The rules must be published (`node tools/firebase/rules.mjs publish`) when this merges. Published by flipping `flags/copa-crear` to all.
