## D-525 · Babel and Shinobi score with no cap; Babel rewards streaks
**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** amended by D-526 · **Relation:** amends D-517 (Babel two points a block up to 100, Shinobi capped at 100) · **Topics:** cup, random-and-daily
**Context:** Before launch the owner asked that Babel reward perfect streaks on top of the tower's height, and that the score not stop at 100. La Copa already ranks each day by position (D-95: 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1-1-1), so a game's number only has to order the players; its scale does not matter there.
**Decision:**
- **Babel:** 1 point a floor; a streak is 3 or more perfect drops in a row, worth +3 once when it reaches 3 (`STREAK_BONUS`), and each perfect from the 3rd on adds its place in the streak minus 2, +1, +2… up to +5 a block (`LENGTH_MAX`). `puntos(kinds)` in `cup/games/stack/engine.js`. A save without `kinds` counts its blocks.
- **Shinobi:** its own points as they were (1 a bridge, flag streak bonus up to +5), without the cap at 100.
- **No cap** on their page, the records and a cup day. Where a 0–100 is averaged across games, the Grand Final's rounds and Uno al día, the cup caps the number at 100 (`Math.min(100, s)`), which changes nothing for the other games.
- **Scores already saved stay as they are** (the testers' included): there is no record of each old game's perfects to recount, so a tester's entry is replaced only when a new game beats it.
**Why:** the owner's request: a long tower and a long run should keep paying, and streaks are the skill the game is about.
**Consequences:** The breakdown shows floors, streaks and their length; the rules text says it in one line in es, en and pt. Babel's lab prototype keeps its own height score until the cleanup PR.
