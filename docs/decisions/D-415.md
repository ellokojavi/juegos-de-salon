## D-415 · A day a 🧊 saved goes up to Firebase, and the month's points are rebuilt from it every night
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** amended by D-465, D-475, D-499 · **Relation:** amends D-414 (what the server knows of the streak) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, rankings
**Context:** /today/ first drew the month's points from the phone (927 for the owner) and an instant later the ranking's from Firebase (871). The phone keeps the streak through a missed day its 🧊 saves; Firebase only had the days played, so October's backfill (D-414) broke the streak on those days and the bonus started over. The owner asked to fix it at the root.
**Decision:**
- **The phone uploads the days its 🧊 saved** as markers in the history, `unoAlDia/<jid>/<n>: { c: true, at }` (`jugador.anotarSalvado`, from `sincronizar` on opening /today/, past days too). A marker carries no score, is written once, never over a day played, and never gets `w` or `p`.
- **The server's walk keeps the streak through them** (`walk(days, today, { saved, gifts })` in `daily-points.js`): it spends the 🧊 it can see (every 7 days in a row, and the gifts from `invitados/`) on any missed day, as the phone does, and a marked day it can't cover that way takes one of 2 more it lends once over the whole history (the most a phone can hold, earned on days the server never saw). A missed day with neither breaks the streak. The phone's walk now spends the gifts too, as `recorrer` does.
- **The month is rebuilt every night**: `uad-points-backfill.mjs --rebuild` recomputes each player's month from scratch (history, markers, +10 per friend already counted that accepted in that month) and rewrites the row when it differs; the `uad-rebuild` workflow runs it at 08:23 UTC, and in a month's first three days also for the month before. Running it twice writes nothing new. A client cannot correct a past total itself: the rules check one added day at a time and cannot add up a month, so the correction is the administrator's.
- **/today/ does not jump:** signed in, its first drawing shows the last total Firebase gave this phone (`uad-pts-cache`), not the phone's own count; signing out forgets it.
**Why:** The owner's request. The ranking has to say what the player sees, and only the phone knows which days its 🧊 saved.
**Consequences:** What the rules enforce for a marker: its shape, once per day, only by whoever is seated as the player, never over a day played. What they can't: whether the phone really held a 🧊 (the rules can't do arithmetic over day keys or count wildcards), so a phone could mark days it didn't save; the rebuild's allowance bounds what that buys (2 lent days over a whole history, beyond the 🧊 the server can see). The panel's return counts skip markers. A total can go down at night if a phone had added a day with a streak the server can't back.
**Later (2026-10-27):** a day's points count only if its result reached the server near the day's date; a day uploaded later keeps the streak and earns nothing, in the rules and in the rebuild (D-465).
