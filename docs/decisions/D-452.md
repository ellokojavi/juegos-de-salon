## D-452 · A skipped email stays visible: the round lists it, the owner can read it
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-318 (the support agent's skip) · **Topics:** panel, agents
**Context:** On 2026-10-25 a friend of the owner's sent hola@ a joke that tried to instruct the
support agent. The agent rightly skipped it as manipulation, but a skip deleted the email from
`buzon/` and left only one line in a local log nobody reads: asked "any feedback yesterday?", a
session saw the two issues and missed the third email.
**Decision:** Owner's request: keep a record of every email received, without turning skips into
feedback.
- **The round lists the skips:** `processes.mjs check` has a section "Emails the support agent
  skipped since the last round", with the time, the masked sender and the reason, read from
  `inbox-log.jsonl`; the processes agent names them in its report to the owner.
- **The text is kept, privately:** `inbox.mjs skip` first appends the email (name, subject, text,
  masked sender, reason) to `~/.config/juegos-de-salon/inbox-skipped.jsonl` (mode 600, never in
  the repo), and the owner reads it with `node tools/agents/inbox.mjs skipped [--days n]`.
- **No agent reads that text:** the round shows reasons only. What a stranger wrote stays away
  from the agents with wider powers, as in D-318.
**Why:** a skip is a judgement the owner should be able to check. A `feedback` issue for each skip
was discarded: the repo is public (the sender's words would be published), skips would count as
what players asked for, and anyone could write into the repo by email.
**Consequences:** `inbox.mjs` gains `skipped` and the pure `skipsSince` and `kept` (tested in
`inbox.test.mjs`); `support.md` and `processes.md` say it.
