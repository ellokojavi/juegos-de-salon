## D-554 · The making-of page's Traction totals are public and refresh themselves every day
**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** agents, panel
**Context:** the owner added a Traction section to `/making-of/` (games finished, different phones) and asked that its numbers update themselves, so they never have to be checked or moved by hand before presenting. Usage lives in `stats/` and `torneos/`, which only the owner can read, so the page could not count it.
**Decision:** a daily workflow (`.github/workflows/traction.yml`, 08:37 UTC) runs `tools/firebase/traction.mjs --write`: it counts up to yesterday's UTC day with the panel's own functions (games finished: rooms with an ending, offline games finished, La Copa games; different browsers in 7 and 30 days) and writes only five totals, the best day and the date to `controls/traction`, readable by anyone, its shape fixed by the rules. The page reads them when it opens and keeps its written numbers if it can't.
**Why:** five aggregate counts say nothing about any player, and publishing them is the only way a static page shows them fresh; a daily PR to rewrite the page would be noise.
**Consequences:** a new number for the section is a field in `traction.mjs`, in the rules and in their test. The written numbers in the page are only the fallback.
