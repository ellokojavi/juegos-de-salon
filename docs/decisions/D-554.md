## D-554 · The making-of page's Traction totals are public and refresh themselves every day
**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** agents, panel
**Context:** the owner added a Traction section to `/making-of/` (games finished, different phones) and asked that its numbers update themselves, so they never have to be checked or moved by hand before presenting. Usage lives in `stats/` and `torneos/`, which only the owner can read, so the page could not count it.
**Decision:** a daily workflow (`.github/workflows/traction.yml`, 08:37 UTC) runs `tools/firebase/traction.mjs --write`: it counts up to yesterday's UTC day with the panel's own functions (games finished: rooms with an ending, offline games finished, La Copa games; different browsers in 7 and 30 days) and writes only the totals (games in all, in the last 7 days, on the best day; browsers in 7 and 30 days), the records (the best 7 days of games, the most browsers in any 7 and 30 days), the best day and the date to `controls/traction`, readable by anyone, its shape fixed by the rules. The page reads them when it opens and keeps its written numbers if it can't. It shows the records, labelled as such ("in the best week so far"), not the last 7 days: the owner asked that a slow week never undersell the reach, and a record labelled as one is still true.
**Why:** five aggregate counts say nothing about any player, and publishing them is the only way a static page shows them fresh; a daily PR to rewrite the page would be noise.
**Consequences:** a new number for the section is a field in `traction.mjs`, in the rules and in their test. The written numbers in the page are only the fallback.
