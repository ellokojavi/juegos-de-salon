## D-441 · Pending work is watched from GitHub: stuck PRs, branches without a PR, and a waiter that does not go blind
**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-400 (`wait-pr.mjs`), D-218 and D-252 (the worktree cleanup that pushes abandoned branches) and D-440 (watching from GitHub, away from the Mac) · **Topics:** agents, publishing
**Context:** the owner asked for a mechanism to not lose track of pending PRs. Open PRs themselves rarely get lost: of the last 300, 297 merged, half within minutes, none after more than a day. What got lost was around them. A session's `wait-pr.mjs` read the PR only through `gh pr view`, which some cloud sessions refuse, and retried "trying again" for 25 minutes while #743 sat red; it also stops after two hours, and the session may end. The processes round lists waiting PRs, but only when it runs, and it stalled for six days (D-440). And work on a branch with no open PR is seen by nobody: on 2026-10-24 ten branches held commits that never reached `main`, idle 4 to 24 days, two of them pushed after their PR had already merged, others pushed by the worktree cleanup "so nothing is lost".
**Decision:** (1) `tools/agents/pending.mjs` finds, through the REST API only, the open PRs that are stuck (a required check red or a conflict with no push for 6 hours, a day open without auto-merge, two days open, a draft older than a week) and the stranded branches (commits not on `main`, no open PR, idle more than a day; `main`, `gh-pages` and decision reservations excluded). (2) `pending-watch.yml` runs it daily at 17:47 UTC and keeps one "Pending work" issue current, editing its body instead of commenting so it emails once, and closes it when nothing is left. (3) `processes.mjs check` lists the stranded branches, and the round settles each one: deletes it when `main` already has the work another way, opens its PR when the work is still wanted, or asks the owner with one tap. (4) `wait-pr.mjs` reads the REST API when `gh pr view` fails, and after five failed reads in a row prints `unreadable` and exits 3.
**Why:** each place that waits for a PR can stop (a session, its waiter, the round), so the safety net is the outcome on GitHub checked from GitHub, as in D-440. A branch is the only work no list showed. Grace periods (6 hours for a red PR, a day for a branch) leave a session's work in progress alone, so the issue speaks only when something is really forgotten. A waiter that retries one error forever is worse than one that stops, because the session believes it is watching.
**Consequences:** `pending.mjs` and its test, `pending-watch.yml` (in `jobs.json`), `wait-pr.mjs` and its test, `processes.mjs` (the new section; the issue stays out of the owner's backlog), `processes.md` step 4 and `CLAUDE.md` (points on waiting for the merge and on the worktree cleanup). The ten branches found today are the first list for the round to settle.
