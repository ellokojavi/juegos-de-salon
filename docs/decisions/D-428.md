## D-428 · Faster releases without testing less: CI is the gate, and the groups fit the real times
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-307 (how often durations are measured, and the long scripts' parts) · **Topics:** tests
**Context:** the owner found releases slow and asked why. Measured over October 20–22 (80 merged PRs, 18
sessions): a PR merged in a median of 9 minutes after opening, and publishing took 3, so most of the time
was before the PR. A third of the sessions' working time (3.9 of 11.6 hours) went to running end-to-end
scripts locally, 57 times the whole `ci.mjs` (up to 10 minutes each), which the required `end-to-end`
check then ran again in parallel. On GitHub, `cup/languages.mjs` took 370 s while `durations.json` said
201 s, so its group ran 9 minutes and set the whole check; `durations.json` was measured weekly while ~45
PRs a day merged.
**Decision:** (1) the `end-to-end` check is the gate: a session runs locally only the script of the
screen it changed, never the whole `ci.mjs` before pushing, and the unit tests in parallel (`xargs -P8`,
~20 s instead of ~60). (2) `cup/languages.mjs` is split into the parts `cup`, `standalone`, `screens-a` and
`screens-b`. (3) the processes round measures durations every round instead of weekly, and
`tools/e2e/durations.json` alone runs no script (it only sorts scripts into groups).
**Why:** nothing that gated a merge or a publication stops gating it: the same required checks and the
same scripts. What goes is repetition (a local run of what CI runs) and a group sized by a stale number.
Also considered: letting `publish.yml` run only what the merge reaches when `main` moved after the PR's
run. Measured on the five publications that ran the whole suite on October 22, it would have saved wall
time on one (the longest remaining script, ~295 s, still sets the pace), at the cost of a weaker net on
`main`; dropped.
**Consequences:** the full suite's longest group goes from ~370 s to ~313 s (the check from 8–10 minutes
to 6–7, and the publications that run it from 10–14 to ~8); sessions skip ~10 minutes of local runs per
PR. The next long parts are `cup-games/languages.mjs:paint-mines` (~294 s) and `:plots` (~268 s).
