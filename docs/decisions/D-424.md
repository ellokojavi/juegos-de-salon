## D-424 · A season of Uno al día that ends is told on the home page and in a notification
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-423 (the recap window) to D-414's monthly season, and D-230 (One a Day's notifications) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, rankings, home
**Context:** After La Copa's recap (D-423) the owner asked for the same for Uno al día's monthly season (D-414): when a month ends, tell whoever played it how it ended, on the home page and with a notification to those who have One a Day's notifications on. The table only keeps each player's total, so nothing knew how places moved over the month.
**Decision:**
- **The places by week are computed at night.** The points rebuild (`uad-rebuild.yml`, `uad-points-backfill.mjs --rebuild`, D-415) already walks every player's history: it now also writes `uadTemporada/s<yyyy-mm>` = `{ c: the cut dates, t: the day it ran, j: { <jid>: { n, co?, s, l: [place at each cut] } } }`. The cuts are each Sunday of the month and its last day; at each, everyone with points is ranked by points, ties sharing the place, as the table does; 0 before a player's first points; an invited friend's +10 counts on the day they accepted. Public to read, written only by the administrator (the rules have no `.write`). A month is complete once `t` is after its last day: the run of the 1st (which rebuilds the month before, D-415) closes it.
- **The window.** In the first week of a month, a phone that played a day of the month before (its own history) and has not seen that season gets the recap window (D-423's, `assets/js/recap.js`) with "Terminó la temporada", "Uno al día: octubre", who won and, when signed in, the player's place and points ("Quedaste #3 de 8, con 901 pts. Ganó Chan."), the top 10 plus their own row with each place week by week (▲▼ from their first week), "La temporada de noviembre ya empezó: todos parten de 0." and **📅 Ir a Uno al día**. La Copa's goes first if both are due: one window per visit, before the splash. `assets/js/daily-recap.js` reads the season over REST, with nothing else loaded; it needs no hidden frame, since it uses no game engine. Once per season (`juegos-de-salon:uno-al-dia:temporada-vista`).
- **In the lab first** (D-395, D-409), with its own card (`#seccion-resumen-temporada`) and `?recap=season-demo`.
- **The notification.** `temporada:<yyyy-mm>` (tools/push/daily.mjs): on the 1st to the 3rd, from the player's hour, once, if they played since the month before began, and only once the rebuild closed that month: "🏆 Terminó la temporada de octubre. Toca para ver el ranking final." Its link, `/?recap=season`, opens last month's recap for whoever taps it, with or without a lab key: a notification cannot be held behind the lab, and the owner asked for it. Its texts are in daily.mjs, not in i18n.js, because only that sender says them.
**Why:** The owner's request. The rebuild already reads every history each night, so the places cost one write; reading one small node keeps the home page as light as before.
**Consequences:** A season is told only to phones with its days in their history (a signed-in player gets the other phones' days through `juntar`). Who is not signed in sees who won, not a place. The window links to Uno al día, not to last month's table: no page shows a past month's ranking yet. The notification's tap (`notif=dailyseason`) is not counted by the panel yet: its key goes in `parametros.js`'s `VALORES.notif`, whose change runs every end-to-end script, so it waits for the next PR that touches that file; the sent ones are counted (`mandados/uadtemporada`).
**Later (2026-10-23):** the owner validated it in the lab: the recap opens for every player, with no lab key, through the home page's one-window gate (D-431); its card moved to La Bodega, 🚀 Lanzado.
