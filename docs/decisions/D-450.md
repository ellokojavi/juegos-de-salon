## D-450 · A finished Cup's podium is saved within the hour of its close, even if nobody reopens the Cup
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-212 (who saves a Cup's podium) · **Topics:** cup, rankings
**Context:** The app saves a Cup's podium (`torneoPodios/<code>`, the medal table of the rankings) only when someone opens the Cup after it ends (`cup/game.js`). The Reno Farewell Cup (a one-day Cup, 25 October) and Copa Originales (17 October) ended with nobody reopening them, so their champions never counted. The owner asked for their champions to count and then for this not to happen again.
**Decision:** `node tools/firebase/rankings-history.mjs --podiums [--write]` writes only the missing podiums of finished non-lab Cups with at least two active players, built exactly as the app builds them (`missingPodiums`): players who linked their own player carry their jid, the rest add up by name. The `cup-podiums` workflow runs it twice a night, at 7:47 and 8:47 UTC: Cups close at midnight Pacific, and one of the two always falls 47 minutes after it, in summer and in winter time (at first only 7:47 UTC, which in winter falls 13 minutes before midnight; changed the same day). Hourly was weighed and dropped: 24 runs a day that almost always find nothing, for the few Cups there are. The app still saves the podium on opening, and a saved podium is never touched. The two missing podiums were written by hand on 26 October.
**Why:** a ranking that depends on someone reopening a page misses exactly the short, ad-hoc Cups that nobody revisits. The full history script also writes legacy players and records, too much to run unattended.
**Consequences:** a Cup counts in the medal table within 47 minutes of closing. The job needs the `FIREBASE_ADMIN_JSON` secret; without it, it warns and ends green.
