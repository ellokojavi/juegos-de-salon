## D-446 · Stick Ninja: a hold-and-release bridge game in Juegos en prueba, on the day's course, in English
**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-445 (Stack, the lab's first one-tap game), D-304 and D-413 (a lab-only PR) · **Topics:** stick
**Context:** the owner sent a video of Stick Hero (Ketchapp, 2014) and asked for an evaluation. The evaluation:
it is about judging a distance, not a reflex, so it sits closer to the house's "work it out" games than
Stack; and unlike Stack, its run *is* its sequence of gaps, so a seed makes a course everyone can share.
Then: "Build it as a labs prototype but make it much more stylish and complete with feedback to users upon
achievements (eg bonus when the stick hits the dot, or making the dot a flag, and create a nice emerging
pulse signal for the bonus). Find nice improvements".
**Decision:** a solo prototype at `/labs/stick/`, in 🎲 Juegos en prueba, credited on the cover and the lab
card, in English only like Stack. Hold to stretch the stick, let go and it falls across the gap; on the
next pillar the ninja runs over and scores 1. The original's red dot is a **flag** at each pillar's center:
a tip on it is a perfect, worth +1, then +2, +3… for each one in a row, up to +5, and it answers with an
emerging pulse (three rings of the house's neon from the tip, sparks, a gold flag that pops and stays,
the stage's edge glowing, a rising sound). A tap while running hangs the ninja under the bridge to grab
the ⭐ in some gaps (+1); still upside down at the pillar is a crash. Achievements drop in as banners
(the first flag, 3, 5 and 8 in a row, the first star, 3 stars, a new best, 10/25/50/100… points). The
course is **the day's** (a seed from the date): everyone crosses the same gaps today, and the result
lists today's runs on this phone, so a group can pass it around and compare. The original's cherries
shop, ads and rewarded videos are left out. Details in `docs/games/stick.md`.
**Why:** the owner's request. The day's course is what Stack could not have (D-445: every tower plays
nearly the same) and what would let a score go to La Copa or a ranking one day: same gaps, and a stick
measured between the press's and the release's own timestamps, so neither the phone nor the course
favors anyone.
**Alternatives discarded:** a random course each run (scores could not be compared); cherries as a
currency to spend (nothing to spend them on; a star is a point instead); a separate "pass the phone" mode
with names (today's runs on the result already let a group compare, without a setup screen); the red dot
as in the original (a flag reads from farther away and can turn gold once hit).
**Consequences:** the second lab game in English only; `engine.test.mjs` and `tools/e2e/stick/lab.mjs`
cover it. What would prove it: players who play more than one run in a visit, and runs on the same day
from different phones.
