## D-609 · Offline, the whole game takes Candlelight, and the switch is immediate
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-538 (its plan's PR 8 left the play screens in their own colours; probes once a minute) · **Topics:** pwa
**Context:** The owner tested offline mode by switching a loaded phone to airplane mode: it took several seconds (first up to two minutes, then up to ~23 s) to switch, and the games did not look like the home page's Candlelight. They had understood that the whole palette changes when the game is offline, and asked for it on every game, new ones too, and on newer experiences such as the profile, consistently.
**Decision:**
- **Every screen takes the offline palette** while offline: the home page, the menus, the covers and the play screens of every game, sheets, sparkles and confetti. `offline-ui.js` sets `html.offline-shell` from the mode alone. What is a game's own drawing (Aletazo's sky, Shinobi's night, Reinas' region colours, Tembleque's scene) keeps its colours: it is the board, not the palette. Colours written outside the tokens take the palette next to them (`html.offline-shell …`), and the party colours come from the tokens (`party-colors.js`).
- **The sticker "📴 Sin conexión" steps aside while playing** (`html.offline-play`), so it never covers a board; it stays on the covers' and the setup screens' titles.
- **Every page that runs offline mode paints Candlelight from its first frame**, games old and new: `offline-first-paint.test.mjs` fails a page that loads `instalable.js` without the first-paint script, and a game whose page lacks either.
- **A kept game keeps its sub-pages** (Tembleque's levels): `precache.mjs` stores them and they are never dimmed.
- **Immediate switch**: a probe refused outright (no route, as in airplane mode) is asked again 0.5 s later and two refusals switch; probes every 5 s while the page is in view, on each touch of the screen (at most every 2 s), when Android reports a network change, and on coming back to the front (1 s later, and a refusal then is confirmed 1.5 s after, so a waking radio does not flash offline). A slow connection still needs two slow probes.
- **The profile and Rankings need signal**: their buttons are dimmed with "📡 Solo con señal" and a tap says why, like La Copa's server parts; they are not kept on the phone. The tag said "Con señal" / "Com sinal", which read as "has signal" on a dimmed button: now "Solo con señal" / "Só com sinal", as the English "Needs signal".
- **Orange and cyan keep apart offline** from the amber accent and the creams (orange was the same amber as pink: Batalla Naval's hit and sunk looked alike); `offline-palette.test.mjs` keeps the accents at least 40 apart.
- **No false flash**: one refused probe is asked again 0.5 s later before switching (airplane mode shows in about half a second; a DNS miss or a Wi-Fi to data handoff does not); a cancelled request does not count; offline only by refusals, one good answer is the way back.
**Why:** offline must feel like one version of the app, not a home page in one colour and games in another; and a player who switches airplane mode on should see it at once, or the mode looks broken.
**Consequences:** a HEAD of `precache.json` every 5 s while a page is in view (none while hidden): about 720 an hour per open page, a few hundred bytes each. A new game needs nothing of its own: the tokens, the first-paint script (checked) and its folder in the precache do it.
