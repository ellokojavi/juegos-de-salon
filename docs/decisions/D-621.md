## D-621 · No alert dot on the panel's Health tab
**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-251 (drops its pink dot) · **Topics:** panel
**Context:** D-251 put a pink dot on 🩺 Health whenever any page failed to start today or yesterday.
**Decision:** The navigation shows no dot for Health. The section itself keeps every failure, rate and detail.
**Why:** The owner: some pages failing to load is normal background, so the dot was lit almost always and said
nothing to act on. An alert without an action is noise, and it trains the eye to ignore the panel's dots.
**Consequences:** A Health alert comes back only with a threshold that means a real anomaly and a clear action;
it is a new decision, not this dot again. The green dot on Ahora (something in play) stays.
