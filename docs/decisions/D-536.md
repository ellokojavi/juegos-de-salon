## D-536 · The first visit opens in the browser's language
**Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-47 (Spanish by default); amends D-74 (the language in the link still wins, but the browser now decides a first visit) · **Topics:** languages
**Context:** the owner bought parlorgames.app as the English way in, redirecting to the same site with `?lang=en`, and wants English speakers to land in English as easily as Spanish speakers land in Spanish, on one site. A link to juegosdesalon.cl shared with someone abroad still opened in Spanish, and they had to find the toggle.
**Decision:** on a device where no language was ever chosen (no toggle, no `?lang=`), the first page loaded saves as the choice the first of `navigator.languages` the app speaks (`es`, `en`, `pt`; a retired one maps as in `RETIRADOS`), or Spanish, and reloads in it. It runs once: from then on the saved choice rules and the toggle changes it, as before. `?lang=` in a link still wins. Behind the flag `browser-lang` until the owner publishes it.
**Why:** D-47 feared a phone in English in the hands of a Spanish-speaking table; that phone now opens once in English with the toggle in sight, while every English or Portuguese speaker stops landing in a language they do not read. D-47's own consequence foresaw this as a visible choice, and the toggle is that.
**Consequences:** `idiomaDelNavegador()` in `public/assets/js/i18n.js`, unit-tested in `i18n.test.mjs`. End-to-end scripts start without a saved language, so their Chrome's language is set to `es-CL` (`--accept-lang` in `tools/e2e/cdp.mjs`; `--lang` does not reach `navigator.languages` on Linux): with the flag *all* they still start in Spanish.
