## D-594 · A card in /labs/ says what it tries in 25 words or fewer
**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** structure, identity
**Context:** The cards of the features and games on trial in `/labs/` had grown into full specs: up to 150 words each, with every rule, exception and test step. The owner reads them on a phone to decide what to publish, and asked to simplify them drastically and to keep every new one short.
**Decision:** The text of each active card in `/labs/` (its paragraphs under the title; a treatment tried inside a card, `.seccion-sub`, counts as a card of its own) is at most 25 words: what it tries, and how to try it only when that is not obvious. The detail lives in the PR, its decision and its game's doc. 🤖 `public/labs/short-cards.test.mjs` fails a longer card. La Bodega is parked and outside the check.
**Why:** a short card is read and judged at a glance; a long one hides the one thing the owner has to see.
**Consequences:** every active card was rewritten short in the same PR; a session adding a card writes it short from the start. A paragraph with an id (a live status line, like offline mode's) does not count.
