## D-517 · A game can launch by flag: Ninja and Babel wait, finished, for the owner's 🚀
**Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-498 and D-510 (runtime flags, switched on their /labs/ card) to whole games; follows D-456 (how a lab game graduates: one screen, La Copa's); amends D-480 for these two cards (🚀 is the flag, not a session) · **Topics:** structure, cup, random-and-daily, home
**Context:** the owner asked (2026-10-31) to leave 🥷 Stick Ninja (D-446) and 🧱 Stack (D-445) fully built, in every place a game reaches, so that one button on Sunday publishes them, with a splash to follow. D-480's 🚀 for a game starts a session that graduates it (an hour or more); flags (D-498) switched only features inside pages. The owner also set the names, **Ninja** and **Babel** (first called Stacks, renamed the same day for being too close to the original's Stack): a game may copy another's mechanic, never its brand or other intellectual property (its name as ours, its art); like every inspired game, the cover credits the original by name with a link, for transparency.
**Decision:**
- **Graduated as La Copa games** (D-456's way): `cup/games/stick/` and `cup/games/stack/` serve the standalone pages `/ninja/` and `/babel/`, Uno al día and La Copa, in es, en and pt. The ids stay `stick` and `stack` (stats, rankings). Their scores are 0–100: Ninja, its points (100 or more is 100); Babel, twice its blocks.
- **A manifest field `lanza: '<flag>'`** marks a game launched by a flag. `games.js` reads, at once, what the phone last saw of `flags/` (`lanzado()`): 🚀 everyone, 🧪 lab with a lab key saved, ⚪ nobody; until then the entry carries `oculto` and stays off the home page, the search, the type filters, /records/ and the pool a new cup is offered (`POZO_ABIERTO`). The home page refreshes `flags/` on each visit and redraws once if a launch happened since. The game's page, if hidden, asks Firebase before sending the visitor home; inside a cup and in Uno al día (`?today`) it always opens.
- **What must be the same on every phone does not read the flag:** `POZO` (a cup's calendar is checked against it), the day of three's rounds (`POZO_FIJO`, without these games until the cleanup), and Uno al día, which goes by date: both may come out from **2026-11-03**, the day after the planned launch, and join the next deck, shuffled on 2026-11-08 (no day before changes).
- **The home page's filters** get the type 🎯 Pulso (Skill, Reflexo), a short name chosen with the owner on a prototype measured at 320–390 px: Habilidad overflowed the row in Spanish and Portuguese. With five types, the row on a narrow phone is five equal columns, and Cartas/Dados becomes 🎲 Dados (Dice, Dados): Julepe and Cuarto Rey left the home page (D-420), so only dice games were under it, and ⭐ Favoritos, before leaving the shortcuts' row, tries the short names Nuevo · Pop · Favs (D-241, D-364's order of tries, one step more).
- **The switch** is the flag rows `stick` and `stack` (`flag-list.js`), on their cards in /labs/, which now point to the finished pages and keep the English prototypes as a link. Search engines get the pages with the cleanup PR, which removes `lanza`, the rows and the prototypes (their old paths become bridges).
**Why:** publishing a finished game is data, like a feature (D-498): instant, from a phone, and reversible with the same tap.
**Consequences:** `cup/engine.test.mjs` checks that a flag-launched game is in `POZO` but not drawn before its launch; `search.test.mjs` lets a hidden game keep its keywords. If the flag is not switched by 2026-11-03, Uno al día still deals these games (their page opens for it): to delay that too, move `desde` in a PR.
