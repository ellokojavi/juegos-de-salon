## D-535 · One name inside and out: everything internal is the English version of the game's name
**Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-192 (ids never change) · **Topics:** structure, identity
**Context:** The lab's machine puzzle was built as `contraption` (its folder, tests, generator and spec), played as «Artilugio» (id `artilugio`, keys `juegos-de-salon:artilugio-*`, hook `window.__artilugio`), and the owner then renamed it Artefactos (2026-11-06). Three names for one game, and D-192's rule that a game's id never changes would have kept the first two forever, as it keeps `'ahorcado'` in `/hangman/`.
**Decision:** The owner's tenet of design and construction, canon C-20: a game is called one thing inside and out. The player reads its name; everything internal (folder and URL, id in the usage signal and `games.js`, phone keys, test hooks, card ids, CSS custom properties, `tools/e2e/<name>/`, generators, `docs/games/<name>.md`) is the English version of that name. A new game takes it from the start; a rename renames everything in the same PR and carries over what was kept under the old name (a lab game moves its `localStorage` key once; a published game also migrates Firebase paths, rankings and panel history and keeps its old URL as a bridge, as a plan the owner approves first). Artefactos is the first: `contraption` and `artilugio` become `artifacts`, and the best kept under `juegos-de-salon:artilugio-stages` moves to `juegos-de-salon:artifacts`.
**Why:** one name is one word to search, read and talk about: the owner's request was consistency inside and outside the game. D-192 kept ids because they are stored data; storing is a reason to migrate with care, not to keep a name nobody uses.
**Consequences:** C-20 in `docs/CANONS.md` with its line in the checklist; CLAUDE.md and `docs/ADD-GAME.md` say it. Games published with Spanish ids (`ahorcado`, `reinas`, `copa`…) keep them until a PR renames one on purpose, with its migration; nothing renames them in passing. Artefactos' usage signal starts again under `artifacts` (a lab game: its few earlier plays stay under `artilugio`).
