## D-399 · The home page's lab bar opens with the newest experiment on
**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-389 (`/labs/home/` opened with nothing on) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** the owner asked that the experiments bar always start with the last experiment built, and
let the others be ticked on top of it.
**Decision:** each entry of `EXPERIMENTS` in `public/labs/home/experiments.js` says the day it was last
built or changed (`built`; a splash campaign without one counts from D-351's day). `/labs/home/` opens
the home page with the newest one on (`latest()`), and the bar lists the newest first. The other
checkboxes add theirs on top, and unticking the first leaves none on, as before.
**Why:** what the owner opens the lab to see is almost always what was just built.
**Consequences:** a new experiment or a change to one moves its `built` to that day, and with it to the
front. `tools/e2e/home/lab.mjs` checks that 🔎 Buscar opens on and first, and that unticking it leaves none.
