## D-505 · Teasers build expectation and give the game away never, on one engine
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-351, D-430 · **Topics:** splash, marketing
**Context:** The first Stack and Stick Ninja teasers were drawn apart from their games (a flat side view, a full moon the game does not have) and then showed the play itself. The owner asked for teasers that look like the game but are cryptic, and for an engine so a new one is made fast and to these goals.
**Decision:** Every new teaser follows `marketing/splash/TEASERS.md`: it draws with the game's own scene (`scene.js`), shows the world and the goal never the play, stops on one unresolved moment, carries no words in the clip (an intriguing title instead), and runs on `marketing/splash/teaser/_teaser.js` (8 s, 30 fps, 512×320). A new one starts with `teaser.mjs new`.
**Why:** a teaser that does not look like its game is not recognized on launch day, and one that shows the play spends the surprise before the game exists.
**Consequences:** `teaser.test.mjs` fails on a teaser that imports nothing from its game, is not on the engine, or draws text. A game whose drawing lives in `game.js` moves it to `scene.js` before its teaser. Tren en Vilo's teaser predates this and stays as it ran.
