**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-471 (the lab gate) · **Topics:** panel
**Context:** D-471 kept the panel's **Don't count this browser** checkbox behind the lab key until the
owner validated it. The owner tapped 🚀 Publicar para todos on its `/labs/` card (D-480).

card moves to La Bodega under ✨ Mejoras lanzadas (D-479).
