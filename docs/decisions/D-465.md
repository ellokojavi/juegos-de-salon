## D-465 · Uno al día points count only near the day's own date, and only after its start
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-415 (what the rebuild counts) and D-433 (the start, now required for points) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, rankings, anti-cheat
**Context:** A day's key in `unoAlDia/<jid>/<n>` is the phone's local date (`numDia`, the date's UTC midnight in days since 1970). History has to stay writable for any day, because days played offline or before signing in go up later and keep the streak on another phone (`sincronizar`). A day's points flags (`w` for the week, `p` for the month's season) belong to the day itself, so they are tied to the server's time and to the day's start (D-433), and the nightly rebuild (D-415), which recomputes the month from that history, follows the same window.
**Decision:**
- **Rules:** `w` and `p` validate only when the day's UTC start `n × 86400000` is after `now − 2 days` and before `now + 1 day`, and `unoAlDiaInicio/<jid>/<n>` already exists. Every time zone fits (a local day lies between 14 hours before its UTC midnight and 36 hours after it), with the next morning to spare in most. The check is on `.validate`, so it also holds when the flags come inside the result's own write. The rules cannot turn text into a number, so the key's value is summed digit by digit with `matches` and `?:` (keys from 20000 to 29999, years 2024 to 2052); `uno-al-dia-jugador.test.mjs` runs the expression as JavaScript against `numDia` for several dates and hours.
- **Phone:** `subirHoy` and the "played before signing in" branch of `sincronizar` write the day's start (`empezarDia`, once, as before) before the result, in case the start of the attempt did not get through; `sincronizar` adds points only for today and yesterday.
- **Rebuild:** `uad-points-backfill.mjs` (`mesDe`, `rehacerMes`, `temporadaDe`) counts a played day's points only if its result's `at` (the server's time of the write) is within the same window of its date (`onTime`). A day uploaded later keeps the streak but earns nothing and gets no `p`, as on the live path.
- The test store (`jugador-local.js`) enforces the same, so the unit and end-to-end tests see what Firebase does.
**Why:** a day's points have to be earned on that day; the history keeps its place in the streak.
**Consequences:**
- Days played offline or on a phone before signing in, uploaded more than a day later, add no points (they still keep the streak). The rebuild applies the window to the whole month, so a total can go down once.
- A phone with no player counts every day in its own walk; once signed in, /today/ shows Firebase's total (D-415).
- The window is checked against the server's `now`, not the phone's clock.
