## D-426 · The owner opens a cup's recap to all its players while the window is in the lab
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-423 (the recap window, in the lab) · **Topics:** cup, home
**Context:** La Copa's recap window (D-423) opens only for lab key holders until the owner validates it. The owner asked to turn it on for the players of Thursday Guys #4 (`QNPFM`, 10 players), which ends on 2026-10-23 at midnight Pacific time; only 2 of its phones have notifications on, so the `fin` notification alone would reach few of them.
**Decision:** `OPEN_CUPS` in `public/assets/js/recap.js` lists the cups whose recap every player of theirs sees on the home page, lab key or not, under the same rules as D-423: once per cup, during the week after it ends, before the splash. It starts with `QNPFM`. Everyone else's cups, and Uno al día's season (D-424), still need the lab key; a phone whose open cup cannot be read does not get the season without one. Opening another cup is adding its code to the list, in a PR.
**Why:** The owner's request: try the window with a real group before it reaches everyone.
**Consequences:** Thursday Guys #4's players see the window from their first visit to the home page after the cup ends, for a week. The list can keep codes of cups already told: a cup older than a week is never due again.
**Later (2026-10-22):** the owner asked that the window be Thursday Guys #4's and no other cup's: a cup in `OPEN_CUPS` now goes first among the cups due on a phone (`dueCup`), so a newer cup that ended after it does not take its turn; once it is seen, the next one due comes as usual.
**Later (2026-10-23):** the recap window left the lab for everyone (D-423, D-424), so opening a cup to all its players is moot; `OPEN_CUPS` keeps only its order (a listed cup is told before the others due on a phone).
