## D-437 · The 3-day Copa, offered on the new create screen (in the lab)
**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-100 (the 3-day cup only for testing) and D-425 (it moves out of "More options"); behind the lab key (D-395, D-409) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** The owner asked for a 3-day modality of La Copa. It already existed since v0.43 (two games and the final, D-100), but only for testing: offered with `?three` in the URL or in the test store, and on the new create screen (D-425) folded under "More options". A week is a long commitment for a group that wants to try La Copa or has only a long weekend.
**Decision:** The new create screen offers the length up front, next to when it starts: **¿Cuánto dura?** with *Copa de 3 días* ("Dos juegos y la final.") and *Copa de 7 días* ("Seis juegos y la final."), starting at seven. Choosing three draws a week of two games, its title says "Los 3 días" instead of "La semana", and the line under the button says when it starts and ends. Like the whole new screen, it is seen only with a lab key (`labAccess()`, card `#seccion-copa-crear` in `/labs/`); the screen of before keeps the 3-day cup behind `?three` or the test store. The rules of the cup do not change: the 3-day cup was already played end to end by `tools/e2e/cup/tournament.mjs` (grace day on day 1, the final ×2 replaying both games, signing up until the final, and nobody can be out of the cup, since the last two days never count, D-347).
**Why:** the owner's request; it goes to the lab first (D-409) and reaches everyone when the new create screen does.
**Consequences:** `mode3Sub` drops "Para probar"; a new text `weekTitleDays` in the four languages. `tools/e2e/cup/create-week.mjs` checks the length is in the first panel, not in "More options", and that three days draws two games under "Los 3 días".
