## D-495 · Dudo's classic rules leave the lab, for everyone
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-487, D-490 (their "lab only"); follows D-409, D-479 · **Topics:** modes
**Context:** D-487 and D-490 built Dudo's classic Chilean rules behind the lab key: the setup switch (no opening in aces, half plus one into aces, calzar in a duel and with one die, a correct calza at five keeps five, the open obligada round), its folded Variants row (Pasar, Dudar a la siciliana) and the one-time line that says what calzar is. On 2026-10-28 the owner said: "Test all Dudo features yourself… If they pass, simply push to prod."
**Decision:** The owner's go. After the Dudo scripts passed (local, classic, languages, shake-clock, and the room scripts online and rooms-classic against the production Firebase), the `labAccess()` gate comes off: every player sees the **📜 Classic rules** switch (the 🧪 that marked a lab feature becomes 📜), off by default, with the same Variants row under it, and the one-time calzar line shows to everyone, with or without the classic rules. The lab card `#seccion-dudo-tradicional` moves to La Bodega marked 🚀 Lanzado (D-479).
**Why:** The owner asked for it, conditioned on the tests passing, and they did. With the switch off by default, a table that does not choose it plays exactly as before (D-69, D-71 stay the public defaults).
**Consequences:** `public/liars-dice/game.js` no longer imports `lab-access.js`. D-487 and D-490 stay in force for what the rules are; only their "offered only to lab key holders" is replaced by this decision.
