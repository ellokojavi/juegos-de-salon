## D-439 · The weekend cup: three days of three games, a one-off closed the night it opened
**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** closed for new cups the same night, 2026-10-23 (see *Closed early*); follows D-438 (the 3-day cup without a final) and D-418 (the final's rounds, reused); for everyone without the lab, at the owner's request (an exception to D-409) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** The owner asked for a tailor-made weekend Copa: "not one game per day, but 3 games per day for 3 days", scored "the same way as we do today, meaning relative points accumulated on the 3 games per player", every other trait of a normal cup kept, "available to me and to others", and "sunset by midnight Sunday".
**Decision:** A third length on both create screens (the one everyone sees and the lab's): **🎉 Copa de fin de semana · 3 días, 3 juegos cada día.** **Only in Spanish for now** (the owner's follow-up): it is offered on a screen in Spanish, and its words go in Spanish whatever is picked in More options. It was to be offered until the end of Sunday 2026-10-25, Pacific time (`FINDE_HASTA` in `public/cup/engine.js`), and was closed early (below); after that the option is gone, and the cups already created play on to their end. While it is offered it takes the plain 3-day cup's place on the lab's screen.
- **The days:** 🎲 draws nine different games of the pool, three a day, balanced like a week (D-425); 🎲 draws them again. A day is written `linea+tango+donde` in `meta.cal`, and its game is `trio` ("Tres juegos: ⏳ ☀️ 📍" on the board).
- **Playing a day** is the Grand Final's screen (D-418) with every game whole (none in its short version): one after the other, each with its how-to first, on one clock, one start and one result.
- **The score:** each game scores 0 to 100 as always; the day's score is the average of the three (the same order as their sum), and the day hands out the usual points by place (10-8-6-5…), the tie broken by the shorter active time over the three. "Relative points" are therefore the day's places, as in every cup.
- **Everything else is a normal cup's, as the 3-day cup of D-438:** no Grand Final and nothing worth double for everyone; the wildcard on any day; the grace day on days 1 and 2; out of the cup after two days in a row (never, in three days, D-347); 2 to 10 players; notifications, table, chart, podium, image and CSV.
- A day of three does not go to any game's ranking (it is not one game).
**Why:** the owner's request, for this weekend. Reusing the final's rounds keeps one result per player per day, so the database rules, the table and the notifications work unchanged.
**Consequences:** `engine.test.mjs` covers the calendar, the meta and the sunset; `tools/e2e/cup/tournament.mjs --part weekend` creates a weekend cup and plays day 1 with two players (in the test store `&timer` now also shortens Zip's and Desenredo's clock inside a cup's day). After the sunset the option disappears by itself; removing the code is a later cleanup.
**Closed early** (the owner, the same night, once their cup was created): "hide the weekend 3x3 Copa option from prod, but keep the one I created alive. This is a one-off Copa for friends." `FINDE_HASTA` moved to Friday 2026-10-23, 22:00 Pacific, so no create screen offers it; the cups already created play their three days as before, since the day of three (`trio`) and its calendar stay. The test store still offers it with `&weekend=on`, so `tournament.mjs --part weekend` keeps playing one.
