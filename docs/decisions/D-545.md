## D-545 · Each piece of news is told to a player once, by the first channel that shows it
**Date:** 2026-11-08 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-424 (the season's notification); extends D-431 (one window per visit) to notifications · **Topics:** home, pwa
**Context:** The owner noticed that a season of Uno al día could be told twice: the home page's recap window showed it, and hours later the notification arrived saying the same. The two channels kept separate accounts: the phone remembered what it saw (`temporada-vista` in localStorage), the sender only what it sent (`pushEnviadosDia`). The owner asked for a sensible logic between notifications and splash screens, so that the app is neither invasive nor redundant.
**Decision:**
- **The rule:** a piece of news (a season or a cup that ended, a campaign) is told to a player once, by whichever channel reaches them first; the other channel stays quiet about it.
- **Seen on the home page, not notified.** When the season's recap is seen, the phone tells its One a Day notifications (`pushDia/<subId>/ts` = the last season seen, `yyyy-mm`, sent with the rest through `refrescar`); `tools/push/daily.mjs` does not send `temporada:<month>` when `ts` is that month or later. A browser must show every push it receives, so the phone cannot drop it on arrival: the sender decides before sending.
- **Notified, not shown again:** tapping the notification opens `?recap=season`, which marks the season seen, so the home page does not show it again (already so since D-424).
- **What follows, in later PRs:** La Copa's ended cup (`fin`) gets the same treatment; and, behind a lab flag, a visit that arrives from a notification opens no home window, and a splash whose game the phone already played is not shown.
**Why:** The owner's request: what a player already saw should not interrupt them again. Keeping the seen season next to what the sender already reads costs one short field and no new read.
**Consequences:** Only phones with One a Day's notifications on carry `ts`; the others get no notification anyway. A phone that sees the recap with the notifications on but without permission any more writes nothing, and nothing is sent to it either. The Firebase rules gain the `ts` field (🔴 lane).
