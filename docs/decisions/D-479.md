## D-479 · Whatever leaves the lab for everyone goes to La Bodega
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** amended by D-510 · **Relation:** extends D-372 and D-422 (what La Bodega keeps), D-395 and D-409 (everything in development behind the lab) · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** La Bodega's ✨ Mejoras lanzadas already kept some launched improvements, but nothing
required it: the 🎃 Halloween card said 🚀 Lanzado and still sat under ✨ Mejoras en prueba.
**Decision:** a general rule, the owner's: when something tried in the lab is implemented for
everyone, its card moves to La Bodega in the same PR that launches it, folded (D-280): a game to
🎲 Juegos guardados, an improvement to ✨ Mejoras lanzadas, marked 🚀 Lanzado. Under 🎲 Juegos en
prueba and ✨ Mejoras en prueba stays only what is still being tried. The Halloween card moved now.
**Why:** the owner's request: the lab shows at a glance what still waits for validation.
**Consequences:** `public/labs/bodega.test.mjs` fails if a card on trial says 🚀, or if an
improvement on trial is no longer named by any page's `lab: #<id>` comment (its gate came off, so it
reached everyone). Canon C-1's lab paragraph says it.
