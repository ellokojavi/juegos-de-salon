## D-534 · A flag on *all* moves its card to La Bodega on its own, when /labs/ opens
**Date:** 2026-11-05 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-510 (the card moved in the next PR) · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** D-510 said a flag launched for everyone moves its card to La Bodega at once, but the move was a PR: someone had to edit `public/labs/index.html` after each 🚀, so launched cards sat under ✨ Mejoras en prueba for hours or days (`cup-again` on 2026-11-04, `cup-podium-early` on 2026-11-05). The owner asked (2026-11-05) that every lab feature marked *publish for everyone* go to La Bodega automatically.
**Decision:** `/labs/` reads `flags/` when it opens (`public/labs/launched.js`, the same public read as `flags.js`) and, before La Bodega folds its cards, moves every card under ✨ Mejoras en prueba whose flag is *all* into ✨ Mejoras lanzadas: folded like the cards written there, `data-launched` = the day the flag went live (`flags/<id>.at`), «🚀 Lanzado.» first, without the lab-only «Para verlo» sentence and with `&lab` out of its demo link. It keeps its 🧪 / 🟢 / ⚪ switch there (D-510). The HTML of `index.html` is not touched by the launch: the cleanup PR that removes the gate and the `FLAGS` row moves the card's HTML for good, because without the flag there is nothing to read.
**Why:** a launch is one tap on the switch (D-498); its card following by hand was the one step left that waited for a PR. Reading the flags on the lab page costs one request that the site already makes, and a flag taken back to *lab* puts the card back under ✨ Mejoras en prueba on the next visit, with nothing to undo.
**Consequences:** `public/labs/launched.test.mjs` checks which cards move and their day; `tools/e2e/labs/launched.mjs` the move on the page; `bodega.test.mjs` keeps checking the HTML as written (a card still in ✨ Mejoras en prueba is not marked 🚀 there). `publish.js` finds a moved card in La Bodega and gives it its switch without 📦. The cleanup PR's step (D-498) now also moves the HTML.
