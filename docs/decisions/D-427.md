## D-427 · La Copa's recap shows the final table as the image the cup shares
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-423 (the recap window's table), reuses D-141 and D-153 (the table image) · **Topics:** cup, home, sharing
**Context:** La Copa's recap window (D-423) drew its own table: a row per player with a small line of places and ▲▼. The owner asked that it show the same image the cup shares at the end (the podium's 📤): the trajectory day by day and the table, with the first three names on gold, silver and bronze plates.
**Decision:** The window shows that image in place of its rows. The drawing moves out of `cup/game.js` into `cup/table-image.js` (`imagenDeTabla`, `cabeceraTabla`, the chart's colors), with no screen state: it takes the cup, who looks, the time, the texts and the link. `game.js` shares it as before (the same pixels: checked against main), and the hidden page `/cup/recap/` (D-423) draws it after reading the cup, in the cup's language (D-170) like the shared one, with its link `juegosdesalon.cl/cup/?<alias or code>`, and posts the PNG with the rows. The page loads the app's fonts (Bangers, Nunito) and waits for them, since it has no base.css. The window keeps "La Copa terminó" and the sentence with who won and your place above the image; the cup's name, which the image carries, stays only for screen readers. If the image cannot be drawn, the window shows its own table as before. Uno al día's season (D-424) keeps its table: there is no shared image for it.
**Why:** The owner's request: the same picture the group already knows from the chat.
**Consequences:** The hidden page now also loads `rules.js` (the cup's texts) and `table-image.js`; the home page still loads none of La Copa's files (D-377). A change to the image runs the cup's scripts, as before, and `home/recap.mjs` checks the window shows a 1080 px image.
