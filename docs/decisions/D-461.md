## D-461 · The splash campaigns are measured: reach, taps and closes against the days each one was shown
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-454 (the 📣 Campaigns section) and D-44 (what is recorded) · **Topics:** panel, home
**Context:** The owner runs the splash campaigns from the panel (D-454): on, off, dates, order. Nothing
said what a campaign did once it was on, so there was no way to tell whether a window reached
people, whether they tapped it, or when it had run its course. The owner asked for a simple
analytics view in the same section, to understand reach and engagement against exposure time,
remembering that a campaign can be switched off and on again.
**Decision:** The window counts, per UTC day and campaign, in `stats/<env>/days/<day>/splash/<id>/<event>`
(counters only, like `support/`): `shown` each time it opens; `reach` the first time it opens on a phone
(a list of ids in `juegos-de-salon:splash-alcance`, so reach is unique phones without any id leaving
the phone); `play` its link tapped; `close` closed by ✕, Not now, the backdrop or Escape; and how long
it stayed open before either, in three bands (`t0` under 3 s, `t1` 3–10 s, `t2` 10 s or more). A preview
(`?splash=`, the lab demo) never counts. The rules accept only those events, each a +1.
The panel's 📣 Campaigns shows **📊 How they did** for the range chosen above: one row per campaign
shown in it (reach, with the tapped share, % tapped, days shown, new phones a day), and the chosen
one's detail: phones reached and windows per phone, % tapped through (play / reach), % closed,
coverage (windows over home page views on its days), % that stayed 3 s or more (and 10 s), days
shown, and one bar per day from its first day to its last. **Exposure is counted in days shown, not
calendar days**: a day with no window is drawn as `off` and the next day shown goes on as day n+1, so
switching a campaign off and on again does not dilute it. Saturation is read as new phones a day in
its first 3 days shown against the days after, and the days shown it took to gather half its reach
(`campaignStats` in `public/panel/campaigns.js`). Each campaign's days leave as CSV.
**Why:** Each phone sees a campaign once (D-351), so the natural curve of a campaign is falling reach as
the regulars have seen it; what the owner needs to decide is when it stopped finding new phones and
whether the ones it found acted on it. Days shown is the honest clock for that, because the switch can
pause it. Counters by day are what the panel already reads (`stats/<env>/days`), so nothing new is
downloaded and nothing identifies anyone.
**Consequences:** Counting starts with this version: earlier campaigns show nothing. Not behind the lab
key (D-395): players see no change, the counts only reach the owner's panel. A tap that opens a game
is counted, but whether that game was then finished is not attributed to the campaign (a next step if
the owner wants conversion). Reach is per phone and per browser: a phone that clears its storage counts again.
