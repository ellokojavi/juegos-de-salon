## D-412 · Uno al día points: the owner's answers, and the points saved in Firebase for lab key holders
**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-395 and D-409 (behind the lab) and D-230 (Uno al día with a player) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, rankings
**Context:** The lab prototype of Uno al día points (`/labs/daily-score/`) listed six open questions. The owner answered them all.
**Decision:** The streak bonus stays +5 a day, up to +50. The season is monthly (everyone back to 0 on the 1st); how its end is shown (days left, closing date or span) is chosen in the lab. Whoever finishes the month first wears a 🏆 champion badge next to their name the following month. Inviting a friend who finishes their first Uno al día gives a 🧊 and +10 points (tunable). Uno al día's notices and links open the player's Uno al día instead of rolling the dice. The points are saved in `records/uno-al-dia-pts/s<yyyy>-<mm>/<jid>` (a month, not a week), only for lab key holders for now: each day of the history adds once (marked `unoAlDia/<jid>/<n>/p` in the same write), its game score plus the streak bonus, and the rules let the streak `r` (counted up to 10) grow at most one per day added.
**Why:** The monthly season lets newcomers compete; the badge gives the month an end worth reaching; points for inviting make the wildcard worth earning. Checking each day against the history keeps the ranking honest, as the day and week tables already do.
**Consequences:** `firebase/database.rules.json` gains the `uno-al-dia-pts` clause and the `p` and `r` fields, mirrored by `jugador-local.js` and tested in `uno-al-dia-jugador.test.mjs`. The rules cannot see missed days (they do no date arithmetic), so the streak a phone claims is bounded (+1 a day, 10 at most, ≤ 10 on a month's first day) rather than recomputed; the invite points and the champion badge are not saved yet. The players' screens are still a prototype: the hub, the badge and the invite points reach players only after the lab.
**Later (2026-10-21):** the real screens are in `/labs/daily-score/live/`, and the month's ranking is the site's own `bloqueRanking` (tabs `mes`, `amigosMes`) in the hub, in /records/ and under Uno al día's result, all behind the lab key, so the three always agree.
**Later (2026-10-21):** the owner chose how the season's end is shown: "quedan X días" ("último día" on the last). The other two forms and the chips that chose between them are gone from the real screens.
