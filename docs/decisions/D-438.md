## D-438 · The 3-day Copa has no Grand Final
**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** amended by D-521 · **Relation:** amends D-437 (the 3-day cup on the new create screen) and D-100 (two games and the final); behind the lab key (D-395, D-409) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** D-437 offered the 3-day cup on the new create screen as it had been made for testing: two games and the Grand Final, which replays them and counts double. The owner, trying it: "For 3 days don't add the finals."
**Decision:** A 3-day cup created on the new create screen is **three games of the pool, with no final**: "Copa de 3 días · Tres juegos, sin final." Its calendar (`meta.cal`) ends with a game instead of `final`, and `meta.final` is `'0'`.
- **Nothing counts double for everyone:** every day is worth its points; the wildcard (×2) can go on any of the three days, the last one included.
- **The last day has no grace day**, as the final had: the cup closes at 23:59 of day 3, and the card and the reminder say "until today".
- **Signing up** stays open until the last day starts, and whoever arrives later reads "Esta copa ya está en su último día…" instead of "…en la final".
- **The tiebreak:** more days won, then the better place on the last day ("desempató quien quedó mejor el último día").
- **Notifications:** day 3 sends the usual "the day opened" instead of the final's.
- The 3-day cups from before (test cups, with the final) keep their final: whether a cup has a final is read from its calendar (`tieneFinal(meta)` in `public/cup/engine.js`), and `esFinal()` answers for that day only when it does. The screen of before (`?three`, test only) still makes 3-day cups with the final.
**Why:** the owner's request. Three days is a short tournament: a doubled third day would decide it almost alone, and a final that replays two games adds little.
**Consequences:** the Firebase rules accept `meta.final` = `'0'` (published by `publish.yml` on merge). `engine.test.mjs` covers the calendar, the meta, the wildcard on day 3 and the last-day tiebreak; `tools/e2e/cup/create-week.mjs` checks three games and no final row.
