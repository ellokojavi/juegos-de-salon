## D-466 · A splash campaign's conversion: the game its Play opened, started and finished
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-461 (what a campaign counts) · **Topics:** panel, home
**Context:** D-461 measured each splash campaign up to the tap on its Play link and said, as a next
step, that whether that game was then played was not attributed. The owner asked to measure
conversion too.
**Decision:** Tapping a campaign's Play leaves `{ id, at }` in the tab's sessionStorage
(`juegos-de-salon:splash-via`, written by splash.js). In that tab, within 2 hours of the tap
(`SPLASH_VIA_MS`), the first game started (`trackStart`) counts `splash/<id>/start` and the first game
finished counts `splash/<id>/finish`: an offline game through `trackFinish`, a room through `trackEnd`.
Each counts once per tap, so "Otra vez" does not inflate it; a finish with no start seen in the tab
counts both. The rules accept `start` and `finish` among the campaign's events. The panel's
📊 How they did adds **finished a game** (finishes over taps, with how many started one) and
**converted** (finishes over phones reached), in the campaign rows and on each day's bar.
**Why:** A tap is interest; a finished game is what the campaign was for. The tab is the honest unit:
the tap opens the game in the same tab, and sessionStorage leaves with it, so nothing is kept on the
phone beyond the visit and nothing identifies anyone (counters only, D-44).
**Consequences:** A game played in another tab, or the next day, is not attributed. Games that record
their end some other way (La Copa's results, which do not go through trackFinish) count as started but
not finished. Counting starts with this version.
