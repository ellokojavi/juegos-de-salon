## D-546 · News told once on the phone too: no home window after a notification, no splash for games already played
**Date:** 2026-11-08 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-545 (each piece of news told once) to the home page's windows (D-431) and the splash (D-351) · **Topics:** home, pwa
**Context:** D-545 stopped the season's notification after its recap was seen. The owner asked, in the same dialogue, for a logic between notifications and splash screens that is neither invasive nor redundant.
**Decision:** behind the runtime flag `news-once` (its /labs/ card, D-498):
- **A visit from a notification opens no home window** (`?notif=` in the URL): the player came for what it said; the recap, splash or install bubble wait for the next visit.
- **A splash for games already played is not shown.** `trackFinish` keeps the ids of the games this phone finished (`juegos-de-salon:jugados`, local only); a campaign whose items are all games in that list is skipped. A campaign with an item that is not a game (a teaser, the support toast) is unaffected. It is skipped, not marked seen, so turning the flag off restores it.
- **A cup's end seen on its board is not recapped.** The ended cup's notification opens its board; when the board shows the final podium, the cup is marked seen (`markCupSeen`), and the home page's recap does not tell it again. The other direction (recap seen first, then the `fin` notification) is left: `fin` goes out within the next 15 minutes of the end, before a visit is likely.
**Why:** The owner's request: what a player already saw or did should not interrupt them again.
**Consequences:** The privacy page lists the games played kept on the phone. The splash's measured reach (D-461) drops by the players who already knew the game, which is the point: its conversion counts the players it brought.
