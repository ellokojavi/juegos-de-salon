## D-409 · Every change goes to Labs first; decisions answer in three
**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-395 (behind the lab, now also listed in it) and D-273 (one-tap decisions) · **Topics:** agents, home
**Context:** the owner approved Toque y Fama's box choice (#604, #607). It was built behind the lab key (#611)
but not listed in `/labs/`, so the owner could not find it to try it.
**Decision:** owner's rule: every change goes to Labs first, for the owner to validate, unless the owner
says otherwise. What is behind the lab key on a page players visit is listed in `/labs/` (✨ Mejoras en
prueba, or the home page's experiments): the code that asks `labAccess()` names its card in a
`lab: #<id>` comment. A one-tap decision that changes something answers in three: **Yes, in Labs** (the
default), **Yes, and publish** and **No**. `processes.mjs decide` adds the third box on its own when the
Yes queues work: the same task, marked `[labs]` or `[publish]`; a prepared PR offers publishing only if
the decision names its own `publish` actions. A Yes that only takes note or closes something stays Yes / No.
**Why:** the owner validates what reaches players; something behind the lab that is not listed there
cannot be validated. Publishing directly stays one tap away for what the owner does not need to try.
**Consequences:** `public/labs/listed.test.mjs` fails when a page asks `labAccess()` without naming a card
that exists. The processes round builds a `[labs]` task behind the key with its card, and a `[publish]`
task for everyone. Taking a lab change to everyone is its own decision, once the owner has tried it.
