## D-432 · A back swipe mid-game asks before leaving
**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** complements D-86 · **Topics:** touch
**Context:** the owner noticed that the browser's back swipe (from the left edge) takes the page, and
the game with it, while playing. D-86's `overscroll-behavior: none` already stops the swipe that
belongs to the browser (Chrome on Android and on a Mac trackpad), but iOS's edge swipe (every iOS
browser) and Android's system Back gesture belong to the phone: no page can block them.
**Decision:** what a page can do is catch where both land: they only go back one history entry. While
a game is on, the page adds one entry for its own address (`public/assets/js/back-guard.js`), so a back
swipe lands on it and a bottom sheet asks **«¿Salir de la partida?»** with **▶ Seguir jugando** and
**Salir**. Seguir jugando puts the entry back (a second swipe asks again); Salir goes to the page before
the game. `trackStart` arms it and `trackFinish` / `trackEnd` disarm it (`transport/stats.js`), so every
game gets it at once; La Copa also arms it when a day resumes (Seguir jugando) and disarms it at the
result. Once the game is over the entry goes, and Back works as on any page. **The header's «‹ Menú»
asks the same** (the owner's request, 2026-10-23): tapping it mid-game is the same wish to leave, so
the guard catches it and Salir follows the link; before and after the game it is a plain link, and a
tap the page already handles (La Copa's day cover, which goes back to its board) does not ask. **In the lab first**
(D-409): only with a lab key; its card is `#seccion-volver-atras`.
**Why:** blocking the gesture is impossible on the phones where it hurts most, and a question costs
one tap to someone who meant to leave and saves the game of everyone who did not.
**Consequences:**
- Chrome skips, on Back, an entry a page added without a tap. `arm()` runs inside the tap that starts
  the game, and the lab key is asked when the guard is created so the answer is ready.
- No automated test can make the phone's gesture (as in D-86): `back-guard.test.mjs` checks the
  history logic against a fake history, and the gesture itself is tried on a real phone in the lab.
- Only «‹ Menú» (`a.back`, in every game's header) asks among the page's links: the buttons a game
  shows inside itself are its own, and what they do is the game's.
