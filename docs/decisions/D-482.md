## D-482 · Uno al día's share carries a neutral emoji grid, never one that spoils
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** amended by D-485, D-508 · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** random-and-daily, sharing
**Context:** #889 asked for a spoiler-free emoji result on every Uno al día game. Every La Copa solo
game already builds a share card (`resultado().t`), but Uno al día's share used none, and many cards
carry the game's emoji (👑, ☀️🌙, 🧶), which #215 forbids: the share never names the day's game.
**Decision:** one helper, `public/assets/js/share-grid.js`: `shareGrid(id, card)` keeps only neutral
marks (🟩🟨🟧🟥🟦🟪⬛⬜🟢🟡⚪🎯✅❌💡), at most 8 rows; `vidasGrid` gives El Ahorcado its lives. No grid
for Agentes (its card is the board in order: it shows where the agents are) or Conexiones (retired;
its rows are the groups), nor for Dudo and Generala (dice: the score says it) and Batalla Naval
(no per-shot record at the end; a later step). The grid goes between the header and the streak,
only with `labAccess()` while it was tried (card `#seccion-uad-grilla` in /labs/); for everyone since D-485.
**Why:** a grid is what gets a daily game shared (Wordle), and one filter over the cards the games
already have adds no per-game code.
**Consequences:** a new game's card is filtered the same way; one whose squares map the answer goes
in `SPOILS`. Going public is removing the `labAccess()` check in `compartirHoy`.
