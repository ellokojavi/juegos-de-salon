## D-443 · Every email leaves from hola@, and each one proves it
**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-317 · **Topics:** agents
**Context:** the owner's rule: every email the project sends goes out from hola@juegosdesalon.cl.
`send-mail.py` sent from `from` when the config had it and fell back to `user`, the owner's personal
Gmail, when it did not. And Gmail sends as hola@ only through its "Send mail as" alias: if the alias
stops working, Gmail does not fail, it rewrites From to the personal account without a word.
**Decision:** `send-mail.py` sends only from `SENDER`, hola@juegosdesalon.cl: a config whose `from`
is anything else is refused before connecting, with no fallback. After each email it reads the
copy in Gmail's Sent folder over IMAP (the same app password) and logs where it left from (`from`:
ok, other or unchecked). Before writing to anybody but the owner, the last checked email must have
left from hola@ less than a week ago; if not, a test email to the owner goes first, and the real
one follows only if that test left from hola@. `send-mail.py --check` runs the test by hand.
**Why:** a rule about the sender has to hold on its own, not by memory, and the failure Gmail hides
is the one that matters. The fallback is not another sender but not sending: the command exits with
an error, `feedback.mjs reply` stops before closing the report, and the reply goes out once the
alias is fixed, so nothing is lost and nothing leaves from the personal address.
**Consequences:** at most one test email a week to the owner, in their inbox. If IMAP is off in
Gmail the check cannot read the Sent folder, and no email to a player leaves until it is on. A
rewritten sender on a real email (the alias broke between checks) is reported as an error and
forces a check before the next one.
