## D-449 · ☕ Invítame a un café is a card over the page, not a page of its own (in the lab)
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-448 (the support page) and D-431 (a `?support=` visit opens no other window) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** D-448 put the support invitation on a page of its own, `/labs/support/`, which the
footer link and the end-of-game link opened. The owner looked at it and asked for a card that
rises over the screen the player is on, the way the home page's share sheet does, taking less of
the screen if it can: it does not deserve a view of its own.
**Decision:** The two links open the same sheet from the bottom (`openSupportSheet` in
`public/assets/js/support.js`), built like the share sheet (`.hoja`): the page behind stays put,
and a tap outside, Escape or "Ahora no" closes it. It holds the title, one sentence of what the
money is for, one short line on how paying works (you choose how much, Stripe, the card is never
seen), the pay button and its note. It fits a 320×568 phone in German, the longest language,
without scrolling. Stripe returns the donor to the home page with `?support=thanks`, which opens
the thank-you as the same sheet; `?support=open` opens the card (the lab's link to it). While
`?support=` is in the address, the home page's own windows (D-431) wait for another visit. The
page `/labs/support/` is gone.
**Why:** The invitation is a short, optional aside: a sheet over the screen keeps the player
where they were, the end of their game or the home page, so saying "not now" costs one tap and
nothing is lost. A page of its own made a small ask feel bigger than it is.
**Consequences:** The Payment Link's confirmation page is `https://juegosdesalon.cl/?support=thanks`,
and it stays that when support leaves the lab: only the `labAccess()` checks go.
**Update (2026-10-26):** those checks went (PR `support-public`): the card is for every player, and
`?support=open` is no longer the lab's link only. Opening the card
is what counts `open` and rests the end-of-game invitation for 30 days (D-448).
