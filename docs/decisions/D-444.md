## D-444 · An unused wildcard is applied on its own before the Grand Final
**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-94 (one ×2 wildcard per player: an unused one is no longer lost); the gold ×2 of D-151 marks it too, as **×2 auto**; published to everyone at the owner's request, not behind the lab (D-409) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** Each player has one wildcard (×2) per cup and picks the day before tapping Empezar; it is never valid in the final. A player who forgot it lost it. The owner: "make sure that the card to double the points is always applied at the very end of the season… so that it doesn't get lost. Of course, this happens to the last game, not to the finals, because the finals is always double."
**Decision:** In a cup with a Grand Final, a wildcard nobody used lands **on the last day before the final that the player played** (day 6 in the 7-day cup; an earlier one if they skipped day 6, so it never doubles a 0).
- **When:** only once the player can no longer use it on any day before the final: each of those days is played, started or closed. Until then nothing is applied, so a player who plays day 6 and still has day 5 in its grace day can spend it on day 5 by hand.
- **Nothing is written:** the engine reads it from the store (`comodinAuto`, `comodinEfectivo` and `multiplicador(L, d, pid, now)` in `public/cup/engine.js`), and it holds **retroactively for every cup still running** when this was published (the owner's request): it applies to a cup whose `meta.end` is after `AUTO_WILD_FROM` (2026-10-25T17:00:00Z, in `engine.js`). A cup that had already ended keeps its table and its saved podium (`torneoPodios`) as they were. The table, the day table, the shared image, the spreadsheet ("comodín (auto)"), the owner's panel and the notifications all count it.
- **What the player sees:** on day 6, not played yet and with the wildcard unused, the day card and the game's cover say "🃏 No has usado tu comodín ×2. Se aplicará hoy automáticamente." (the owner's wording, with ×2 as in the pills). Once applied, the day carries a gold **×2 auto** pill (in "Tus días", the day card and the day table) and its result screen says "🃏 No usaste tu comodín: se aplicó automáticamente al día {d}, el último que jugaste antes de la final."; the score breakdown says it too. The wildcard's explanation on the earlier days, and the Cup's rules, say that an unused one goes on its own to the last day played before the final.
- **A cup without a final** (the 3-day cups, D-438, and the weekend cup, D-439): the owner first left them out, then asked for them too the same morning, with the weekend cup running. There the wildcard is valid on any day, so it lands on the last day the player played, the cup's last day included; the warning shows on that last day, and the result note and breakdown drop "antes de la final" (`wildAutoNoteLast`, `bdWildAutoLast`, `wildOffAutoLast`). `ultimoDiaComodin(meta)` in `engine.js` says which day is the last one a wildcard can go on.
- **Not for:** a cup the admin ended before its last day (D-161): there an unused wildcard stays unused, as before.
- The "Usar el comodín en este día" button stays on day 6: using it by hand there gives the same result.
**Why:** the owner's request. A wildcard is part of everyone's score; losing it to forgetfulness punished the players least used to the rules and made the table say less about how they played.
**Consequences:** the totals of a cup in progress can rise on the final's day for whoever never used the wildcard. `engine.test.mjs` covers when it lands, which day, the grace-day case, a day started and not finished, the final, a cup without a final (the last day played, the last day included) and one ended early; those tests use a cup from 2026-10-20, still running at the cut-off, and check that one ended before it gets nothing; the fixtures of the medals, tiebreak, panel and notification tests are cups ended before the cut-off, so their totals did not change. The `last-day` and `last-day-three` demos and the `copa` look paths `wild-today`, `wild-today-cover`, `wild-today-three`, `wild-auto` and `wild-auto-result` show the screens.
