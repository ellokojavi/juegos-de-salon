## D-444 · An unused wildcard is applied on its own before the Grand Final
**Date:** 2026-10-25 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-94 (one ×2 wildcard per player: an unused one is no longer lost); the gold ×2 of D-151 marks it too, as **×2 auto**; published to everyone at the owner's request, not behind the lab (D-409) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** Each player has one wildcard (×2) per cup and picks the day before tapping Empezar; it is never valid in the final. A player who forgot it lost it. The owner: "make sure that the card to double the points is always applied at the very end of the season… so that it doesn't get lost. Of course, this happens to the last game, not to the finals, because the finals is always double."
**Decision:** In a cup with a Grand Final, a wildcard nobody used lands **on the last day before the final that the player played** (day 6 in the 7-day cup; an earlier one if they skipped day 6, so it never doubles a 0).
- **When:** only once the player can no longer use it on any day before the final: each of those days is played, started or closed. Until then nothing is applied, so a player who plays day 6 and still has day 5 in its grace day can spend it on day 5 by hand.
- **Nothing is written:** the engine reads it from the store (`comodinAuto`, `comodinEfectivo` and `multiplicador(L, d, pid, now)` in `public/cup/engine.js`), so it also holds for the cups already running when this was published. The table, the day table, the shared image, the spreadsheet ("comodín (auto)"), the owner's panel and the notifications all count it.
- **What the player sees:** on day 6, not played yet and with the wildcard unused, the day card and the game's cover say "🃏 No has usado tu comodín ×2. Se aplicará hoy automáticamente." (the owner's wording, with ×2 as in the pills). Once applied, the day carries a gold **×2 auto** pill (in "Tus días", the day card and the day table) and its result screen says "🃏 No usaste tu comodín: se aplicó solo al día {d}, el último que jugaste antes de la final."; the score breakdown says it too. The wildcard's explanation on the earlier days, and the Cup's rules, say that an unused one goes on its own to the last day played before the final.
- **Not for:** a cup without a final (the 3-day cup, D-438, which the owner left out of this) and a cup the admin ended before its final (D-161): there an unused wildcard stays unused, as before.
- The "Usar el comodín en este día" button stays on day 6: using it by hand there gives the same result.
**Why:** the owner's request. A wildcard is part of everyone's score; losing it to forgetfulness punished the players least used to the rules and made the table say less about how they played.
**Consequences:** the totals of a cup in progress can rise on the final's day for whoever never used the wildcard. `engine.test.mjs` covers when it lands, which day, the grace-day case, a day started and not finished, the final, a cup without a final and one ended early; the fixtures of the medals, tiebreak, panel and final-notification tests changed their totals or spend their wildcards on an unplayed day. The `last-day` demo and the `copa` look paths `wild-today`, `wild-today-cover`, `wild-auto` and `wild-auto-result` show the four screens.
