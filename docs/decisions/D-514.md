## D-514 · German leaves the app
**Date:** 2026-10-30 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends C-3, D-47 and D-197 · **Topics:** languages
**Context:** German came in through the lab (D-191) and left it in D-197. Few players come from German-speaking
countries, and those who did also read Spanish or English. Every new text for the player still had to be written
in four languages, German being the hardest to keep right (long compounds that break buttons, a glossary,
nobody on the team to review it).
**Decision:** the app offers Spanish (default), English and Portuguese. Every German text, deck and grid is
removed. Nothing breaks for whoever had it: `RETIRADOS = { de: 'en' }` in `i18n.js` turns a stored `de` and a
`?lang=de` link into English, the daily push does the same for a phone subscribed in German, and `/de/` is a
plain redirect to `/en/` (no card, `noindex`). Cups already created on Berlin time keep their `meta.tz`.
**Why:** a careful English serves a German speaker better than a German no one can check, and a third of the
translation work goes to a very small audience.
**Consequences:** C-3 and the parity tests ask for three languages. A language that returns comes in through
the lab (D-191) like any new one; `docs/ALEMAN.md` stays as history.
