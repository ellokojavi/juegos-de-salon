## D-512 · Firebase moves to the pay-as-you-go Blaze plan, with a US$10 alert-only budget
**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-18 (created on Spark); the small-read rules of D-470, D-473 and D-499, written while staying on Spark, stay in force · **Topics:** rooms
**Context:** The owner wants to post the site beyond friends (Reddit and others). On Spark the database refused new connections past about 100 at once (≈50 two-phone games): one popular post would take multiplayer and La Copa down, with nothing to do in the moment.
**Decision:** The project `juegos-de-salon` is on **Blaze** since 2026-10-29. It still belongs to the machine's account; it is billed to the Cloud Billing account **Juegos de Salón** (`01B627-966553-2CB035`), owned by the owner's personal account, US, with the machine's account as *Billing Account User*. A budget **Juegos de Salón - monthly** (US$10/month, all projects, **alerts only**: 50 %, 90 %, 100 %) emails the billing admins. **No enforced spend cap**: it would pause the database for everyone mid-game, the same failure Spark had.
**Why:** Blaze keeps Spark's free quotas, so the expected bill is $0, and lifts the connection cap to about 200,000. A bill from abuse is the new risk; the strict rules, the existing alerts and the budget email cover it, and unlinking billing returns the project to Spark.
**Consequences:** `firebase/README.md` describes the plan, the accounts and the alerts. The reads kept small for Spark (D-470, D-473, D-499) stay: the download quota is now what costs money. No paid service (Cloud Functions, Storage) is turned on without its own decision.
