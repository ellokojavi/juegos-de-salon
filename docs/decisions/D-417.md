## D-417 · La Gran Final's running score is the average so far, out of 100
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-113 (every mini-game from 0 to 100; the final is the average of its rounds) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** a player wrote to hola@ (#637): between rounds the final said "Llevas X de 300 puntos" (100 times the rounds played) while the maximum is 100. The number was `puntaje()`, the average over all five rounds with the missing ones as 0, and the maximum was the sum of the rounds played: two scales in one sentence. Three perfect rounds read "60 of 300".
**Decision:** the owner chose it: between rounds the intro shows the average of the rounds played so far, out of 100 (`puntajeParcial` in `public/cup/games/final/engine.js`), with the words saying it is an average: "Vas con {pts} de 100: el promedio de tus rondas hasta ahora", in the four languages. After the fifth round it is exactly the result.
**Why:** it is the only reading on the result's scale and the rule's (each round from 0 to 100, the final their average), so nothing jumps to another scale on the results screen. It can go down from one round to the next, which is how the final actually stands.
**Alternatives discarded:**
- **The raw sum out of rounds × 100** ("300 of 300"): literal, but it jumps to /100 at the end, the confusion the player reported.
- **Points banked toward 100** ("60 of 100"): it only grows, but it needs each round to be worth 20, against the 0 to 100 every round shows.
- **The per-round card** (⏳100 🔢85 …) instead of a number: unambiguous, but with no quick sense of how you stand; the owner did not want it added.
**Consequences:** `juegos.test.mjs` checks the running score after three rounds and that it equals `puntaje` after five. Scoring, the table and the ×2 of the final do not change.
