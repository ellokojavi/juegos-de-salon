## D-555 · Answered decision issues are swept by code: closed when done, settled when asked again, and a branch is deleted on the tick
**Date:** 2026-11-14 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-273 (one-tap decisions), D-411 (settling a question) and D-441 (`pending-watch.yml`) · **Topics:** agents
**Context:** the owner found four decision issues open, three with nothing for them to do: #1381 answered and built (#1417 merged two days before), #1415 asking the same three questions that #1435 asked the next day, and #1435 answered but with its queued tasks undone, one of them deleting a branch the round had no permission to delete. Closing depended on the round remembering, at the end, to record the work and close the issue. The owner asked that they never have to clean these by hand.
**Decision:** (1) `tools/agents/sweep.mjs` runs first in `pending-watch.yml` every day: for each open `processes` issue, a question that a newer open issue asks again (same words, or naming the same branch, id or PR) is settled "asked again in #n"; an issue with every decision made and every queued task done (in `queueDone`, or a merged PR linked in a comment by the owner's account) is closed with the reason; a queued task still undone two days after the issue opened is listed under "Answered decisions nobody carried out" in the "Pending work" issue. (2) `processes.mjs decide` refuses a decision an open issue already asks and the owner has not answered. (3) A new `delete-branch` action deletes a branch when the owner ticks it (`approve.yml` has the permission), instead of queueing it for a round that may not.
**Why:** a round that forgets is the failure, so the close is the outcome checked from GitHub, as in D-440 and D-441. Repeated questions cost the owner twice and leave the first unanswerable. A branch deletion needs no judgement after the Yes, so waiting for a round only adds a day and a permission it may lack.
**Consequences:** `sweep.mjs` and `sweep.test.mjs`, `pending.mjs` (the section and the call), `pending-watch.yml`, `approve.mjs` (`delete-branch`), `processes.mjs` (`repeatedIn` in `decide`, the action's words) and `.claude/agents/processes.md`. Its first run closes #1381 and #1415.
