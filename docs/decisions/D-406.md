## D-406 · A lab page to choose how the two filter rows look
**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** amended by D-407 · **Relation:** extends D-362 and D-369 (the shortcut row above the types), D-390 (the 🔎 pill) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** the owner asked whether the home page's first filter row (🔎 ⭐ Favoritos ✨ Nuevos 🔥 Populares) should look slightly different from the second (the types), and asked for a simple page to compare options and pick one. Both rows are one single choice (`filtro.tipo`): a treatment that makes them look like two independent filters would promise a combination that does not exist.
**Decision:** `public/labs/pills/` shows six treatments side by side, each with its pros and cons, at 320, 390 and 600 px: A as today, B shortcuts outlined (recommended), C shortcuts faint and types stronger, D types as tabs, E shortcuts tinted by their emoji, F the 🔎 as a round action apart plus B. In every option the chosen pill is the same cyan in both rows, except D, which shows the cost of breaking that. A tap selects one pill across both rows, as on the home page; nothing filters. A "👍 Me gusta esta" button remembers the pick on that phone only. The page is listed among the lab's improvements; the home page does not change until the owner picks.
**Why:** a visual choice is made looking at it, not from a description.
**Consequences:** once one is picked, it reaches the home page in its own PR (C-17), and this page goes to La Bodega.
