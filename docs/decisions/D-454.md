## D-454 · The splash campaigns are run from the panel
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-351 (how a campaign is switched) · **Topics:** panel, marketing
**Context:** The owner wanted to manage the splash campaigns from the phone: turn one on or off, set its end day, see and order the ones that overlap. Switching one meant rewriting `ACTIVE` in `splash.js` with `splash.mjs` and a PR, which needs a session.
**Decision:** The panel has a 📣 Campaigns section (`#/campaigns`, `public/panel/campaigns.js`): one card per campaign of `CAMPAIGNS` with an on switch, start and end days (with +1 week, +2 weeks, +1 month), ▲▼ order and a preview link, what the home page shows today, the overlaps of the next 60 days and a 30-day strip. Each change writes Firebase `splash/<id>` = `{ on, order, from?, until?, at }` (public read, owner write). The home page reads `splash.json` once per visit (`loadActive`, 2.5 s timeout) and uses it as the queue (`fromRemote`); while the panel has never saved, or Firebase does not answer, `ACTIVE` holds. `from` (inclusive) is new: a campaign can be scheduled. Making a campaign (texts, clips) still goes through a PR in `CAMPAIGNS`, and the owner asks Claude for it.
**Why:** Dates and order are what change during a campaign, and they are data, not code: a PR and a publish for each one is slow from a phone. The fallback keeps today's behaviour when the database is not reachable.
**Consequences:** One small public read per home page visit (not under the end-to-end scripts). `splash.mjs` still edits `ACTIVE`, which only matters until the panel's first save; after that the panel is the switch. Ids in `splash/` must match `[a-z0-9-]`.
