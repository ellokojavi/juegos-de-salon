## D-431 · One window per visit on the home page, through one gate
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current (in the lab) · **Relation:** amends D-351 and D-430 · **Topics:** home
**Context:** the home page opens windows on its own when it loads: La Copa's recap (D-423) or Uno al
día's season recap (D-424), the promotional splash (D-351), which since D-430 may chain several
campaigns one after another, and the bubble that invites to add the app to the home screen (D-232).
Each decided alone (the recap only held the splash back), so a returning player could get two splash
windows in a row and the install bubble on top. The owner asked that they never pile up.
**Decision:** `public/assets/js/home-windows.js` is the one gate for every window the home page opens
on its own, and it opens at most one per visit, by a fixed priority: **recap, then splash, then
install bubble, then the rest** (`PRIORITY`). The splash shows **only the first pending campaign** of
D-430's queue, in its order; the next one waits for the next visit. A recap that turns out to have
nothing to show (no network, the cup's data gone) gives way to the next candidate, never to a second
campaign. The choice is pure (`readPending` reads what the phone keeps, in every format it has kept,
including the bare splash id from before D-430 and the install bubble's '1'; `choose` returns the one
window and leaves the rest), tested in `home-windows.test.mjs`. Not part of the gate: what the player
opens by tapping (the share sheet, the inbox, the install steps, menus), what is not a window (the
dice's arrival veil, Uno al día's app badge) and the lab's `?lab` bar; `?recap=` and `?splash=`
previews behave as before. **In the lab first** (D-409, the owner's choice): with a lab key the gate
decides; without one the home page behaves as before. Its card is `#seccion-una-ventana`.
**Why:** one gate with one order is the only place where "never two at once" can hold; spread over each
window's own code, every new window would have to know about all the others.
**Consequences:** a campaign may expire before a player who visits rarely reaches it: with two
campaigns on, someone who comes once sees only the first. That is accepted; the queue keeps its order
and a campaign that matters can be given a longer `until`. A new window the home page opens on its own
goes into `PRIORITY` and `readPending`, not beside them.
