## D-544 · La Copa offline: a pending day plays on the phone and uploads before its day closes
**Date:** 2026-11-08 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-538 (La Copa was never offline, never queued) · **Topics:** pwa, cup
**Context:** D-538 left La Copa out of offline mode because its days, deadlines and PIN need the server. The owner (2026-11-08): a player with a pending day should be able to play it with no signal; the score waits on the phone and uploads when the signal is back, with an expiry like any game: if the signal does not come back in time, the day counts as not played.
**Decision:**
- **Its own lab flag, `cup-offline`** (card #seccion-cup-offline in /labs/), on top of offline mode: with it off, La Copa stays as D-538 left it.
- **Only cups this phone already knows.** While online, each read of a cup the phone is seated in is kept whole with the server's clock offset (`cuenta.snapshot`, only while the flag `cup-offline` is on and the cup is on). Offline, `/cup/` opens from that copy (`store-offline.js`): the board says the table is from the last signal, and a pending day plays as always. A cup never opened online on this phone, creating, joining, the wildcard and the admin need signal (`data-signal`, dimmed with "📡 Con señal").
- **The expiry is the server's.** The result waits in an outbox (`assets/js/cup-outbox.js`) and uploads start first, then result, with the server's clock: the rules already refuse both outside the day's window (grace day included), so a result that arrives after its day closed is refused and the day counts as not played. No rule change. The phone also refuses offline a day already closed by its own clock plus the kept offset.
- **The signal lost mid-game** is the same case: an online result that cannot be written goes to the outbox while its day is open.
- **One attempt, still.** A day queued on this phone cannot be queued again; a result already on the server (played on another phone) wins, and the phone's is dropped. The start goes up before the result, so the rules for a player out of the cup or a cup the admin ended still apply.
- **Said three times** like the other games: on the day's cover (it has to upload before the day closes, at that time), on the result screen ("Guardado en tu celular…"), and in the reconnect toast (days uploaded, days that arrived after the close, days already played elsewhere). The chip counts queued days. Uploads run from any page, through the rankings' Firebase store.
**Why:** a player on a plane or with no data loses a day of the cup for nothing, though the game itself is on the phone; the server's windows already give the right expiry.
**Consequences:** `/cup/` is precached (its own files and what they import, not `cup/games/`'s pictures); the copy is a few KB per cup in course. Clearing the browser's storage before the signal comes back loses the day, as for any queued game. Offline, the table and other players' results are the last ones seen.
