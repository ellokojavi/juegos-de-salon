## D-488 · One "Cómo se juega" on a game's cover: who plays, how and what it costs go inside it, never in a separate FAQ
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends PR #909 (the lab's collapsed ❓ FAQ under the rules, which had no decision of its own) and the FAQ part of #888 · **Topics:** identity, modes
**Context:** #909 put a collapsed ❓ "Preguntas frecuentes" block, three questions, under "Cómo se
juega" on six covers, in the lab. The owner found a second block explaining the game overkill on a
cover that should be easy, and #887 removed it; the rule was agreed but never recorded as a decision.
**Decision:**
- **A game's cover has one section that explains it, "Cómo se juega".** No FAQ block beside it, no
  question list, no second heading, no second collapsible (U-18).
- What the FAQ answered (players and minutes, one phone, several or alone, free with nothing to
  install and no account) is the **first line inside "Cómo se juega"**, three short sentences, built
  by `public/assets/js/how-to-play-facts.js` from `games.js` and the modes the cover offers, so it
  cannot go stale. It does not repeat the rules text. Tried in the lab first (D-395, D-409), card `#seccion-faq`;
  launched for everyone on 2026-10-28 at the owner's request from /labs/, in Timeline, Bulls and Cows, Hangman, Liar's Dice, Generala and Battleship.
- Search engines keep getting the rules as a one-question FAQPage (`tools/release/og.mjs`); the
  facts line joined that JSON-LD when it left the lab on 2026-10-28 (Google wants what every visitor sees).
- **The Cup games' own pages** (Reinas, Zip, Tango…, issue #883 W1.2, owner on 2026-10-29) follow the same rule: the facts line opens their one "Cómo se juega" (lab flag `cover-facts`, card `#seccion-cover-facts`; alone, since `games.js` gives them no `players`), and `og.mjs` gives search engines their Cup rules (`JUEGOS_COPA[id].como` in `public/cup/rules.js`) as the same one-question FAQPage; since `cover-facts` went to *all* (2026-10-29), that answer opens with the facts line too, as the page shows it.
**Why:** the owner's rule: one block explains the game; a FAQ on a game page is overkill.
**Consequences:** `how-to-play-facts.test.mjs` checks the four languages, a three-sentence line per
game and language, and that no cover carries a separate FAQ block.
