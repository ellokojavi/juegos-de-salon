## D-408 · The panel's timeline of key events
**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-207 and D-348 (a new panel section) · **Topics:** panel
**Context:** the owner asked for a timeline in the panel of the "moments of truth" (a game played to its end, feedback sent…), with who and when, filtered by day, week and month, to see how many happen and when. Opening a page, starting a game or leaving it halfway are not key events.
**Decision:** `#/timeline` (🕑 Timeline, after Summary) lists, newest first and grouped by Pacific date, every room that reached an ending, every offline game and One a Day with an ending, every La Copa day played, every cup created, joined or finished with a winner (one the admin cut short does not count), and every feedback message. Each kind is a tile with its count and its change against the period before, and a toggle filter; "When" counts them by day, week or month (tap a period to list only it) and "Time of day" by Pacific hour. The pure accounts are `public/panel/timeline.js`; it reads what the panel already reads, plus `feedback/` (public read).
**Why:** the aggregate numbers say how much; the owner also wants to see who and when, one event at a time, to read the rhythm of real use. It only shows names the panel already shows in its logs (rooms, offline games, cups); it is not searchable by person (D-44).
**Consequences:** rooms and offline games follow the panel's environment; cups and feedback have none. The range's days are read as Pacific dates ending today in Pacific time (`pacificRange`). A new kind of key event is one more branch in `keyEvents` and one more line in `KIND` in panel.js.
