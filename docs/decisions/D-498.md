## D-498 · Lab gates are runtime flags: "Yes, and publish" is a toggle
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** amended by D-509, D-510, D-515 · **Relation:** amends D-395 (how a feature in development is gated) and D-409 (publishing a lab change is its own PR); extends D-477 (📣 Campaigns gains 🚩 Lab flags) and D-480 (🚀 Publicar para todos) · **Topics:** structure, panel
**Context:** the 2026-10-27 audit (#834, #857) counted about 81 lab PRs against 19 "for everyone" PRs, in pairs like #726→#729: each `labAccess()` gate was written by hand, so publishing a validated change was another code PR with its end-to-end run and its agents. The owner decided (2026-10-28): runtime flags in Firebase, switched from the panel.
**Decision:** a feature tried on a page players visit asks `flag('<id>')` (`public/assets/js/flags.js`) instead of `labAccess()`. Firebase `flags/<id>` = `{ state: 'lab' | 'all' | 'off', at }`, public to read, only the owner writes it: *lab* is the lab key holders only (D-395, and the default with nothing saved or no answer), *all* is every player, *off* is nobody, the lab included. The page reads `flags/` once per visit and keeps the answer on the device, as `controls.js` does. Each id is a row of `FLAGS` and a card `seccion-<id>` in `/labs/`. The owner flips them in 📣 Campaigns → **🚩 Lab flags** (🧪 Lab / 🚀 Everyone / ⚪ Off), or with 🚀 Publicar para todos on the card itself, which for a flagged card writes `all` at once (no Mac mini, no PR) and then offers ↩️ Volver al laboratorio. Cards without a flag (a game, or an improvement still gated by `labAccess()`) keep D-480's PR flow. Previews of things that have their own switch (the splash campaigns, the seasonal templates, the home page's `?lab` bar) keep asking `labAccess()`.
**Why:** publishing what the owner already validated is data, not code: a toggle is instant from a phone and costs no CI, agents or session. Taking it back is the same tap.
**Consequences:** a new lab feature on a page players visit gets a flag (a row in `FLAGS`, its card, `flag('<id>')` with its `lab: #seccion-<id>` comment); `public/labs/listed.test.mjs` fails if a `flag()` id has no row or no card, or a row is asked by no page. A flag left on *all* is cleaned up later in an ordinary PR that removes the gate and its row and moves the card to La Bodega (D-479); until then the card stays under ✨ Mejoras en prueba and says 🟢 Publicado para todos. `firebase/flags.test.mjs` checks the rules, `public/assets/js/flags.test.mjs` the reading, `tools/e2e/labs/publish.mjs` the flagged card. Today's flags: `copa-crear`, `bn-salva` (Dudo's classic rules left the lab by PR the same day, D-495).
