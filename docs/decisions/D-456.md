## D-456 · Tren en Vilo leaves the lab: its page, Uno al día and La Copa, launched with its splash
**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-332 and D-421 (how a lab game graduates) and D-340, D-346, D-387 (Tren en Vilo in the lab); amends D-163 (the pool) and D-334 (Uno al día's deck); uses D-351, D-430 and D-454 (the splash and its panel) · **Topics:** cup, random-and-daily, home, marketing
**Context:** 🚂 Tren en Vilo, the bridge builder inspired by Poly Bridge, was tried in the lab in Spanish only, with its own page (`labs/bridges/game.js`) ahead of La Copa's screen (`cup/games/bridges/ui.js`, made for the owner's one-day cup of 2026-10-25): the deck laid from the start, steel on the later crossings, the house style (neon) the owner approved, the visitors, a summary after each stage and the bridge that could have crossed. The owner asked to graduate it to production with a splash that shows the game, says what it is and that it is in Uno al día and La Copa, with a button to play, and not to publish the game until that splash is on.
**Decision:** Owner's request.
- **One screen, La Copa's** (D-198, D-331): `cup/games/bridges/ui.js` takes everything the lab's page had, with the cup's clock (a stage's summary pauses it with `ctx.pausa`) and its texts in the four languages, and serves the standalone page `/bridges/`, Uno al día and La Copa. The lab's engine, scene, art and catalogs move to `cup/games/bridges/` (`physics.js` is the lab's `engine.js`; the catalogs in English: `catalog.json`, the wood one played, and `catalog-beams.json`; the tighter test catalog goes away). `labs/bridges/` becomes a bridge to `/bridges/` (`deLabs`); its sheets of trains and visitors stay in the lab, listed in 📦 La Bodega as 🚀 Lanzado.
- **The house style everywhere** (`NEON` in `scene.js`); `?style=pixel` still brings back the pixel art. The visitors' parameters (`?ovni`, `?ballena`, `?topo`, `?wake`) stay the owner's: only from the workshop or with a lab key. The lab's ⏭ stage skip does not come along.
- **On the home page** as `puentes` (La Copa's id), ✨ Nuevo from 2026-10-26, type 🧩 Lógica, 5–7 min. **In La Copa's pool** (`POZO`); the Gran Final still skips it (`NOT_IN_FINAL`: seven minutes of building). **In Uno al día** from 2026-10-27 (`DESDE_PUENTES`), joining the next deck shuffled; no day before changes.
- **Its splash** `tren-en-vilo-ya` (a clip of a bridge built and crossed, its name, what it is, that it is in Uno al día and La Copa, and ▶ Jugar) replaces the teaser `tren-en-vilo-pronto`. Since the panel's queue in Firebase rules over `ACTIVE` (D-454), the campaign is switched on there before the merge: until the new code is live, `queue()` ignores an id it does not know, so the game and its splash reach players together.
**Why:** the owner validated the game in the lab and wants it in front of everybody, announced.
**Alternatives discarded:** keeping the lab page as the production game (a second screen to keep in step with La Copa's, without the cup's clock, ranking, sharing and 🐞 report); publishing first and switching the splash on later (the owner asked for both together).
**Consequences:** `tools/e2e/bridges/game.mjs` plays it end to end at `/bridges/` (the deck, a real drag, the five crossings with their summaries and the paused clock, the result, a given-up game's solution, 320 px in four languages). `uno-al-dia.test.mjs` counts 19 games. The lab's sheets load `cup/games/bridges/`, declared in `REUSES` (`tools/e2e/affected.mjs`).
