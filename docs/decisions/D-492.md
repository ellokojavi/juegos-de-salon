## D-492 · An archived Cup's memorial, for everyone
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-478 · **Topics:** cup, rankings
**Context:** D-478 put the memorial of an archived Cup (its name, when it ended and its podium, instead of "No encontramos una copa con ese código") behind the lab key. The owner tapped 🚀 Publicar para todos on its card `#seccion-copa-archivada` in `/labs/` (D-480).
**Decision:** the `labAccess()` gate is gone: every player who opens the link of an archived Cup with a podium in `torneoPodios/` sees the memorial, also in the test store without `&lab`. A code with no podium, or no network, still shows the error. The card moves to La Bodega under ✨ Mejoras lanzadas (D-479).
**Why:** validated in the lab; the owner asked to publish it.
**Consequences:** `tools/e2e/cup/archived.mjs` no longer checks the lab-less error, only the memorial and the code with no podium.
