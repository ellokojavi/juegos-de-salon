## D-435 · The home page draws its last look at once; Uno al día's place refreshes on the card
**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-414 (the home card's place, until now cached by /today/ alone) and D-409 (lab first); completes D-362 (its second row of filters, now reserved before the module draws it) · **Topics:** home, random-and-daily
**Context:** the owner noticed La Copa's and Uno al día's cards taking a moment to show on the home
page. Nothing in them waits for the network: the whole list waits for the page's JavaScript (about 16
modules, 250 KB). On a throttled slow 4G (150 ms per request, 1.6 Mb/s) the list showed at 2.9 s
while the page's first paint was at 1.4 s. Preloading One a Day's late modules (PR #731) put its card
next to La Copa's, but the list itself is bound by how much JavaScript there is to fetch. The card's
monthly place, for its part, was only the one /today/ cached the last time it was opened.
**Decision:** in the lab first (D-409), card `#seccion-portada-al-tiro`; **for everyone since
2026-10-23**, approved by the owner, the key checks gone and the card in La Bodega, 🚀 Lanzado:
- **A copy of the list on the phone.** Once drawn, the home page saves the list's HTML
  (`juegos-de-salon:portada-foto`) with a key of version, language and local day. A classic script
  right under `#games` puts it back on the next visit, before any module arrives, so the list shows in
  the page's first paint; the cards are links, so a game opens even before the modules. The module
  then draws the real cards in its place in the same task (they come in without their entrance
  animation, `.games.foto`), keeping the copy's Uno al día card until the real one arrives. The copy is
  never used with a filter in the link (`?type=`), never saved from a filtered or searched list, saved
  again when a ⭐ changes.
- **The place, fresh.** Once today is played and someone is signed in, after the page is drawn,
  `assets/js/uad-place.js` reads the month's table, writes the same `uad-pts-cache` /today/ writes,
  and swaps the chip if the place or the points changed. No spinner: until it answers, the card shows
  the last place cached, as before.
- **The reserved space above the list matches what the module draws**, for everyone: the filters are
  two rows since D-362 (104 px, 98 px from 557 px wide, not 50), and the 🏅 rankings button is in
  the HTML instead of appearing later and wrapping the top bar at 320 px.
**Why:** a copy kept by the phone is right for this player (their language, their ⭐, their Uno al día
state and place), never stale by more than a day or a version, and needs no build step. Cards written
into the HTML at publish time would also help first visits, but only in Spanish, with the date-bound
parts (✨ Nuevo, the order, Uno al día) able to go stale between publishes, and a second source of the
cards to keep in step with `games.js`. Measured on the same slow 4G: a returning visit shows the list
at 1.4 s, with the first paint, instead of 2.9 s; the layout shift of a first visit at 320 px went from
0.127 to 0.02.
**Consequences:** a first visit still waits for the modules. The title's font (Bangers) still arrives
after the first paint, and at 320 px the fallback font's title takes two lines, so the list moves up
about 40 px once it loads. The module must clear `#games` before drawing (it does), and anything new
drawn into the list must look the same in the copy as when drawn, which `tools/e2e/home/instant.mjs`
checks (the copy's HTML equals the real list's).
