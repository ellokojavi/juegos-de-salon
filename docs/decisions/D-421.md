## D-421 · Agentes leaves the lab in Conexiones' place; Conexiones goes to La Bodega
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-286 and D-287 (Agentes in the lab) and D-332 (how a lab game graduates); amends D-288 (no board of the day), D-163 (the pool), D-230 and D-334 (Uno al día's deck) and D-280 (La Bodega) · **Topics:** agents-game, cup, random-and-daily, home
**Context:** Agentes, the one-player Codenames, was tried in the lab since v0.157.0, in Spanish only. The owner asked to swap Conexiones for Agentes: Agentes out of the lab and marked new, Conexiones off to La Bodega.
**Decision:** Owner's request, with its scope answered by the owner: everywhere, La Copa included, and the clues in the four languages.
- **Agentes is a La Copa game**, built like the other one-player games (D-198, D-331): its engine, its four decks of clues, its screen, its styles and its texts live in `public/cup/games/agents/`, and the same screen serves the cup day, its trial, Uno al día and the standalone page `/agents/`. Id `agentes`, as in the lab's signal. It is on the home page with the ✨ Nuevo label from 2026-10-22 (`nuevoDesde`), and `/labs/agents/` is a bridge to `/agents/` (`deLabs`).
- **Each language has its own deck** (D-170): `clues.js` (Spanish from Chile, the lab's 118), `clues-en.js`, `clues-pt.js` and `clues-de.js`, each about 118 clues adapted, not translated. In a cup the words are the cup's language (`CON_PALABRAS`), so everybody plays the same board; standalone, the player's.
- **A random board every time standalone**, as in D-332: the lab's board of the day and its clue calendar (D-288) go away; Uno al día is the daily challenge. In a cup, the code and the day decide it; the trial is two clues (3 and 2 agents), one neutral and the assassin; the Gran Final plays it whole on its own day (D-418).
- **In La Copa it takes Conexiones' place**: in the pool (`POZO`) and in the fixed calendars of the demos (`CALENDARIOS`). Conexiones stays registered in `JUEGOS`, so the cups created before keep playing it, the final included.
- **In Uno al día**, from 2026-10-24 (`DESDE_AGENTES`): Conexiones leaves (`hasta`), also from the deck in play, and Agentes joins the next deck shuffled. The days before keep their game.
- **Conexiones goes to La Bodega**: off the home page and the search (`bodega: true` in `SUELTOS`); its page `/connections/` still opens (old links, rankings), and the lab lists it folded in 📦 La Bodega.
- The lab's comments form and its "back to the lab" went away: the 🐞 report of every game takes their place.
**Why:** the owner liked Agentes and wanted it in front of everybody; two association word games side by side were one too many.
**Alternatives discarded:** keeping Conexiones on the home page next to Agentes (the owner asked to remove it); Agentes only on the home page, outside La Copa and Uno al día; English, Portuguese and German screens over the Spanish board (the owner chose the clues in the four languages).
**Consequences:** `public/cup/games/agents/engine.test.mjs` checks the four decks (one word per card, at most 15 letters, 9 or 10 words per clue and 3 temptations) and 300 boards in each; `uno-al-dia.test.mjs` checks no day before 2026-10-24 changes; `tools/e2e/agents/game.mjs` plays it end to end at `/agents/`.
