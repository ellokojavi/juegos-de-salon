## D-377 · La Copa's rules and engine stay out of the pages that are not La Copa's
**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-329 (a PR runs the scripts its changes reach); extended by D-378 (every game's files, and its guard moved to `tools/e2e/scope.test.mjs`) · **Topics:** tests
**Context:** a PR that only changed La Copa's table (#552) ran 67 of the 74 end-to-end scripts.
`cup/rules.js` reached 22 pages and `cup/engine.js` 20, though only the Cup and its own games show
them: the home page, Ahorcado, Batalla Naval, Dudo and Generala loaded `cup/rules.js` through Uno al
día (the dice's names, and the notification sheets' texts), Toque y Fama and Línea de tiempo loaded
`cup/engine.js` for `codigoAlAzar`, and Generala loaded it through `cup/games/solo.js` for the clock.
And every script that passes through the home page ran with them.
**Decision:** those pieces live in small modules of their own, and the Cup files re-export them, so
nothing inside La Copa changes: `cup/code.js` (the cup code and `codigoAlAzar`), `cup/clock.js` (`reloj`,
`mmss`, `pausaDeLectura`) and `assets/js/notification-sheets.js` (the 14 texts of the sheets that help
turn notifications on, which `cup/rules.js` merges into its languages). The Uno al día dice takes its
names from `games.js`, and the two games only La Copa has (Línea Relámpago and Toque y Fama: adivina el
número) carry theirs in `JUEGOS_DIA`.
**Why:** the owner's request, after asking whether #552's tests were excessive: a change to La Copa
should test La Copa. A change to `cup/rules.js` went from 58 scripts to 35, and one to `cup/engine.js`
from 49 to 35; what is left are La Copa's own pages (the Cup, its games, Uno al día, records, the panel).
**Consequences:** `public/assets/js/uno-al-dia.test.mjs` checks that no page loads `cup/rules.js` or
`cup/engine.js` except La Copa's own (its screens, which load `cup/game.js`, and Uno al día, the records
and the panel, which show its data), and that the two names in `JUEGOS_DIA` say what La Copa says. A
new import of La Copa from any other page, a new game's included, fails that test. Since D-378 that
page check lives in `tools/e2e/scope.test.mjs`, for every game's files, not only La Copa's.
