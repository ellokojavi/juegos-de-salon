## D-463 · A privacy page in four languages, and an unanswered feedback email is deleted after 90 days
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-317 (when the optional email is deleted) · **Topics:** home, agents
**Context:** The 2026-10-27 audit (#834, A5; issue #842): the site keeps player names, a country taken
from the time zone, device class, push subscriptions, reply emails and many `localStorage` keys, but
had no privacy page anywhere. Minors play, and Chile's Law 21.719 and the GDPR (for EN, PT and DE
players) expect purposes, retention, rights and a contact. The feedback form promised "then we delete
it", yet an email whose report was never resolved stayed in `feedbackContacto/` forever.
**Decision:**
- **`/privacy/`** (`public/privacy/`, texts in `texts.js`, es/en/pt/de, Spanish by default): what is
  kept on the phone and in Firebase, why, for how long, who else sees it (GitHub Pages and Firebase,
  and YouTube, Stripe, Cloudflare and Gmail only when used), a plain word for children and
  parents, and how to ask to see, correct or delete data (hola@juegosdesalon.cl, never asking for the
  PIN). The controller is "Juegos de Salón (juegosdesalon.cl)", never a person. It names Law 21.719 and
  the GDPR as the frame it was written in and claims no certification. Where something has no deletion
  date yet (usage statistics, feedback text, Cups until their creator deletes them, LIG-31), it says so.
- **Linked** from the home page footer (beside "código en GitHub", `COMMON.privacy`) and from the note
  under the email field of every feedback form (`campoCorreo` in `buzon-form.js`, a new tab so the
  message is not lost).
- **Retention of the reply email: 90 days.** `tools/agents/feedback.mjs expire` deletes every
  `feedbackContacto/<id>` whose `at` is older than `RETENTION_DAYS` (90) and comments on its issue
  (with the `FORGOTTEN` marker, so `pending` stops listing it). `sync` runs it after every pass, so the
  Mac mini's `feedback-watch` job (every 5 minutes) carries it out; no new scheduler. The form's note
  now says it: "Lo borramos al responderte o a los 90 días" (`DIAS_CORREO`, which
  `feedback.test.mjs` keeps equal to `RETENTION_DAYS`).
- **Published for everyone, not behind the lab** (an exception to D-395/D-409): a privacy page only a
  lab key holder can see protects nobody, and the owner's issue asked for it in the footer.
**Why:** people should be able to read, in their language, what the games keep about them and how to
have it deleted; and a promise to delete an email has to hold even when nobody answers.
**Consequences:** the page is a description of the code, so a PR that stores something new, or keeps
it for a different time, edits `public/privacy/texts.js` in the same PR (C-17). `texts.test.mjs`
checks the four languages have the same shape, name the contact and the 90 days. The page says
feedback text is copied to the public GitHub issues with the name masked, which was already true
(D-279, D-366). Google Fonts is not named: the fonts are served from the site since #851.
