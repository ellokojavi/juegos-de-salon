## D-414 · Uno al día points reach everyone, on /today/, and October is backfilled
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** carries out D-412 (out of the lab, "Yes, and publish" in D-409's terms) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, rankings
**Context:** The owner validated the real screens of Uno al día points in the lab (`/labs/daily-score/live/`, D-412) and asked to publish them: in the four languages, with every player's October recalculated so the ranking shows the season as if it had run all month, and everyone back to 0 in November.
**Decision:**
- **The page is /today/.** It already was Uno al día's page, the URL the notices, the share links, the invitations (`today/?inv=`) and the result's "📅 Volver a Uno al día" open, so a new URL would only add a bridge. /today/ is now the lab's hub on top of what it had: the season box (the month's points, the place and who is just ahead, today's points, streak, best streak, wildcards and today's bonus, "¿Cómo se ganan los puntos?"), Jugar (the die) or today's result with Compartir and Jugar otro, Entrar, the ranking, the calendar with each day's points and its details under the grid, Por juego, inviting, the notices and a footer (home, rankings, 💬 last). The lab's page goes; its prototype with notes stays at `/labs/daily-score/`, and its card moves to La Bodega, 🚀 Lanzado.
- **The home page's 📅 card opens /today/** always (before, it rolled the die until today was played); the die rolls from its Jugar. The notices, the share links and the invitations already opened /today/.
- **One ranking:** Uno al día's block has the same tabs on /today/ and under the result: Mes (the points), Hoy, Rachas and Amigos (the month's points among friends); /records/ shows the points table to everyone. The week's tab gives way to the month's.
- **No lab gate left:** the points are saved for every player with a name (`uno-al-dia-red.js`: today's when it is played, and on opening /today/ the month's days not yet counted, after one history read).
- **Four languages:** every text of the page in `COMMON[lang].uad.pts` (C-3), and the page's screens in `tools/e2e/paths.mjs` (`today`), checked by `tools/e2e/daily-languages.mjs`.
- **October's backfill:** `tools/firebase/uad-points-backfill.mjs` (dry run by default, `--apply` writes) walks each player's history in Firebase as the phone does (`daily-points.js`) and adds each October day not yet counted, in order, the way `anotarPuntosDia` does, marking its `p`. It runs once, by hand, after this merge publishes the code and the rules; running it twice writes nothing new. November is another period (`s2026-11`): everyone starts at 0 on their own.
**Why:** The owner's request, after the lab. One URL keeps every link that already exists working; the backfill makes the first season fair to whoever played before the launch.
**Consequences:** What the server does not know does not count: days played on a phone without a name were never uploaded, and the 🧊 won by inviting does not enter the walk (neither on the phone nor in the backfill), so a missed day with no 🧊 earned by the streak breaks it and its bonus starts over. The +10 for inviting stays out until it is saved (D-412). `daily-points.js` (moved from the lab's `score.js`) is shared by /today/, the lab prototype and the backfill.
**Later (2026-10-22):** in the lab (D-409), the +10 per invited friend is saved (`anotarInvitado`, `p` on the acceptance, the rules check exactly +10 once; no October acceptances existed, so no backfill), and the home 📅 card gets two destinations: the card opens /today/, its chip «▶ Jugar hoy» plays today's game, «✅ Tus puntos» once played. Card `#seccion-uad-invitar-jugar` in /labs/.
