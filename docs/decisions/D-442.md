## D-442 · Agentes: no clue is pink, red only means a mistake
**Date:** 2026-10-24 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-287 (the color per clue) · **Topics:** agents-game
**Context:** a player wrote that the pink or red cards looked like errors when they were not: the
third clue's color was pink (255, 111, 168), close to the assassin's red, and the notice «¡Bien! Es de
los tuyos.» took it too, so a right answer read like a wrong one.
**Decision:** the clue colors are **green, cyan, blue (77, 166, 255) and violet**. Red and pink are
kept for what went wrong (the assassin, a cut clue, the `.mal` notice); yellow stays the marks'.
`public/cup/games/agents/palette.test.mjs` fails if any clue color's hue falls in the reds and
pinks (from magenta to orange) or in the marks' yellow (owner's request, after player feedback).
**Why:** a color that means "mistake" everywhere else cannot also mean "right". The site's brand
pink stays on buttons and titles, where it is not a verdict on a move.
**Consequences:** dimmed blue and violet cards (closed clues) are told apart mostly by the border;
they no longer count for the current clue, so that is enough. The README's Cup screenshot of
Agentes was taken again.
