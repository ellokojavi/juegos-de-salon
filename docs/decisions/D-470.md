## D-470 · Rankings are read over REST: a solo page opens no Firebase websocket
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** rankings, rooms
**Context:** The owner stays on Firebase's free Spark plan, which caps simultaneous connections at about 100 and goes down past it (firebase/README.md). Every game page that showed a ranking, /today/, /records/ and the home page's Uno al día chip loaded `jugador-firebase.js`, which brought the whole SDK (about 113 kB), an anonymous sign-in and a websocket that stayed open for the life of the tab, only to read a table (#852, from the #834 audit).
**Decision:** `jugador-firebase.js` reads over the RTDB REST API with `fetch`, as `stats.js` and `daily-recap.js` already did: `get`, `top` (`orderBy="k"&limitToFirst`, on the existing `.indexOn`), `vecinos` (`endAt`/`startAt` with a margin for ties on `k`, which are left out as the SDK's `endBefore`/`startAfter` did) and `podios`. The SDK loads only when the player writes (`update`) or signs in (`listo`), in two halves: auth alone (HTTP, no websocket) for the sign-in and for the two paths the rules let only a seated phone read (`invitados/`, `jugadorCopas/`), read over REST with `?auth=<ID token>`; the database half, and its websocket, only for the first write. Online rooms and La Copa keep their own SDK connection.
**Why:** A connection is the scarce resource, not reads. Measured with Chrome against production (signed-in player, `?records=firebase`): Hangman, Bulls and Cows and /records/ went from 1 websocket and 113 kB of Firebase to 0 websockets and 2–10 kB; /today/ from 1 websocket to none (69 kB: the auth half for the seated read of invitations).
**Consequences:** A page now counts toward the 100 only after the player saves a score or signs in. Writes still go through the SDK, with the PIN attempt flow of D-464 unchanged. `jugador-firebase.test.mjs` checks the URLs, the order, the ties and that no read loads the SDK.
