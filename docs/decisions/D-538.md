## D-538 · Offline mode: switches on by itself, plays the local games, uploads scores later
**Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-221 (the service worker will cache, with network-first pages); closes RP-13 when published · **Topics:** pwa
**Context:** RP-13 ("work without a connection") has been pending since the start, and D-221 kept the service worker away from the network so no phone could stay on an old version. The owner wants the app to keep working with no signal or a poor one, without adding a toggle to an already crowded home page, and with players understanding which games are out and what happens to their scores.
**Decision:** an offline mode, built in eight PRs behind the flag `offline` (plan: [OFFLINE.md](../OFFLINE.md)):
- It **switches on by itself** when the phone is offline or the connection is too poor to use, and off only after the connection is steady again. No switch for players; testers get "Forzar modo sin conexión" on its `/labs/` card.
- **Every game is offline-capable in its local modes** unless its manifest entry says `offline: false` (La Copa, which needs the server for its days, deadlines and PIN). `offlineOk(g)` in `games.js` is the one answer. Two-phone rooms are never offline.
- **Online-only games and modes stay on screen, dimmed, tagged "📡 Con señal"** (Needs signal / Com sinal), with one line on tap. They are not hidden.
- **Scores go to the existing pending queue** (`guardarPendiente`, `subirPendientes`) and upload when the signal is back, into the week they were played. **Uno al día played offline counts for its day** if it uploads within 48 h. La Copa is never queued.
- The player is told three times: a one-time sheet on entering the mode, "saved on your phone" on the end screen, and a toast when the scores upload.
- **The service worker caches**, amending D-221: network-first for pages (an online player always gets the latest version), versioned caches for assets, an update toast. **Music is never precached**: offline, a game is silent until a song has played once online.
- **An offline-capable page imports nothing from another site when it opens** (the Firebase SDK stays behind `import()`), checked by `offline.test.mjs`.
**Why:** the owner asked for it (RP-13, 2026-11-07) and decided the four open points: Uno al día counts, a tester-only switch, dimmed with a label, no music precache. Most of it already existed: the local modes, the lazy Firebase imports and the pending-score queue.
**Consequences:** a new game is offline-capable by default and must keep Firebase behind `import()`; a game that needs the server says `offline: false` in its entry. PR 1 (this one) brings the decision, the plan, `offlineOk` and the import check; nothing changes for players until the flag is on.
