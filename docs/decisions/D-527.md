## D-527 · A launch can be scheduled: the `launch` workflow flips flags and splash at the hour
**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-498 and D-517 (a flag switched on its /labs/ card publishes) and D-454 (the panel switches splash campaigns) · **Topics:** structure, home, marketing
**Context:** The owner asked that Shinobi, Babel and their splash `shinobi-babel` go live at 00:00 Pacific on Monday 2026-11-02, without being awake to tap the switches. Flags and campaigns live in Firebase and only the owner's phone or an admin key write them.
**Decision:** `tools/firebase/launch.mjs` writes what the switches write (`flags/<id>` = { state: 'all', at }, `splash/<id>` patched { on: true, at }) and refuses to write before `--not-before`. The `launch` workflow runs it with the admin secret (D-218) on `workflow_dispatch`; a scheduled task dispatches it at the hour (GitHub's own cron can run late). The switches stay where they were: the result is the same as tapping them, and they turn it back off.
**Why:** a launch at midnight is a date, not a person; the same write, timed, and harmless if it runs early or twice.
**Consequences:** The first use: flags `stick` and `stack` and the splash `shinobi-babel`, not before 2026-11-02T08:00:00Z.
