## D-507 · "New" lasts one week after launch
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-333 (two weeks) · **Topics:** home
**Decision:** owner's request: a game carries the "New" label for **one week** from its `nuevoDesde`
(the day it reached the home page), `DIAS_NUEVO = 7` in `public/assets/js/games.js`. Then it goes
off by itself and the game returns to its place by popularity (D-335). No game is marked or unmarked by hand.
**Why:** with two weeks, five games said "New" at once and the label stopped meaning anything; a week
leaves only the latest launch.
**Consequences:** on the day of the change only Tren en Vilo (2026-10-26) stays new; Agentes'
date moved one day back so its label ends today, as the owner asked.
