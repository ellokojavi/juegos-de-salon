## D-464 · PIN attempts are rate-limited, and changing a PIN needs the current one
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-96 and D-212 (how a seat is taken and who changes a key) · **Topics:** cup, rankings
**Context:** D-96 and D-212 take a seat (`torneoSeats`, `jugadorSeats`) when the write carries the
same hash as the key, with nothing between one try and the next, and let any seated phone rewrite
the key (`jugadorKeys/<jid>`, and every cup player's `torneoKeys` from the admin's seat). Both
decisions accepted that a 4-digit PIN "separates friends"; the stakes have grown since (season
points, podiums, notifications).
**Decision:**
- **One PIN attempt every 5 seconds per player.** Before a seat, the phone writes its attempt:
  `jugadorIntentos/<jid>` or `torneoIntentos/<code>/<pid>` = `{ h, u, at }` (the hash, its own uid,
  the server time). The rules accept the write only on a player with a key and if the previous
  attempt on that player is more than 5 s old; nobody reads or deletes it (the cup admin deletes a
  cup's attempts with the cup).
- **A seat needs the attempt.** A seat with the PIN is accepted only if the attempt on that player
  was written by the same uid within the last minute and holds the current key. Creating a player
  or signing up to a cup (the key is born in the same write) and claiming a legacy player (no key
  yet) need no attempt, as before; sitting as a linked player in a cup (D-359) does not change.
- **Changing a PIN needs the current one.** A player's key is rewritten only from a seated phone
  whose fresh attempt holds the current key: the PIN change in the player card asks for the
  current PIN and then the new one. La Copa's "New PIN" for a player stays with the admin, who now
  confirms with their own PIN: a fresh attempt on the admin's cup player, or on the admin's linked
  player (D-359), whose PIN is the one they know.
- **The phone waits on its own.** If its attempt is refused because the last one is too recent,
  the app waits the 5 s once and tries again, so a quick retry after a typo just takes a moment; if
  it is refused again, it says to wait a few seconds (`errWait`, `errEspera`). A wrong current PIN
  or admin PIN says so (`errCurrentPin`, `errAdminPin`).
- The test stores mirror all of it (`jugador-local.js`, `public/cup/store-local.js`), and
  `firebase/pin-attempts.test.mjs` checks the published rules themselves through a small rules
  simulator (`tools/firebase/rules-sim.mjs`).
**Why:** the rules cannot count, but they can compare `now` with a timestamp written earlier: one
attempt per player every 5 s makes trying many PINs slow without costing a legitimate sign-in more
than one extra write. The limit is per player, whichever phone tries. Asking for the current PIN before changing it keeps a phone that was
only left signed in from changing a PIN.
**Consequences:** signing in is two writes instead of one, and a retry within 5 s waits. The rules
are published by `publish.yml` on merge, and the client and the rules must go out together: an old
page that sits without an attempt is refused until it reloads. Forgetting the player PIN still has
no fix (D-212): changing it now needs the current one.
