## D-473 · Bounded Firebase reads: the season recap reads its top, the panel queries by date
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** amended by D-475 · **Relation:** amends D-424, D-80 · **Topics:** panel, random-and-daily
**Context:** The project stays on Firebase's free Spark plan (10 GB/month of download), and the 2026-10-27 audit (#834, #848) found reads that grow with players × days: the season recap (D-424) downloaded the whole `uadTemporada/s<month>` table on every early-month home visit, and the panel downloaded all of `torneos/` and `feedback/`, and redrew on every move of every live room. The owner decided that the nightly jobs are the single authority for totals (#853, #854).
**Decision:** (1) The nightly points rebuild (`uad-points-backfill.mjs --rebuild`) also writes `uadTemporada/s<month>/top` (`seasonTop` in `daily-recap.js`: `{ c, t, p: players, j: the rows down to the 10th place, ties included }`); the recap reads only `top` and the player's own row `j/<jid>`, over REST as before. A month with no `top` yet is tried on the next visit. The rules do not change: the season is public to read and only the admin writes it. (2) The panel queries `torneos/` by `meta/end` and `feedback/` by `at`, from two days before the first day it loads (the period and the one before it), with `.indexOn` on `torneos` (`meta/end`, `meta/alias`) and `feedback` (`at`). A cup that ended earlier is read on its own when opened by its code or its own link. Room updates redraw the panel at most once a second.
**Why:** Every panel number of a period only looks at cups that ended on or after its first day (results, starts, sign-ups and the end all happen before `meta/end`), so the query changes no number: compared against production for every range, all identical, while the default week downloads 45.7 of 76.5 KB of cups and 6.8 of 11.2 KB of feedback, and the gap grows as cups accumulate. The recap's read stops growing with the players.
**Consequences:** The live rooms are still read with their messages: Firebase cannot leave a child out of a query, and the panel's Moves, Chat and Last move counts come from the messages, so reading rooms without them needs the rooms to keep their own counters (a later step). `unoAlDia/` and `invitados/` are keyed by player, not by date, so they cannot be queried by date until Uno al día is indexed or sharded by month (#848 step 4). Archiving finished Cups and old daily tables is #848 step 3.
