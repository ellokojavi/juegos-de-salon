## D-490 · Dudo's classic rules, round two: the owner's answers to #897
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** amended by D-491, D-495 · **Relation:** amends D-487 and D-71 (only under the classic rules) · **Topics:** modes
**Context:** D-487 put two Chilean rules in the lab and left eight questions in #897. The owner answered all of them, and added that at a table of six on one phone nobody ever saw the Calzar button. Source for the rules: [Dudo (juego), Wikipedia in Spanish](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dudo_(juego)).
**Decision:** Under **🧪 Classic rules** (`config.traditional`, lab only, card `#seccion-dudo-tradicional`):
1. **Into aces is half plus one** when the quantity is even (6 fives → 4 aces), half rounded up when odd (5 fives → 3): `Math.floor(n / 2) + 1`. Leaving aces stays double plus one.
2. **Calzar** is also allowed **in a duel** (this amends D-71's "no calzar with two" for the classic rules only), and **a player with one die may calzar at any time**. The first time the button opens to a player in a game, a line under it says what calzar is (lab too, D-409).
3. **A correct calza at five dice keeps five**: no reserve die.
4. **The obligada round starts by itself** and is **announced to the whole table** with a full-screen notice (☝️ Ronda obligada · «X quedó con un dado»), once per phone and round.
5. **It is open**: everyone sees every other player's die and not their own (shown as ❓). In a room the dice of that round travel in the clear along with their hash; each screen hides its own (honour, as at the table). On one phone, the pass screen and the notice clear the table under them, and the notice asks whoever does not hold the phone not to look at it.
6. In it, **a player who also has one die may change the face**, always for a higher quantity (Wikipedia: "puede cambiar la pinta por una cantidad mayor").
7. **Variants**, in one folded **Variantes** row that shows only with the classic rules on, each off: **Pasar** (`config.pasar`: with all five dice all different, all equal or a full house, a player may pass once per round; the next player may doubt the pass, and only that hand is judged) and **Dudar a la siciliana** (`config.siciliana`: doubting the game's first bid is worth two dice). Not built: **picado** (two aces reverse the direction; what "doubting the picado" judges is not clear in the source) and **juego sucio** (opening in aces as the lowest face contradicts point 1 of D-487); both are listed in #897.
8. **Obligada also applies with two players left**, announced the same way.
The bot follows all of it: in the open round it counts the dice it sees and treats its own as the unknown ones, it passes with a qualifying hand and doubts a pass now and then.
**Why:** these are the owner's answers. Why nobody saw Calzar at a table of five: it was **on** (the default) and drawn correctly in every mode; the button shows only on your turn once the bid reaches half the dice, and with 25 dice that is a bid of 13. Simulated with the bot, a five-player game first allows calzar around round 11 of ~23, and only in 16% of rounds, with nothing announcing it. Not a code bug, so nothing changes for players without a lab key; the hint is the fix, tried in the lab first.
**Consequences:** `minBid(…, { halfPlusOne, changeFace })`, `calzarOk(bid, total, players, { classic, oneDie })`, `pasaOk(dice)`, a `paso` play and a `dudoPaso` ending, `last.lost` (2 with the Sicilian doubt); `buildState` returns `rules` for the player on turn, `canPass`, `pass` and `passStanding`. `botMove` takes `seen`, `canPass` and `passStanding`. Without the switch nothing changes, and saved games resume the same.
