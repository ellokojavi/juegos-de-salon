## D-420 · Cuarto Rey leaves the home page for La Bodega
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-280 and D-372 (La Bodega also keeps a game taken off the home page) · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** Cuarto Rey (id `cuarto-rey`) was the last game on the home page by games finished
(`POPULARIDAD`), and the owner asked to take it off the site and keep it in the lab's La Bodega.
**Decision:** Cuarto Rey is no longer offered to players. It keeps its entry in `GAMES` with
`labs: true` and `available: false`, like El caso in `SUELTOS`, and its page moves from
`/fourth-king/` to `/labs/fourth-king/`, behind the lab's door (`labs-puerta.js`, D-281) and with
`noindex`. Its card goes to 📦 La Bodega, folded to its title like the others (`#seccion-cuarto-rey`),
and La Bodega's intro now also names the projects that left the home page.
- **Off everything players see:** the home page (`ordenPortada` leaves out every `labs` entry, of
  `GAMES` too), 🎲 Juego al azar, the 🔎 search (its `KEYWORDS` are gone), `POPULARIDAD`, and the
  game lists of the home page's descriptions and social cards.
- **The old links:** `/fourth-king/` and `/cuarto-rey/` stay as bridge pages to `/labs/fourth-king/`
  (`og.mjs` generates them from the registry: a `GAMES` entry whose path is under `labs/` gets a bridge
  from its old folder). Without a lab key the door sends the visitor to the menu.
- **What does not change:** the id, the saved games in `localStorage` and the panel's history,
  which still names it (`gameLabel`). Its `slug` (`fourth-king`) keeps its URL name, its spec
  (`docs/games/fourth-king.md`) and its screenshots folder. The README keeps it in the games table
  as 🧪 lab, with its section.
**Why:** the owner's request. Keeping it in La Bodega instead of deleting it keeps the work and lets
a key holder still play it; bringing it back is undoing the entry and moving the folder back.
**Consequences:** `tools/e2e/fourth-king/` plays it at `/labs/fourth-king/`; `tools/e2e/fourth-king/lab.mjs`
checks it is in La Bodega, folded, and that both old routes lead there.
