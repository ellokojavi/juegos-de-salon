## D-548 · A game can be "New" and 🔥 Popular at once
**Date:** 2026-11-09 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-361 · **Topics:** home
**Context:** The 🔥 Popular label left out the games still carrying "New", so Shinobi, second in the weekly count in its first week, showed only "New" and the Popular slot went to the fourth game.
**Decision:** `populares` takes the first three of `POPULARIDAD` (the weekly order the Monday workflow writes), La Copa left out, new games included; a game in both wears both labels and appears in both filters. With an overlap there are fewer than six labelled games (five or four), and no game is pulled in to make up three and three.
**Why:** The owner's request: a new game that people already play most is news worth showing.
**Consequences:** It ranks by `POPULARIDAD`, not by the home page's order, where new games sit on top whatever their plays. The weekly workflow is unchanged: it only rewrites `POPULARIDAD`, and the label reads it.
