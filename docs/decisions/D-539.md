## D-539 · An online game's room goes through room-state.js
**Date:** 2026-11-07 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows #860 (room-flow.js, how a room is opened) · **Topics:** rooms
**Context:** each online game handled `hello`, the host's `start` and `rematch` by hand, and the copies drifted: in Generala and Liar's Dice a second rematch offer replaced the first, so two phones could end up in different rooms, and Hangman could throw on a reveal that arrived before the start. PR #1259 moved Hangman, Timeline, Generala and Liar's Dice onto one shared machine, `public/assets/js/room-state.js`. The owner then asked to make it the rule.
**Decision:** a game with online rooms applies its messages through `roomMessage(M, msg, { minPlayers, onStart })` first, and handles only its own moves after it. The room's rules live there: messages already seen are dropped, `hello` names a seat in any phase, only the host's first `start` with enough named players moves the room from lobby to playing, and a seat's first rematch offer wins. A game with something hidden (Battleship's fleet, Bulls and Cows' secret) works out its phase with `phaseOf()` in `hidden-match.js`, and accepts the commit and the reveal in any phase, because messages are ordered by each phone's clock and dropping an early one would hang the game.
**Why:** one tested machine instead of hand-written copies that drift; the two bugs above came from the copies.
**Consequences:** C-7 in docs/CANONS.md lists it beside `room-flow.js`, and the checklist asks for it. Every game with online rooms uses it: Hangman, Timeline, Generala and Liar's Dice since #1259; Battleship, Bulls and Cows (two seats, no host start, so no `minPlayers`) and the lab's Julep (its hello carries the seat's key, through `onHello`) since the next PR.
