## D-509 · Lab flags are switched only on their /labs/ card
**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-498 (the panel's 🚩 Lab flags tab is gone) · **Topics:** structure, panel
**Context:** the owner found the panel's 📣 Campaigns → 🚩 Lab flags and /labs/ confusing: two places switching the same `flags/<id>`. The owner chose (2026-10-29) to gather everything in /labs/.
**Decision:** /labs/ is the one place to see and switch what is being tried. Each flagged card shows its state (🧪 lab, 🟢 everyone, ⚪ off) and a button to each other state; 🚀 still asks first. The panel drops the 🚩 tab (📣 Campaigns keeps splash, features and skins) and its 🧪 Labs menu link shows how many flags are live for everyone (`n/m 🚀`).
**Why:** one place per thing (D-213): the lab already lists every feature with its description, so the switch belongs on its card.
**Consequences:** `#/campaigns/flags` opens the splash tab; `tools/e2e/labs/publish.mjs` checks the three states on a card.
