## D-623 · A skin in the lab shows only in its preview, never by its dates
**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-457 · **Topics:** home, identity
**Context:** Under D-457 a template with `public: false` turned on by itself, on its days, for every lab key holder, as a lab feature does (D-395). Preparing Thanksgiving, the owner set the rule apart: a future skin campaign is not a new feature; lab users should not get it unless they go to the panel and preview it.
**Decision:** A template in the lab never turns on by its dates, for anyone. It shows only through its preview, `?season=<id>`, which the panel's 📣 Campaigns → 🎨 Skins → «Live Preview» opens, and the lab card's button too; the preview still needs a lab key while the template is not public. A public template turns on by its days for everyone, as before.
**Why:** A skin dresses the whole site (D-622): turning it on by itself for testers mixes a campaign still being decided into every page they use, which is not what a lab key is for. The owner wants to look at a campaign when they choose to.
**Consequences:** `chooseSeason` in `season.js` returns nothing for a lab template unless `?season=<id>` asks for it; `season.test.mjs` checks both. Lab feature flags (D-498) are unchanged.
