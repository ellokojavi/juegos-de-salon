## D-398 · The home page's 🔎 search opens and closes with a motion
**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-390 (the pill that opens into a field) and D-396 (the pill first in its row) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** the owner asked that the 🔎 pill animate when it opens into the search field and when it closes.
**Decision:** opening, the field grows out of the pill (a clip from the pill's 44 px to the whole row, 0.28 s);
closing with ✕, Escape or "Ver todos", it folds back into the pill (0.22 s) and only then do the other
pills come back, with a short fade. Opening again while it closes keeps it open. With reduced motion
there is no animation. All in `public/labs/home/search.js`.
**Why:** the field takes the place of the pills; growing from the 🔎 says where it came from and where it goes.
**Consequences:** `tools/e2e/home/search.mjs` checks that the field grows on opening and folds before the pills return.
