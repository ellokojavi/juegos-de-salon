## D-603 · Duels: one player challenges another, same seeds, three days at most
**Date:** 2026-11-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** rankings, anti-cheat
**Context:** The owner asked for a rivals mode: challenge someone, from any ranking or from the profile, to a set of rounds (best of 3, best of 5, most points; a custom duel later also best of 7 or 9), with each player's record of duels on their profile. Mockups on the real pages were chosen with the owner (U-41): ⚔️ on a ranking's rows opening a centred dialog, the profile's duel cards with ＋ New duel, See all and the nemesis, and a third home card after La Copa and One a Day, peeking from the edge, its badge «1 VS 1» or the open duels' count.
**Decision:** A duel (lab flag `rivals`, `public/assets/js/duels.js`) is `duelos/<did>` `{ a, b, an, bn, f, g, sd, at }`: `a` challenges `b` to game `g` (one of the games with their own page, which take a `?seed=`) in format `f` (`bo3`, `bo5`, `pts` over 3 rounds; `bo7`, `bo9` added 2026-11-24, see below), each round with its seed in `sd`, as La Copa's days (D-97). Each player plays whenever they like (the owner: «play whenever you like but duels last at most 3 days»): the game's page opened with `&duel=<did>&round=<i>` marks the round begun (`e/<jid>/<i>`, once) and then writes its result (`r/<jid>/<i>`, once, after it began), and the rules take neither after `at` + 3 days. What decides a duel is pure (`estado`): a round counts once both played it, or when the three days end, a round not played or begun and left being a 0; best of N ends as soon as someone holds more than half. Anyone signed in can be challenged (the owner: «you can challenge anyone for now»); the new-duel sheet suggests the rivals already played first, then the cup friends this phone knows, then a search by the exact name. Each player's list is `jugadorDuelos/<jid>/<did>`, written with the duel.
**Why:** The same seeds make an asynchronous duel fair without a room or anyone online together, and the game pages already take a seed. A duel decided by its clock needs no job to close it. Writing a round's start before it is played stops replaying the same levels until a good score; a phone can still play the seed's levels elsewhere first, the price of no server (as a La Copa day).
**Consequences:** A player could fill someone's list with challenges; acceptable in the lab, to be limited before it leaves it. The test store (`jugador-local.js`) mirrors the rules and `firebase/duels.test.mjs` checks both. The privacy page lists what a duel keeps. A La Copa-like duel with several games (the Mini Cup) is the next step.

**Later (2026-11-23, no new decision):** the Duels page (`/duels/`, still behind `rivals`) also shows the player's own numbers (record, win rate, streaks, rounds, rivals, favourite game), the 🔥 arch rival (the most dueled) next to the 💀 nemesis, every rival head to head, and a ⚔️ Rematch on each rival and each finished duel with the same game and format (`duels-ui.js`, `duels-texts.js`).

**Later (2026-11-24, no new decision):** the new-duel sheet opens straight on the quick duel, with the custom duel as a link under its button. A custom duel first picks how to win, then how many rounds: most rounds won over an odd number, 3 to 9 (`bo3`…`bo9`), or most points over any number, 2 to 9 (`pts2`…`pts9`, `pts` staying 3), one seed per round; the rules, `jugador-local.js` and La Copa's `?round` take round keys up to 8.

**Later (2026-11-24, no new decision):** a game with levels (Sudoku, Croc!, Pintaminas, Loteo) asks for its level right under the game in the new-duel sheet, with none preselected. The duel stores it per round in `lv` (`{ <i>: <variant> }`, only where the game has one; older duels have none, `roundLevel` in `duels.js`), the round link carries it and the game page plays it with no picker, naming the level. The variant list moved to `games.js` (`GAME_VARIANTS`), so the duel pages do not load La Copa's engine; `firebase/duels.test.mjs` checks the rule.

**Later (2026-11-28, no new decision):** every choice a game's page offers is locked by the duel, not only levels (the owner: «each game must be standardized so it selects a single version»). The duel's list is `DUEL_VERSIONS` in `games.js`: `GAME_VARIANTS` plus Toque y Fama's 🔤 `word` / 🔢 `number` (a duel's choice only; La Copa and the home page keep them as two games). A `number` round opens `/word/?…&mode=number`, which plays La Copa's `numero` (`DUEL_VERSION_GAME`) and hides the Número/Palabra switch. Every round's waiting room says it counts for the duel (round, total, rival) and names the version. A game that adds a choice to its page adds it to `DUEL_VERSIONS`.
