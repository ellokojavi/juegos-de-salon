## D-526 · Uno al día scores Babel and Shinobi on a soft curve, not a cap
**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-525 (Uno al día capped them at 100) · **Topics:** random-and-daily
**Context:** With D-525 Babel and Shinobi have no cap, and Uno al día, which compares a 0–100 across games, cut them at 100: a good Babel player passes 100 with about 50 floors and streaks, so the best all tied at 100. The owner asked that Uno al día tell the best apart.
**Decision:**
- Uno al día stores `puntajeDia(points) = round(100 × (1 − e^(−points/80)))` for these two games (`DIA_K` in each engine): 50 → 46, 100 → 71, 200 → 92; it nears 100 without reaching it at once.
- A game that exports `puntajeDia` brings its own curve (`cup/games/index.js`); every other game stays as it was (already 0–100).
- The first attempt of the day (the one that counts) says under the big score what it counts there: «📅 En Uno al día cuenta N de 100.» in es, en and pt.
- The Grand Final keeps the cap at 100 (D-525); La Copa ranks by position (D-95).
**Why:** a cap hides the difference between a 60-floor tower and a 200-floor one; a curve keeps it at every level and needs no data to start (K can be tuned once there are scores).
**Consequences:** No Uno al día day dealt these games before (their first is 2026-11-17), so nothing saved changes.
