## D-503 · Every campaign seen is a key event on the Timeline, with who saw it
**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-408, D-461 · **Topics:** panel
**Context:** The panel counted each splash campaign (D-461) and the donation card (`support/`) only as
numbers, never who. The owner asked for the Timeline to list, as high-value actions, who saw which
campaign, and who opened the donation card (the Keanu one), next to the feedback sent.
**Decision:** Each time a splash campaign's window opens (`shown`, recorded views only, never a
preview) and each time the donation card opens, the phone writes `stats/<env>/days/<day>/seen/<id>`
`{ c, at, name?, co? }` (`noteSeen` in stats.js), `c` the campaign id or `support`, the name the
phone already knows (`nombreDelCelular`), none asked. The Timeline reads it as the 📣 *Campaigns
seen* kind. Skins are always on and tied to no action: they are not campaigns and are not here.
**Why:** Knowing who saw a campaign, next to what they did after, is what tells whether it worked.
**Adjusted 2026-11-10 (changelog `seen-bursts`):** the splash row is written once per phone and
campaign, on its first `reach`, and only once the page is visible; a row from a page that opened hidden
or prerendered carries `vis` (`hidden` / `prerender`), kept while bursts of identical rows are studied.
The donation card still writes one row each time it opens.
**Consequences:** `seen/` holds names, so stats-retention deletes it at 24 months with `rooms/` and
`live/` (D-472), and /privacy/ says it. A new campaign shows up on its own, with no panel change.
