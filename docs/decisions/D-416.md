## D-416 · La Copa: a linked player who lost their session re-enters with the player's PIN
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** completes D-285 (a phone that lost its seat) and D-359 (no cup PIN with a player) · **Topics:** cup, rankings
**Context:** a player in a real cup (Thursday Guys #4, on the day of the final) joined with his
player signed in, so per D-359 he never had a cup PIN. His phone lost its anonymous Firebase session
while keeping the player in `localStorage`. Empezar led to **Ya estoy inscrito** (D-285), but with
his name picked the PIN field was hidden (his cup player is "his", D-359), and "Entrar como Chan"
called `sentarseComoJugador`, which the rules refuse unless `jugadorSeats/<jid>/<uid>` exists for
the current session. That seat was lost with the same session, so he read "Ese PIN no es el de Chan"
without having typed one, and picking another name to make the field appear sat him as someone
else. A dead end: there was no PIN he could type. The test store did not check the player seat, so
the end-to-end scripts never saw it.
**Decision:** when a linked player cannot sit with no cup PIN, the screen asks for the **player's**
PIN, with "Este celular también perdió la sesión de tu jugador {name}". Back from a lost seat
(D-285) as the linked player, it asks right away. The PIN first seats the phone as the player again
(`jugador.reseat(pin)`, which writes `jugadorSeats/<jid>/<uid>` and the rules accept only with the
player's hash) and then sits as the cup player as in D-359. A PIN that is not the player's is tried
as a cup PIN, so a new cup PIN from the admin also works. The field stays visible when switching
names and back. The test store now refuses `sentarseComoJugador` when the phone is not seated as
that player in the test player store, as the rules do.
**Why:** the player's PIN is the one these players know (D-359), and it is the proof the rules
already require. Telling them a PIN was wrong when none was asked for sent them guessing.
**Alternatives discarded:** reading the player seat before showing the screen (`jugadorSeats` cannot
be read, on purpose); signing the player out so the cup PIN field appears (they never saw that PIN,
and they would lose their player on the phone).
**Consequences:** the player seat is restored from La Copa, so the rest of the app (rankings, Uno al
día) works again on that phone too. Lost sessions on other pages that write as the player are not
covered here. Tested by `tools/e2e/cup/player-cups.mjs` and `public/cup/store.test.mjs`.
