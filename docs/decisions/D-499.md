## D-499 · The nightly jobs are the single authority for room sweeping and Cup podiums; phones write only raw events
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-39 (who sweeps), D-450 (who saves a podium), D-414 and D-415 (who writes Uno al día totals, in a second PR); carries out #854 option (a) · **Topics:** rooms, cup, rankings, random-and-daily
**Context:** The 2026-10-27 audit (#834, B3, issue #854) found three decisions patching one pattern: work that needs a trigger was done by whichever phone showed up, and a cron job fixed what was missed. Rooms were swept only by phones, at most 30 days back (D-39, D-321); a Cup's podium existed only once someone reopened it (D-450); Uno al día totals drifted because phones add up their own points and rejected writes are swallowed (D-415). The free plan has no Cloud Functions. The owner stayed on Spark (#853) and chose option (a): phones write only raw events, the nightly jobs are the single authority, the app only reads the views they write.
**Decision:**
- **Rooms:** phones no longer sweep. They still note a room in its bucket when creating it (D-321) and refresh the bucket's `lastAt` on entering one, which is what keeps a bucket closed while a room in it can be alive. The `room-sweep` workflow (09:37 UTC) runs `tools/firebase/room-sweep.mjs --apply` as administrator: every bucket of a past day closed for six hours, however old, is swept (its rooms and the bucket deleted), and the expired rooms no bucket names are deleted and the live ones noted (`unnoted-rooms.mjs --fix`'s job, now nightly). The admin key goes over the rules, so the tool deletes a room only if it is expired by the rules' two clocks (D-89); a code an old bucket names that a live room reuses is kept and noted again. The rules no longer let a phone read or delete a bucket or move `sweptDay`.
- **Cup podiums:** the app no longer writes `torneoPodios/<code>`; the `cup-podiums` workflow (D-450) is its only writer. The rules drop the phone's write; a phone still signs its own place on a podium, once (D-358).
- **Uno al día totals** (the month's points, the +10 per invite, the week's sum and the best streak) move to the nightly rebuild in a second PR, through the lab first, because it changes what a player sees right after playing (the ranking would wait for the night). Until it lands, D-414 and D-415 stand as they are.
**Why:** one writer per derived view: a total, a podium or a sweep that depends on a phone showing up misses exactly the cases nobody revisits, and a phone's failed write is invisible. Blaze (Cloud Functions on each write, option (b)) stays out; if the B1 decision moves to Blaze, these jobs move there.
**Consequences:** a finished Cup's champions reach the medal table within 47 minutes of its close, never sooner (before, at once if someone opened it). Expired rooms live until the next night instead of the next phone's sweep, six hours at most later; nobody sees them, since a room expired accepts nothing. Old app versions still cached try to sweep and to save podiums, and the rules refuse them silently. `room-sweep.test.mjs` covers the plan (old buckets, live reused codes, today's bucket left alone); `records.test.mjs` checks a phone cannot write a podium.
