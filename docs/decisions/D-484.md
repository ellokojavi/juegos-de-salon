## D-484 · Each panel domain counts and lists only its own games, and a script checks every view
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-348 · **Topics:** panel, tests
**Context:** The owner saw solo Tren en Vilo games in 👥 Group's "Offline games" log. That log predates D-348 and was never split by domain. A scan of the panel found the same leak in other places. "Finished on one phone" drew group games *started* as its bar, and its percentage mixed group and solo endings. 🧍 Individual's "finished" tile capped every ending of a game (group ones too) at its solo starts, so a game with group endings showed up to 100% of its solo games finished. Group's "Players per game" read every mode. Nothing checked that a panel view opens clean: `look.mjs panel` only takes pictures.
**Decision:** Each domain counts and lists only its own modes, split by the `individual` flag of `MODES`. `finales()` splits endings into `finGrupo` and `finIndividual`. `summarize()` takes a `modo` filter. The offline log takes a domain: Group lists vs. the phone and one phone, and Individual now has its own log of solo and One a Day games. A game's card still lists all its modes. "Again" (`otra/<game>`) carries no mode, so its tiles say it is not split by domain. The Summary's Individual card says it includes One a Day. `tools/e2e/panel/views.mjs` opens every view in `PATHS.panel` at 900 and 390 px with the seeded data. It fails on an uncaught error, a broken value on screen (`undefined`, `NaN`, `Infinity`, `[object …]`; players' error messages in Health don't count), a page wider than the screen, or an offline log row from another domain (rows carry `data-mode`).
**Why:** A domain whose numbers include another domain's games misleads the owner, and the leak returned with every block added after D-348. A check that runs on every PR touching the panel stops it before the owner sees it.
**Consequences:** A new panel view or card is an entry in `PATHS.panel` (`tools/e2e/paths.mjs`), and with that it gets the same checks. A new list of games in a domain view filters by `isIndividualMode`. Splitting "again" by domain needs the mode written in `otra/` (`stats.js` and the rules); left for later.
