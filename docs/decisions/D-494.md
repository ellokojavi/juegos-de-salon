## D-494 · No blob over 5 MB enters git; big media moves out without rewriting history
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** tests, marketing
**Context:** the audit of 2026-10-27 (#834, #856) found an 887 MB pack after three weeks: 335 MB of
promo-video MP4s re-committed, 268 MB of README screenshots, a Chrome profile with a 37 MB
`model.tflite` (c77d0836) and rewrites of the old single decisions file. Every clone and worktree pays it.
**Decision:** `tools/agents/repo-weight.test.mjs` runs in the `tests` check and fails a PR that tracks a
file over 5 MB or a Chrome profile file, or whose own commits added one even if a later commit deleted
it. The three promo MP4s already over the limit are grandfathered in the test until they move out; the
list may only shrink. How the existing media leaves the repo is the plan in `docs/REPO-WEIGHT.md`.
History is **not** rewritten (`filter-repo`) unless the owner approves it explicitly.
**Why:** a guard stops the growth at once at no cost; the past weight is only removable by a rewrite
that would break every open worktree and PR.
**Consequences:** a new video or recording is hosted outside git (GitHub Releases, YouTube) and the repo
keeps its source and a link. The steps of `docs/REPO-WEIGHT.md` land as their own PRs.
