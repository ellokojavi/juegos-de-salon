## D-418 · The Grand Final repeats the week's games
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** amended by D-521 · **Relation:** amends D-163 (the final no longer keeps five fixed rounds) and D-332 (the three games from the lab can be rounds of the final) · **Topics:** cup
**Context:** Since D-163 the admin picks the week's games and their order, but the final kept its five fixed rounds (Línea, Toque y Fama, Reinas, Palabra, ¿En qué año?). In a cup whose week swapped some of those for Zip, Tango or another game of the pool, the final brought back games nobody had played that week and left out the ones they had; a group noticed on its final ("the final repeats games"). The owner decided the final is a repetition of the week's games, from now on.
**Decision:** The final has **one round per game of the week, in the order they were played**, each with its variant (`finalRounds(meta)` in `public/cup/engine.js`, read from `meta.cal`). A game with a short version (the five of before, `SHORT` in `games/final/engine.js`) plays it, as before. Any other game is played **whole**, with its own generator for the final day, so the content is new; Conexiones, whose grid is fixed per cup, plays another grid (`grillaFinal`). Each round scores 0 to 100 (a whole game with its own `resultado()`), the final's score is the average of all its rounds and it still counts double. A whole game that scores its time (Loteo, Rutas) counts only its own round's time (`t0` in the final's moves); one played full screen (¿Dónde queda?) goes full screen for its round. The waiting room lists the rounds, the folded rules say how each short round is played (with one line above the list for the games played whole, the owner's review option A), and "Round i of n" counts this cup's rounds. The trial plays the same rounds, the others in their trial size. **Finals that opened before `FINAL_FROM_WEEK` (2026-10-22 12:00 Pacific) keep the five fixed rounds**, and so does the final played outside a cup (the lab's practice).
**Why:** The final is the week's summary: playing again what you played is the point, and a game you dropped from the week should not come back on the day that counts double. Playing the five short versions where they exist keeps the final from growing for the classic week. A switch date instead of applying it to every cup keeps any final already open from changing its rounds halfway; `meta.cal` cannot be edited after creation (Firebase rules), so the rule reads only what every cup already stores, and nothing changes in the rules.
**Consequences:** A week of games without a short version makes a longer final (each played whole). The running score between rounds follows D-417 (the average of the rounds played so far, out of 100) for any number of rounds. The result's card and breakdown list this cup's rounds. The rule "La final no cambia" of D-163 no longer holds; inside a cup the final's texts in the four languages no longer say five rounds; outside a cup (the lab's practice and its trial) they do, with their own texts `comoSuelta` and `puntajeSuelto` (the owner's review option A).
