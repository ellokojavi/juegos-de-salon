## D-502 · A campaign for regulars waits only for 5 visit days; every finished game is now counted on the phone
**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-458 · **Topics:** home
**Context:** 🥂 `brindis-apoyo` was switched on on Oct 26 and showed to no one. D-458 asked for 10
finished games, read from the inbox's count (`juegos-de-salon:buzon`), but only La Copa's endings
add to it: a phone that plays Hangman every day never got past zero. Firebase's pseudonymous
`activos/` showed 19 phones with 5+ visit days in four weeks, 13 of them back after the switch.
The owner asked to look back and let the regulars in, and chose visit days alone.
**Decision:** `REGULARS` is `{ days: 5, games: 0 }`: 5 distinct visit days in the last four weeks
and not paid. The visit days (`juegos-de-salon:dias`) reach back to Oct 17, so a phone that
already comes back qualifies on its next visit, with nothing written from the server. From now on
stats.js `trackFinish` also counts every finished game on the phone (`juegos-de-salon:partidas`,
never sent), and `readProfile` takes the higher of it and the inbox's count, so a games threshold
can come back later and mean every game.
**Why:** The games condition was blocking everyone because of how it was counted, not because
those phones do not play; the visit days already measure who keeps coming back.
**Consequences:** The toast reaches the regulars at once. The panel's line drops the games part
while it is 0.
