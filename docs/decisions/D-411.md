## D-411 · A question that no longer needs an answer is settled, not left open
**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** completes D-273 (one-tap decisions) and D-360 (the ideas agent) · **Topics:** agents
**Context:** issue #556 asked whether to try Pips in the lab, but Pips had already been built there at the owner's request in chat (D-381). The question stayed open, and neither box fit: a Yes would queue the work again, and a No would record the wrong answer.
**Decision:** owner's request: a decision whose work was done another way, or that no longer applies, is settled without a box. `processes.mjs settle <issue> <n> "why"` adds a line under its options («✔️ No answer needed: …», `<!-- hecho:n -->`). It also comments on the issue and closes it when nothing is left to decide. When `ideas.mjs record` moves an idea to prototyped, graduated, shelved or discarded while its question is unanswered, it settles that question on its own.
**Why:** the issues stay clean, and the owner never has to pick a Yes or No that no longer means anything.
**Consequences:** a settled decision counts as made: a box checked afterwards carries nothing out. `approve.test.mjs` tests `settle`, `undecided` and `decisionQueuing`.
