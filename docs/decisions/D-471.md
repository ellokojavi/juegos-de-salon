## D-471 · The owner's own browsers are kept out of Live: the `owner` environment
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-44 and D-138 (which environments exist) · **Topics:** panel
**Context:** The owner plays and visits juegosdesalon.cl from their own phones and computers, and
those games and visits counted in the panel's Live numbers as if they were players. Only local,
Tailscale and preview hosts were kept apart (`dev`). The owner asked to remove their own activity.
**Decision:** The panel has a **Don't count this browser** checkbox next to Environment. It saves
`juegos-de-salon:not-counted` = `1` in that browser's localStorage. On the live site such a browser
writes its signals to `stats/owner/` instead of `stats/prod/` (`envFor` in transport/stats.js, the
same check in vigia.js), and the panel lists `owner` as **My devices**. Hosts that are already `dev`
stay `dev`. Rankings, rooms and La Copa are untouched: only the stats environment changes. The
checkbox is behind the lab key (D-395, card `#seccion-no-contar`) until the owner validates it.
**Why:** A separate environment rather than dropping the signals: the owner can still look at their
own test games, and nothing is lost if a browser is marked by mistake. A per-browser mark needs no
account or id (D-44).
**Consequences:** Each browser is marked on its own; clearing its data, or a new phone, counts again
until marked. What was counted before is not moved. A room shared with real players is recorded under
the environment of the browser that writes it.
