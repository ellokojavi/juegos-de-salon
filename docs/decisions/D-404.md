## D-404 · Everything new is indexed in the search, in every language
**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-403 (the search for everyone) and D-387 (its keywords) · **Topics:** home, languages
**Context:** the owner asked that every important new thing in Juegos de Salón (a game, a new kind of tournament) be indexed in the 🔎 search, and that the search work in every language of the app, Portuguese and German included, starting with what exists today. Until now the keywords were in Spanish and English only, and nothing asked a new game for them.
**Decision:** every card on the home page has `KEYWORDS` in `public/assets/js/search.js` in es, en, pt and de, at least three each, and `search.test.mjs` fails otherwise (and if keywords are left for a card that is gone). Something new inside an existing card adds its words to that card: La Copa's keywords carry its kinds (3 and 7 days, the final, global, Chile, Brazil), so searching a kind of tournament finds La Copa. A new language of the app adds keywords for every card when it comes in. C-17 and the step-by-step guide (docs/AGREGAR-JUEGO.md, step 3) say so.
**Why:** a rule that only lives in a document is forgotten; a test that fails on the PR is not. A Portuguese or German player should find "dados" or "Würfel" the same as a Spanish one.
**Consequences:** the 20 games and La Copa now have Portuguese and German keywords. If one day a kind of tournament or mode deserves its own result in the dropdown, that is a new experiment, in the lab first.
