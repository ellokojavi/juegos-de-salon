## D-402 · The 🔎 search opens and closes as one smooth motion
**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-398 (how the field opens and folds) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** the owner found D-398's motion abrupt, not slow: the other pills vanished in one frame before the
field grew, the field grew from a fixed 44 px at the row's left edge while the 🔎 pill sits centered, so on
closing the pill reappeared somewhere else, the fold and the pills' return ran one after the other, and the
field's 🔎 and cyan border swapped in at once.
**Decision:** the field lies over the row (absolute) instead of taking its place, so nothing in the row moves.
On opening, `search.js` measures the 🔎 pill's box inside the row and the clip grows out of exactly that box;
the field's 🔎 starts exactly over the pill's and slides to its place; the other pills fade out while it
grows; the cyan border, ✕ and the placeholder arrive at the end (the pills fade out in 0.08 s, so no text crosses them). Closing is the same motion reversed (0.24 s), with the pills
fading back in during the fold, and the field's 🔎 lands on the pill's. One easing, `cubic-bezier(0.3, 0, 0.2, 1)`,
both ways; durations as before. Reduced motion: no animation.
**Why:** a change reads as smooth when one thing moves continuously and nothing jumps: same start box, same icon
position, overlapping fades.
**Consequences:** `tools/e2e/home/search.mjs` checks that the pills fade instead of leaving and that opening moves
nothing in the row.
