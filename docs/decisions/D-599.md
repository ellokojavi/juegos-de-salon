## D-599 · Nothing under the footer, on every page
**Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-77 (its "the leftover background is invisible") · **Topics:** touch
**Context:** The owner, with a screenshot of Reinas on Android: «No admitas en ninguna portada que el footer deje espacio vacío debajo de él. Esa franja azul oscura es inarmónica.» D-77 builds `.app` for the small height (`svh`) and said the background left over below it could not be seen. It can: when Android hides its address bar the page grows to the large height, and a cover whose footer sits in a wrapper (every La Copa game's cover) or is not pushed down by `margin-top: auto` showed that space as a dark band. Measured on the phone D-77 measured (dvh 1016, svh 960): 20 of 20 La Copa covers and 5 of 14 covers with a game.json had one, up to 172 px (Reinas).
**Scope:** every page, not only the covers (the owner, the same day: «Esto aplica a toda página»). Without the fix 7 of the 20 other pages with a footer had the band too.
**Decision:** The `atardecer` footer carries its last colour on to the bottom edge of the screen with a shadow under it (`footer.atardecer::after` in `base.css`), clipped to the footer's width. A shadow paints without making the page longer, so it changes nothing for D-77's height: `.app` stays `svh`. Every cover is measured: `tools/e2e/footer-bottom.mjs` sizes the screen to 1016 px, makes the column 56 px shorter, scrolls to the end and compares the screen's last row of pixels with the footer's own last row; `conformance.mjs` runs it on every game with a game.json, `tools/e2e/cup-games/footer-bottom.mjs` on every La Copa game's cover and `tools/e2e/pages-footer-bottom.mjs` on every other page with a footer (the home page in each language, Rankings, Uno al día, the lab). `tools/e2e/footer-bottom.test.mjs` makes every page of the site be measured by one of the three or listed in `NO_FOOTER` with why it has none, so a new page cannot slip past.
**Why:** The fix had to hold for any cover's structure, and the first attempt (growing the footer's padding by `100dvh - 100svh`) fixed only the covers whose footer is the screen's last child: `margin-top: auto` absorbed the growth elsewhere, and a footer inside a wrapper never reached the bottom at all. The measure reads pixels and not the rule because headless Chrome has `svh == dvh`: a rule check would have passed with the band in place, which is how D-77 missed it.
**Consequences:** A new page fails the unit test until it is placed. A page measured with no footer on screen fails the check. A new cover structure needs no special care: whatever sits above the footer, the space below it is the sunset's.
