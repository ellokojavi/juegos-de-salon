## D-422 · La Bodega in two subgroups: games kept and improvements launched
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-280, D-372 and D-420 (what La Bodega keeps and how it is shown) · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** La Bodega had grown to nine folded cards in no order: games that did not prosper, a game
taken off the home page and improvements that already reached everyone, mixed together.
**Decision:** La Bodega is split like the lab above it (🎲 Juegos en prueba / ✨ Mejoras en prueba) into
two subgroups, each with a smaller title:
- **🎲 Juegos guardados** (`#bodega-juegos`): El caso, La clave and Cuarto Rey. Each card's text opens
  with why it is there: 💤 No prosperó or 👋 Salió de la portada.
- **✨ Mejoras lanzadas** (`#bodega-mejoras`): the improvements that reached everyone, each marked
  🚀 Lanzado as before.
The cards stay folded to their title (D-280); only their place and the games' tag change.
**Why:** the owner's request, choosing this split over one by outcome or by area of the app.
**Consequences:** a card moving to La Bodega goes to the subgroup that fits it; a game opens its text
with its tag. `tools/e2e/fourth-king/lab.mjs` checks both subgroups and the tags.
