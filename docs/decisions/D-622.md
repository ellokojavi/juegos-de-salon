## D-622 · A skin dresses every page of the site, the games included
**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-457 · **Topics:** home, identity
**Context:** Launching the Thanksgiving skin, the owner asked that it show on every view of Juegos de Salón, not only the home page, and made it a rule for every skin: "la idea es que el estilo permee a través de todas las páginas". Until then D-457 kept skins to the home page and Rankings and the games looked the same all year.
**Decision:** Every page with a player on it loads the skin engine (`season.js`) through `tester-boot.js`, which `vigia.js` already puts on every page but the panel and frames inside another page. So a template's palette, favicon, corner and falling things (`fall`) reach the home page, Rankings, the profile, La Copa and every game, with no per-page work: a new skin is still one row and one stylesheet. The touches that move across the screen (`flyby`, `peek`, `strut`, `scares`) stay on the home page only, where nobody is mid-play. The rest of D-457 holds: nothing takes a tap, covers a text or a button (the shared touches sit behind the page), moves with `prefers-reduced-motion`, or shows with `?test`.
**Why:** The owner wants a date to feel like the whole site dressed up, not a home page in costume. Hanging it on the one module every page already loads makes the rule hold for every page, present and future, instead of a list someone has to keep.
**Consequences:** `season.test.mjs` now checks that `tester-boot.js` loads `season.js` (the old check that no game module loads it is gone). A skin's palette must read well behind any game: its stylesheet only sets `--fondo` and its own corner, and anything it adds sits behind the page. Iframes (a game's cover inside the home page's sheet) take the skin from the page around them, not their own.
