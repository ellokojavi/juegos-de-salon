## D-462 · A tap outside a splash is forgiven once: it comes back on another visit (in the lab)
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-351 (closing a campaign marks it as seen) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** A splash campaign shows once per phone (D-351): whatever closes it marks it as seen, and
it never comes back. That includes a tap on the dark background around the window and Escape. The window rises
a moment after the home page draws (D-431), just as the player is reaching for a game or scrolling,
so that tap can be a slip. With 🥂 `brindis-apoyo` on (the invitation to support the site, D-458), a
slip loses the one chance a regular player had to see it. The owner asked whether a tap outside
was a good idea and chose to keep it, but forgiving.
**Decision:** A **soft close** (a tap outside the window, or Escape) is forgiven once per campaign:
the first one closes the window without marking the campaign as seen, so it comes back on a later
visit through the usual one-window gate; the second soft close marks it (`softClose`,
`SOFT_CLOSES = 2`, counted per id in `juegos-de-salon:splash-suave`). The ✕, "Ahora no" and the
campaign's links are clear choices and mark it at once, as before. It applies to every campaign.
Without storage it counts as seen at once, so nobody is nagged.
**Why:** The gesture is expected: a window that ignores a tap outside feels stuck. But a mistake
should not cost a one-time message; one more chance is enough, and a player who really means to
skip it does so with a second tap and never sees it again.
**Consequences:** In the lab first (D-395, D-409): only lab key holders get the forgiveness
(`labAccess()`, card `#seccion-splash-cerrar`); everyone else keeps D-351's behaviour until the
owner approves it.
