## D-434 · Julepe leaves the home page for La Bodega
**Date:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** follows D-420 (same treatment as Cuarto Rey); replaces D-88 · **Topics:** structure
**Context:** Julepe (id `julepe`) had been off the menu since D-88, but its card stayed on the home page
dimmed as "Próximamente", at the end of the list, and its page stayed public at `/julep/`. The owner
asked to stop showing it on the home page and keep it in the lab's La Bodega, as a game.
**Decision:** Julepe is treated exactly like Cuarto Rey in D-420. It keeps its entry in `GAMES` with
`labs: true` and `available: false`, `slug: 'julep'`, and its page moves from `/julep/` to
`/labs/julep/`, behind the lab's door (`labs-puerta.js`, D-281) and with `noindex`. Its card goes to
📦 La Bodega → 🎲 Juegos guardados (`#seccion-julepe`), folded, its text opening with 👋 Salió de la
portada (D-422).
- **Off everything players see:** the home page (no dimmed card either: `ordenPortada` leaves out every
  `labs` entry), the 🔎 search (its `KEYWORDS` are gone) and `POPULARIDAD`.
- **The old links:** `/julep/` and `/julepe/` are bridges to `/labs/julep/` generated by `og.mjs`,
  keeping the query string, so an invitation's `?room=` still arrives. Without a lab key the door
  sends the visitor to the menu: online rooms now work only among key holders.
- **What does not change:** the id, the saved games, the rooms' Firebase paths, the panel's history,
  the spec (`docs/games/julep.md`) and its screenshots folder.
**Why:** the owner's request. Keeping it in La Bodega instead of deleting it keeps the work (engine,
sealed deal, rooms); bringing it back is undoing the entry and moving the folder back.
**Consequences:** the e2e scripts play it at `/labs/julep/`; `tools/e2e/julep/lab.mjs` checks the home
page does not offer it, that it is folded in La Bodega's kept games, and that both old routes lead there.
