## D-615 · The profile's use is counted by player name, for the owner only
**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-44 ("signals, not people") and D-349 (never joined with rankings) · **Topics:** panel, rankings
**Context:** The player profile (/profile/) went out to everyone on 2026-11-23 (flag `profile`). The owner wants to know whether it moves the needle: how many open it, who, what they use there, and whether players sign in, create their account and come back more because of it. The panel only counted anonymous signals (D-44) and a random id per browser never joined with the rankings (D-349), so "who" could not be answered. Asked, the owner chose named tracking for the profile only.
**Decision:** Two records. (1) Anonymous, for guests and players alike, in the stats day by Pacific date: `perfilPt/<day>/<event>` counters (visits as guest or player, the way in: the pill, its bubble, an achievement window, a link on the site or straight in; the three tabs; another player's sheet; «Ver cómo me ven los demás»; the achievement window shown and «Ver mis logros» tapped; a sign-in started on the profile; and every sign-in and account creation, split on the profile or elsewhere), and `perfilIdPt/<day>/<id>` = 1 with the same random browser id as `activosPt/` (D-349), so the panel can say how many browsers opened it, their share of the active ones and whether they came back. (2) Named, only for a signed-in player: `perfilUso/<jid>/<YYYY-MM-DD>` with small counters (visits, tabs, sheets, «see how others see me», «Ver mis logros», signed in or created here), written only by a phone seated as that player, one up at a time, capped at 200 a day, and readable only by the owner's UID; the panel shows it with the player's public ranking name. To count accounts created before the launch the owner's UID may also list `jugadores/` by `at`. The panel's 🎖️ Profile section (`#/profile`) shows it; the privacy page says it in three languages.
**Why:** The question is about people, and the name a player chose for the public rankings is already public; joining it with what they do on their own profile, visible only to the owner, answers "who" with no new identifier. Guests stay anonymous: no name exists to attach. Writes go over REST with the anonymous session's token, so the counters open no websocket (D-470). The owner's own browser (not counted, D-471) and robots write nothing.
**Consequences:** `perfilUso/` grows a few bytes per player and day and is not swept. Sign-ins and creations by place exist only from this version on: the panel says "unknown" before it, not zero; accounts created by day come from `jugadores/<jid>/at` and go back before the launch. Return of profile visitors against the rest is a correlation, and the panel says so. Code: `public/assets/js/profile-usage.js`, `public/panel/profile-report.js`; rules and their test `firebase/profile-usage.test.mjs`.
