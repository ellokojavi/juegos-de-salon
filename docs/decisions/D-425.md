## D-425 · Creating a cup: the week in view, the 🎲 and week styles, in the lab
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** amended by D-437 · **Relation:** follows D-163 (the admin picks the week), D-331 (variants per day) and D-418 (the final replays the week); behind the lab key (D-395, D-409) · **Topics:** cup, touch
**Context:** With 13 games in the pool, building the week became the heaviest part of creating a cup. The owner asked for a review of the whole create screen and for a button that draws a random week, so nobody has to think it through. The review (a design page with a prototype, shared with the owner) found that the button already existed but nobody saw it: "🎲 Otro orden al azar", a small ghost button below the week and the versions block, whose text said "order" while it changed the games. The week itself was hidden behind "How long does it last?", a question with a single answer outside the test store (7 days). At 360 px the form was about 3,000 px tall with 11 decisions; the six games left out sat in a sideways strip that showed two and a half cards with no word on what each game is; the random draw only avoided two days in a row of one skill, so a week could bring three logic games; and the final's row did not say that it replays the week. The owner approved the six recommendations of the review.
**Decision:** A new create screen, for now only for lab key holders (`labAccess()`, card `#seccion-copa-crear` in `/labs/`); everyone else keeps the screen of D-163. In the test store (`?test`) the scripts see the screen of before unless the URL says `&lab`.
- **One screen, not steps** (the owner's answer 1): the cup's name and when it starts; the week; your name and PIN; and "More options", folded.
- **The week in view from the start**, a Balanced draw of seven days. The 3-day cup (tests only, D-100) moves to "More options". *D-437 brings it up front, next to when it starts, as **¿Cuánto dura?**.*
- **🎲 Crear al azar** ("Make it random"), a big yellow button above the week, draws the whole week again, and the cards show it: each one runs through random games of the pool like a slot machine's reel and lands with a small bump, one after the other, in under a second (the owner's request; nothing moves with reduced motion). On a small phone, where the week starts below the 🎲, rolling first scrolls the page so the 🎲 sits at the top and the whole week is seen (the owner's answer to the usability review). *The first version said "🎲 Otra semana" and kept the days locked with 🔒; the owner's review on trying it (same day) renamed the button and took the locks out: they were not understood, and a day can be changed or moved by hand anyway.*
- **Week styles** (answer 3): ⚖️ Balanced (the whole pool, never two days in a row of one skill, at most two of one skill), 🧩 Puzzles (the seven board games), 🧠 Trivia and words (Línea, ¿En qué año?, ¿Dónde queda?, Agentes and both Toque y Fama) and ✨ What's new (every pool game with the home page's New label, the rest balanced; the chip is shown only while some game has the label). Each one is a rule for the die (`semanaAlAzar` and `ESTILOS_SEMANA` in `public/cup/engine.js`), not a fixed list. A week changed by hand has no style marked, and the die rolls the last one picked. A row of tags counts the week's skills and marks in pink one that appears more than twice, unless the style asked for it.
- **Tapping a game opens a sheet** (the app's own, D-223, with a yellow **Listo** to close it) with the 13 games, each with its home page tagline: picking one outside the week replaces the day, one already in it swaps the two days. The same sheet moves the day one place up or down and picks its version (D-331), which no longer has its own block below the week. The sideways strip of games left out goes away; dragging a day by its ⠿ grip stays as a shortcut (answer 4, D-85).
- **The final's row says it replays the week**: "Repite los 6 juegos y vale doble" (D-418).
- **"More options"** holds the words' language, the audience and the custom link (and the 3-day cup in the test store, until D-437), and says in one line what is chosen ("Español · 🇨🇱 Chile · sin link propio"). A bad custom link opens it to show the error.
- **The PIN is asked once, with 👁 to see it** (answer 5). With a signed-in player it is still not asked (D-359).
- **"When it starts" keeps no option chosen** (answer 2, C-8 and D-38), and each option says its day of the week: **Ahora (jue)**, **Mañana (vie)**, Otra fecha. On this screen Today is "Now" (the owner's review). Once chosen, a line under the Create button says when the cup starts and ends, in words.
**Why:** the owner's request and their answers to the review. The die where the eye already is takes a group to a week it likes in a few rolls, and the sheet fixes the one day it does not; the styles give a reason to roll; the sheet's taglines say what each game is at the moment of choosing. Folding what already comes well chosen halves the form (about 1,850 px at 320 px instead of 3,000) and leaves four required decisions. Lab first (answer 6, D-409): the owner tries it on real cups before it reaches everyone.
**Alternatives discarded:** a wizard in three steps (more taps, and the summary is hidden); "When it starts" preset to Tomorrow (D-38); keeping the strip of games left out next to the sheet (two ways to do one thing); a sticky Create button (the form is now short enough, and no other screen of the app has one).
**Consequences:** `cal` is saved as before (`meta.cal`), so the Firebase rules and every cup already created are untouched. `public/cup/engine.test.mjs` draws 300 weeks per style and checks their rules; `tools/e2e/cup/create-week.mjs` plays the screen end to end; the same script checks the week and a day's sheet in English, Portuguese and German at 320 px, so `tools/e2e/paths.mjs` is left untouched while the screen is in the lab. Measuring how the week is built (as drawn, with dice, by hand) is left for the step that takes the screen to everyone, when there are enough cups to compare. Taking it to everyone is its own decision, once the owner has tried it.
