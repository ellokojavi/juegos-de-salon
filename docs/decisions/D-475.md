## D-475 · Uno al día's history indexed by day, so the owner's readers query only the days they need
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-230, D-415, D-473 · **Topics:** random-and-daily, panel
**Context:** #848 step 4 (the 2026-10-27 audit, #834): `unoAlDia/<jid>/<n>` is keyed by player, so the panel, the nightly points rebuild (D-415), the weekly report (D-352) and the ideas agent (D-360) downloaded the whole history of every player on every read, a cost that only grows with players × days. The player's own page reads only their own history, and the rules of the day, week and points tables check against `unoAlDia/<jid>/<n>`.
**Decision:** The history stays where it is and stays the source. Every played day is also written, in the same update, to an index by day, `unoAlDiaDias/<n>/<jid>` = its score; the rules accept an entry only in the write that creates that history day and only with its score, and only the owner reads it (a 🧊 marker gets none). `unoAlDiaIndice` = `{ desde, at }` marks the index complete; `tools/firebase/uad-index.mjs` copies the older history into it (dry run by default, `--apply` to write), re-reads and checks the copy, and only then writes the mark. With the mark, the panel queries the index from its first loaded day (`orderByKey`, `startAt`), the weekly report and the ideas agent from the first day of their range (`readHistory`), and the rebuild finds the players of the month in the index and reads only their histories (a streak walks back past the month). Without it, every reader reads the whole history as before.
**Why:** An index by day rather than shards by month: a range of day keys is a month, a week or a panel period alike, with no month arithmetic in the rules (they have no number-from-text, D-465), and the history the game and the rules use does not move, so nothing the players touch changes and no data is deleted. The mark makes the switch safe in either order: between the merge and the migration readers keep reading the whole tree.
**Consequences:** One more number per played day. An old cached page writes the history without its entry; running the script again fills it in (its dry run counts what is missing). The one-off October backfill (D-414) still reads the whole history. `unoAlDiaInicio/` and `invitados/` are still read whole.
