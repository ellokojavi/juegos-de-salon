## D-483 · Toque y Fama's deduction notes: a ✏️ Notes toggle under the keypad (lab)
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** revoked · **Relation:** the owner discarded it the same day: it costs key time during the game against the little it adds, so the code and its lab card were removed (#895 closed as not planned); extends D-102 (the shared keypad and its long-press strike) · **Topics:** touch
**Context:** #895 (from the evaluation in #883): casual players want to mark digits while they
deduce, not only strike them. The keypad already strikes a digit with a long press (🚫), and a tap
types it; a second gesture on the same key had to leave typing untouched.
**Decision:**
- **A ✏️ Notes toggle under the keypad.** On, a tap on a digit cycles its mark instead of typing it:
  none → 🚫 not in the number → ? maybe → ★ sure → none. ⌫ and the guess button are off and the keys
  have a dashed outline while it is on, and a one-line hint says what each mark means; the button
  reads ⌨️ Back to typing and turns it off. The long press still strikes, with notes on or off.
- **🚫 is the strike that already existed** (the struck digit does not type); ? and ★ only mark, and
  the digit still types.
- **The marks live in the same set as the strikes** (`notes` of `teclado.js`): a bare digit is
  struck, `?3` and `★5` are the other two. So they persist exactly where strikes already did (the
  game's session, La Copa's `{ i, n }`), for the current game only, with no new storage.
- **No rule or score change**, so it is the same in solo, one phone, rooms, Uno al día and La Copa
  (all of them mount the same keypad).
- **Lab first** (D-395, D-409): `notasLab()` in `public/cup/games/number/ui.js` adds the toggle only
  with `labAccess()`; its card is `#seccion-tyf-notas` in `/labs/`.
**Why:** a mode is unambiguous: a tap never does two things, so typing is exactly as before when it
is off. A long press is taken (the strike), a double tap would delay or misfire every typed digit,
and swipes on 58 px keys at 320 px are unreliable. One small pill under the keypad keeps it out of
the way.
**Consequences:** checked at the end of `tools/e2e/bulls-and-cows/choose-box.mjs`. Publishing it for everyone
is removing the `labAccess()` check in `notasLab()` and moving the card to La Bodega.
