## D-410 · The share sheet shows the vertical Shorts, in a Spanish and an English tab, playing on their own
**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** replaces D-254, corrects D-253 · **Topics:** sharing, marketing
**Context:** The promo video v7 was published as two YouTube Shorts on the new channel (hola@juegosdesalon.cl):
Spanish `zM9v2UwEsM8` and English `UubTGcUoQD8`. Until now the home page share sheet (D-253) showed the
16:9 video behind a thumbnail with ▶ and opened only in Spanish (D-254).
**Decision:** The sheet opens in every language and shows the vertical Short in a tall card, under two
tabs, 🇪🇸 Español and 🇬🇧 English. Spanish opens on the Spanish tab; English, Portuguese and German on the
English one. The tab's Short starts playing on its own as soon as the sheet opens, muted (browsers
only autoplay without sound) and looping, embedded from youtube-nocookie.com; the two share options
stay underneath, and "Compartir el video" sends the Short of the tab on show (`youtube.com/shorts/<id>`).
The Shorts live in `SHORTS` (`public/assets/js/compartir-portada.js`); a new upload changes the id there.
**Why:** the owner asked for it: both videos featured, the video watchable right in the card without
an extra tap, and the share controls under it.
**Consequences:** opening the sheet now loads YouTube (D-253 waited for a tap); it only happens when
someone opens the sheet, not on the home page itself. Without a video in Portuguese or German, those
languages see the English one. The 16:9 video stays on YouTube for the channel, no longer on the home page.
