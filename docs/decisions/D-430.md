## D-430 · Splash campaigns queue up, and a teaser can be a picture alone in Spanish and English
**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Status:** amended by D-431 · **Relation:** amends D-351 · **Topics:** home
**Context:** the owner asked for a teaser of 🚂 Tren en Vilo (still in the lab) over the home page for
seven days, in Spanish for Spanish and in English for every other language, while the
`tres-juegos-demo` campaign was still on. D-351 allowed one campaign at a time and remembered only the
last one a phone closed, so a second campaign would have replaced the first.
**Decision:** `ACTIVE` in `public/assets/js/splash.js` is a queue, `[{ id, until }, …]`, in the order the
campaigns were turned on. A player with several still to see gets them one after the other: closing
one opens the next (the owner's request), and following a link leaves the rest for the next visit.
`localStorage` keeps the list of ids the phone closed (`juegos-de-salon:splash-visto`, JSON); the bare
id it kept before reads as a list of one, so nobody sees `tres-juegos-demo` again. `splash.mjs on <id>`
adds a campaign at the end (again: a new date, same place) and `off [<id>]` takes one or all out.
A campaign may set `langs` (here `['es', 'en']`): the languages it has, the others get it in English,
and the whole window, buttons included, is in that language. An item with no name, text or link is a
**teaser**: its picture alone, wide (`ratio`), crisp pixel art, with a `still` for reduced motion. The
first one, `tren-en-vilo-pronto` (through 2026-10-28), is "Muy pronto · Algo nuevo se viene…" over a
GIF drawn with the game's sprites (`marketing/splash/teaser.mjs`): a train stops at the edge of a gap
where the bridge would start, birds fly by, a "?" pops up. No name, no link, nothing about crossing:
the owner wanted it a mystery.
**Why:** two campaigns on at once is the case that will repeat (a novelty while another runs), and
stacking two windows or dropping one would break what D-351 protects.
**Consequences:** a returning player may see two windows in a row on one visit, never more than the
campaigns on. `covers.mjs` names only the campaigns a PR adds to the queue.
