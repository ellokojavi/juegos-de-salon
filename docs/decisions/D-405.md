## D-405 · Generala opens from the home page and joins Uno al día on October 23
**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** carries out D-248 (the announcement); applies D-247 (Generala in Uno al día) and D-333 (the "New" label) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, home
**Context:** Generala was published complete in `/generala/` but announced on the home page as "Próximamente" (D-248), outside the dice, the favorites and Uno al día, until the owner said to open it.
**Decision:** Owner's request ("Habilitemos generala"): `available: true` in `games.js`, with `nuevoDesde: '2026-10-20'`, so it carries the "New" label for two weeks and goes on top of the home page while it does (D-335). It joins Uno al día with `desde: '2026-10-23'` (`DESDE_GENERALA` in `uno-al-dia.js`), the first day of the third deck, and is listed in `GRUPO` with the other games that have their own page: on its day `/generala/?today` opens 🧍 Jugar solo with the seed's dice and scores a third of the total, up to 100, as D-247 already built.
**Why:** a game enters the daily deck only when the next one is shuffled, so no day already played changes. The deck now being played runs October 13 to 22; October 23 is the next shuffle, so Generala comes out from there, and with 17 games the wait before a game comes back stays at 7 days.
**Mechanism:** `uno-al-dia.test.mjs` checks that adding Generala changes no day before October 23, that it comes out in the deck shuffled that day, and that its page is `generala/?today`; `games.test.mjs` already checks that each group game in the deck is available on the home page.
**Consequences:** if this reaches players after October 22, `DESDE_GENERALA` moves to the first day of the following deck (November 9), or the October 23 deck would change after someone played it. The README row moves from "🔜 coming soon" to playable, and GE-09 and GE-12 close.
