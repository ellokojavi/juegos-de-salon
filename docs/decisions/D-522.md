## D-522 · Sessions always open the PR, without asking
**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** processes
**Context:** Sessions finished the work, pushed the branch and then asked the owner whether to open the PR, an extra round trip with only one answer.
**Decision:** Once the work is pushed, the session opens the PR on its own, without asking. The owner said: "Siempre abre un PR. No me preguntes más".
**Why:** The owner reviews on the PR and its preview (D-429); a branch without a PR is invisible to them and to CI's gates.
**Consequences:** The rule sits in CLAUDE.md's "Rules every session follows". Merging still follows docs/MERGING.md; this only covers opening the PR.
