## D-522 · Sessions always open the PR and drive it to merge, without asking
**Date:** 2026-11-01 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** agents
**Context:** Sessions finished the work, pushed the branch and then asked the owner whether to open the PR, an extra round trip with only one answer.
**Decision:** Once the work is pushed, the session opens the PR on its own, without asking. The owner said: "Siempre abre un PR. No me preguntes más".
**Why:** The owner reviews on the PR and its preview (D-429); a branch without a PR is invisible to them and to CI's gates.
**Consequences:** The rule sits in CLAUDE.md's "Rules every session follows". Opening is not the end: the same session runs the lane check, its agents and turns on auto-merge (docs/MERGING.md, D-216, D-519), and in a cloud session subscribes to the PR's activity so a failing check is fixed on its own. The first version said only "open", and PR #1118 sat red with no auto-merge until the owner noticed.
