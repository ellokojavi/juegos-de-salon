## D-508 · Every Uno al día game shares a spoiler-free grid
**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** amends D-482 · **Topics:** random-and-daily, sharing
**Context:** #883 W2.1 (owner approved): every game in Uno al día must share an emoji result. D-482 left out Agentes, Dudo, Generala and Batalla Naval.
**Decision:** they get one too, built in `share-grid.js`: Agentes only the counts (`agentesGrid`: agents found 🟩 and missed 🟨, wrong taps 🟥, the assassin ❌, sorted, never the board), Dudo its dice left (`vidasGrid`), Generala its score card (`planillaGrid`: 🟩 scored, ⬛ crossed out), Batalla Naval its shots in order (`disparosGrid`: 🟦 water, 🟧 hit, 🟥 sunk, never the cells).
**Why:** a grid is what gets a daily game shared; counts and orders without positions give nothing away.
**Consequences:** `SPOILS` now holds only Conexiones; a new Uno al día game ships with a grid.
