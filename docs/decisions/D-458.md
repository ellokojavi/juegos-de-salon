## D-458 · A splash campaign can wait for regulars: 5 visit days and 10 finished games (the toast to support does)
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** amended by D-502 · **Relation:** amends D-351 (who sees a campaign) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** Every splash campaign shows once to every phone, including one on its very first
visit. For an announcement that is fine; for 🥂 `brindis-apoyo`, the invitation to support the site
(D-448, D-449), it is not: asking someone who has not played anything yet to pay is the opposite of
what the end-of-game invitation does (it waits for the 6th game). The owner asked how to make sure
new players are not bothered by it, and chose the thresholds.
**Decision:** A campaign can carry `audience: 'regulars'` in CAMPAIGNS (`public/assets/js/splash.js`).
Such a campaign shows only on a phone that already plays here (`isRegular`): **5 or more distinct
visit days in the last four weeks** and **10 or more finished games** (`REGULARS`), and that has
not paid through the support card. The profile is read from what the phone already keeps
(`readProfile`): its visit days (`juegos-de-salon:dias`, kept by stats.js for every visitor), the
inbox's count of finished games (`juegos-de-salon:buzon`) and the support card's paid mark
(`juegos-de-salon:support`). A phone that does not qualify does not get the campaign marked as seen:
it waits, and shows the first visit the phone qualifies, once, through the same one-window gate
(D-431). Campaigns without `audience` go on as before. `?splash=<id>` still previews any campaign.
`brindis-apoyo` carries it.
**Why:** The people likely to support the site are the ones who keep coming back; a first visit
should meet the games, not a request for money. The data was already on the phone, so nothing new
is stored or sent.
**Consequences:** Nothing changes for players today: `brindis-apoyo` is off until support leaves
the lab. Not gated behind the lab key: it only narrows who sees a campaign that is off.
