## D-458 · ☕ Invítame a un café leaves the lab, with the footer as a grid
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** completes D-448 and D-449 (both in the lab until now) · **Topics:** home
**Context:** Support (D-448) and its card over the page (D-449) were tried behind the lab key, and the Stripe Payment Link is live. With the coffee, the home page footer had two options, and the owner approved a quiet way to show them: a two-column grid of outlined, unfilled buttons under a thin rule with a 🎲, so they do not read as game cards and more options fit later. The owner then asked to publish all of it ("café completo + footer") and to move it to La Bodega.
**Decision:** The three `labAccess()` checks in `public/assets/js/support.js` go: every player sees ☕ in the home footer beside 💬 Suggestions and comments, `?support=thanks` and `?support=open` open the sheet for everyone, and the end-of-game invitation follows its D-448 rule (from the device's 6th game, at most once a week, never with the inbox, rests 30 days after opening, stops after paying). The footer's options live in `#footer-links` (`public/index.html`): one option is a plain link, two or more become the grid, an odd last one spans the row. The lab card `#seccion-apoyo` moves to La Bodega's launched improvements, 🚀 Lanzado. The 🥂 splash campaign (`brindis-apoyo`) stays off until it is turned on from the panel's 📣 Campaigns.
**Why:** The owner validated it in the lab (D-395, D-409) and chose to publish. The grid keeps the footer tidy as it grows without competing with the games above it.
**Consequences:** A new footer option is one more child of `#footer-links`. Support is counted in the day's stats as `support/<event>` for every player now.
