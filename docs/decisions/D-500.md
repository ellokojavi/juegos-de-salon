## D-500 · Uno al día totals are written only by the hourly rebuild; /today/ adds the phone's own pending days (lab first)
**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** the second half of D-499 (#854 option (a)); amends D-414 and D-415 (who writes the totals, how often the rebuild runs) · **Topics:** random-and-daily, rankings
**Context:** D-499 made the nightly jobs the single authority for room sweeping and Cup podiums and left Uno al día's totals for a second PR: the month's points, the +10 per invited friend, the week's sum and the best streak were still added up by each phone (`anotarPuntosDia`, `anotarInvitado`, `anotarDia`), and a write the rules refused was swallowed, so the nightly rebuild (D-415) kept correcting drift. Moving them to the server means a player's points would wait for the job, which players would see.
**Decision:**
- **Phones write only raw events** behind the runtime flag `uad-night-totals` (D-498, card `#seccion-uad-night-totals` in /labs/): the history day and its index entry, the day's row (`records/uno-al-dia/d<n>`), the 🧊 markers, the day's start and the acceptances. With the flag on, `uno-al-dia-red.js` passes `totales: false` to `anotarDia` (no week sum, no best streak) and calls neither `anotarPuntosDia` nor `anotarInvitado`. Test scripts get it only with `?lab`.
- **The rebuild is the single writer of the totals:** `uad-points-backfill.mjs --rebuild` now also counts every friend who accepted in the month (not only those a phone marked `p`) and marks `p` on them, rewrites each week's sum with a day in the month when it differs from the history (days on time with points, as a phone adds them), and raises the best streak from the server's walk, never lowering it (a phone's days played without signing in may have made it longer).
- **How fresh:** `uad-rebuild.yml` runs every hour (`23 * * * *`), not nightly: another player's day reaches the month's, week's and streak rankings within the hour. A run reads about 20 KB today, far under Spark's download quota. The stats retention step (D-472) still runs once a day, in the 08 UTC run.
- **/today/ before the job:** `conMiCuenta` (in `uno-al-dia-red.js`) adds to the rebuild's row the points of this phone's days after the row's last counted day `u` (the phone's own walk, late days at 0), and places the player among the table read with that total; ties share the place. The month's ranking on /today/ shows the player's row with that same total, the list ordered again (`tablaConMiCuenta`, passed to `bloqueRanking` as `ajustar`). The cache (`uad-pts-cache`) and the home card's chip take that number. Another player's /records/ shows the server's row until the job runs.
- **Launch, in a later PR** (when the owner flips the flag to *all* and validates it): remove the gate, `anotarPuntosDia`, `anotarInvitado` and `anotarDia`'s week and streak steps, and tighten `firebase/database.rules.json` and the `jugador-local.js` mirror so a phone can no longer write `records/uno-al-dia-pts`, the week periods of `records/uno-al-dia`, `records/uno-al-dia-racha`, a day's `p`/`w` or an acceptance's `p` (only the administrator), with the rules tests to match. Until then phones outside the lab keep writing them, and the rules must keep accepting them.
**Why:** one writer per derived view (D-499): a total that depends on each phone's writes drifts silently. An hourly job keeps the ranking close enough without Blaze, and the phone's own count means the player never sees their points wait.
**Consequences:** the first run rewrote six week rows that the phones had added to the wrong week or missed (days uploaded later in the week, refused writes). A lab player's week and streak rows lag up to an hour. GitHub runs the job 24 times a day instead of once; the jobs registry (`tools/jobs/jobs.json`) carries the new cron. `uad-points-backfill.test.mjs` covers the weeks and the best streak; `uno-al-dia-jugador.test.mjs` covers `totales: false` and `conMiCuenta`.
