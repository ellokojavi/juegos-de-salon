## D-620 · Every share text is built by a template of share-templates.js
**Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** — · **Topics:** sharing
**Context:** Each share button assembled its own text with `cabecera()` and a join. The format of U-30 (header, blank line, one idea a line, the link last) was checked only by eye, and the inventory of 2026-11-24 (docs/SHARING.md) found it broken in Uno al día (no blank line), in duels (no header) and in room invites when the inviter's name had not arrived. The owner asked for a system for every use, not fixes one by one.
**Decision:** `public/assets/js/share-templates.js` builds every share text, one template per kind: room, invite, app, game, result, table, video. A page gives it the parts (header, the game's tagline line, lines, paragraphs) and passes the text to `compartir()`, which adds the link. A new share picks a kind; a new kind needs a D-n. `share-templates.test.mjs` (🤖) runs every template in every language and for every game through `formatProblems()`, and fails any page that calls `cabecera()` or writes a `{emoji} *{title}*` header of its own.
**Why:** A rule only an eye checks comes back broken; one place to change a format reaches every share at once.
**Consequences:** Uno al día's three shares get their blank line, a duel challenge gets its header (`⚔️ *{game}* · {format}`, its line now opens with 👋), and a room invite without a name says «Te invitaron a jugar» (`roomLineNoName`). `COMMON.invite` is split into `roomContext`, `roomLine` and `roomLineNoName`. Rules in docs/SHARING.md and U-30.
