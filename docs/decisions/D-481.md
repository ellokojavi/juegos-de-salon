## D-481 · A texture per region on Queens' board, for colorblind players
**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Status:** current · **Relation:** extends D-134 · **Topics:** cup, touch
**Context:** Queens' regions differ only by color (D-134). With protanopia or deuteranopia several of its ten light colors collapse into two or three hues (orange, green and yellow look alike; blue, lilac and teal too), so a board can be unreadable. Issue #896, from the evaluation in #883, asked for a pattern per region and to check the other boards with color regions.
**Decision:** Each Queens region gets a subtle texture over its color, always on (no option): thin dark lines in three directions, dots, rings, a grid or a crosshatch at low alpha, from `public/assets/js/region-patterns.js` (`pattern(z)` gives the layers, `withPatterns(node)` turns them on). Region 0 stays plain. A clash keeps its red stripes and a ✕ gets a white halo, so the crown and the marks read on top. It first showed only with a lab key (D-395), listed in `/labs/` as `#seccion-tramas`; on 2026-10-27 the owner published it for everyone from that card (D-480), on the board and on the drawing in the rules. The other boards were checked: Tango (sun and moon symbols), Zip (a numbered path) and Loteo (outlined plots with numbers) do not rely on color alone, and Pintaminas already stripes its second color (#349), so none of them changes.
**Why:** A texture is the standard fix when color is the only cue, and kept subtle it costs nothing to players who see colors well, so an option would only add a setting nobody finds. Verified with an SVG `feColorMatrix` simulating protanopia and deuteranopia in headless Chrome (body filter over a played board, at 320 px): without textures, regions merge; with them, every region is told apart.
**Consequences:** Going public is dropping the `labAccess()` check in `withPatterns`. A new board whose regions differ only by color uses the same helper.
