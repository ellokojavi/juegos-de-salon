# 🔗 Connections (Conexiones)

A game built in `public/cup/games/connections/` (SUELTOS in `games.js`, id `'conexiones'`): played in a day of
La Copa and on its own at `/connections/`, the Cup's practice screen with a link that says no "cup" (D-212).
Every game is one more game (C-21, D-542); the Cup-wide behaviour (seed, trial, clock, result) is in
[cup.md](cup.md).

## Summary

Sort sixteen words into four groups of four that share something. In La Bodega since D-421 (🕵️ Agentes took its place); its page still opens and the cups created before keep playing it.

## Rules

- Make 4 groups of 4 words that share something; some words seem to fit two groups.
- Tap 4 words and Confirm. Four mistakes end the game.
- Score: 25 per group found, minus 5 per mistake. Tiebreak: the faster player.

The texts the player reads (how to play, scoring, tiebreak) are its entry in `JUEGOS_COPA` of
`public/cup/rules.js`, in the three languages (C-3).

## Modes

Solo only: a day of La Copa, or its own page `/connections/` (no cup; with a player, it goes to the rankings,
D-212). It came out in Uno al día until 2026-10-24. Before each Cup day, a trial with other content that does not count (D-103).

## Flow

Waiting room (how to play, the trial) → the 3-2-1 countdown (D-105) → the board → the result with its
score breakdown (`public/cup/desglose.js`, D-106) and the share card. The grid comes from the cup's language (`grillas.js`, `grillas-en.js`, `grillas-pt.js`); the words follow the cup's language, not the player's (D-170).

## Message protocol

None of its own: the day's content comes from the seed `code:day:salt` (D-97), the same on every phone
with nothing on the network. The Cup saves the result (see [cup.md](cup.md), "Protocolo de mensajes").

## Files

- `public/cup/games/connections/engine.js` — picks the day's grid from the seed and checks a group
- `public/cup/games/connections/ui.js` — the board, Confirm and the mistakes
- `public/cup/games/connections/grillas.js, grillas-en.js, grillas-pt.js` — the grids per language
- `public/connections/index.html` — its page, generated by `node tools/release/og.mjs cards`
- Engine tests: `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs` (with the other Cup games)

## Exceptions to the canons

Debt in `docs/games-debt.json` (the ratchet of `public/assets/js/games.test.mjs`): no `engine.test.mjs`
in its folder (its engine is tested in `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs`) and no `window.__…` hook in
its `ui.js` (the end-to-end scripts drive it through La Copa's `window.__copa`). It does not save with
`createSessionStore` (C-6): the Cup keeps its attempt (`public/cup/cuenta.js`, `docs/memory-debt.json`).
It has no `game.json` (`docs/manifest-debt.json`).
