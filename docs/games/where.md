# 📍 Where Is It? (¿Dónde queda?)

A game built in `public/cup/games/where/` (SUELTOS in `games.js`, id `'donde'`): played in a day of
La Copa and on its own at `/where/`, the Cup's practice screen with a link that says no "cup" (D-212).
Every game is one more game (C-21, D-542); the Cup-wide behaviour (seed, trial, clock, result) is in
[cup.md](cup.md).

## Summary

Pin 5 cities on a globe without names.

## Rules

- Tap the globe where each city is and Confirm; drag to spin it, pinch or double-tap to zoom, and a compass straightens north (D-155, D-156, D-200).
- Score per city: 100 minus 4 per 100 km off; the day is the average of the 5. Tiebreak: the faster player.

The texts the player reads (how to play, scoring, tiebreak) are its entry in `JUEGOS_COPA` of
`public/cup/rules.js`, in the three languages (C-3).

## Modes

Solo only: a day of La Copa, or its own page `/where/` (no cup; with a player, it goes to the rankings,
D-212). It can come out in Uno al día. Before each Cup day, a trial with other content that does not count (D-103).

## Flow

Waiting room (how to play, the trial) → the 3-2-1 countdown (D-105) → the board → the result with its
score breakdown (`public/cup/desglose.js`, D-106) and the share card. Cities come from `ciudades.js` (529: the 195 capitals, famous and second-tier cities, with level and ISO code, D-157) and the map from `mapa.js`, generated by `node tools/generators/map.mjs generate` (`check` keeps every city inside its country). `globo.js` draws the NASA Blue Marble with WebGL (D-159), falling back to the vector map; the globe fades in once the image arrives (D-245); the 4096 px image is downloaded only on a wide canvas or once zoomed (D-460).

## Message protocol

None of its own: the day's content comes from the seed `code:day:salt` (D-97), the same on every phone
with nothing on the network. The Cup saves the result (see [cup.md](cup.md), "Protocolo de mensajes").

## Files

- `public/cup/games/where/engine.js` — the day's cities, the orthographic view and its inverse, the distance on the sphere
- `public/cup/games/where/ui.js` — the screen and the waiting room's spinning globe (`portada()`)
- `public/cup/games/where/globo.js` — the canvas drawing
- `public/cup/games/where/ciudades.js, nombres.js, mapa.js` — the cities, their names per language and the map
- `public/cup/games/where/earth-2004-09/` — the WebP images and tiles
- `public/where/index.html` — its page, generated by `node tools/release/og.mjs cards`
- Engine tests: `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs` (with the other Cup games)

## Exceptions to the canons

Debt in `docs/games-debt.json` (the ratchet of `public/assets/js/games.test.mjs`): no `engine.test.mjs`
in its folder (its engine is tested in `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs`) and no `window.__…` hook in
its `ui.js` (the end-to-end scripts drive it through La Copa's `window.__copa`). It does not save with
`createSessionStore` (C-6): the Cup keeps its attempt (`public/cup/cuenta.js`, `docs/memory-debt.json`).
It has no `game.json` (`docs/manifest-debt.json`).
