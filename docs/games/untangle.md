# 🧶 Untangle (Desenredo)

A game built in `public/cup/games/untangle/` (SUELTOS in `games.js`, id `'desenredo'`): played in a day of
La Copa and on its own at `/untangle/`, the Cup's practice screen with a link that says no "cup" (D-212).
Every game is one more game (C-21, D-542); the Cup-wide behaviour (seed, trial, clock, result) is in
[cup.md](cup.md).

## Summary

Drag the knots until no thread crosses another (D-179; out of the lab since D-190).

## Rules

- A thread passing over a knot that is not its own also counts as a crossing (closer than 24 units of 1000).
- Ten levels, from 6 to 15 knots, in four minutes; 🔄 Restart level returns the knots to the level's start (two taps, the clock runs on).
- Score: 10 per level; tiebreak: when the last one was solved.

The texts the player reads (how to play, scoring, tiebreak) are its entry in `JUEGOS_COPA` of
`public/cup/rules.js`, in the three languages (C-3).

## Modes

Solo only: a day of La Copa, or its own page `/untangle/` (no cup; with a player, it goes to the rankings,
D-212). It can come out in Uno al día. Before each Cup day, a trial with other content that does not count (D-103).

## Flow

Waiting room (how to play, the trial) → the 3-2-1 countdown (D-105) → the board → the result with its
score breakdown (`public/cup/desglose.js`, D-106) and the share card. The engine first draws a crossing-free figure, then scatters the knots on a circle: always solvable, never a single solution. Threads are drawn as curved ropes (D-182, D-183, D-185) but crossings are counted on the straight line. Its share card goes in the shared text, not on the result screen (`SIN_TARJETA_EN_RESULTADO` in `cup/game.js`). Its skill is `espacial`, so the calendar draw keeps it apart from Queens, Zip and Tango.

## Message protocol

None of its own: the day's content comes from the seed `code:day:salt` (D-97), the same on every phone
with nothing on the network. The Cup saves the result (see [cup.md](cup.md), "Protocolo de mensajes").

## Files

- `public/cup/games/untangle/engine.js` — the levels and the crossing count, in integers on 1000 × 1000
- `public/cup/games/untangle/ui.js` — the board, the drag and Restart level
- `public/untangle/index.html` — its page, generated by `node tools/release/og.mjs cards`
- Engine tests: `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs` (with the other Cup games)

## Exceptions to the canons

Debt in `docs/games-debt.json` (the ratchet of `public/assets/js/games.test.mjs`): no `engine.test.mjs`
in its folder (its engine is tested in `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs`) and no `window.__…` hook in
its `ui.js` (the end-to-end scripts drive it through La Copa's `window.__copa`). It does not save with
`createSessionStore` (C-6): the Cup keeps its attempt (`public/cup/cuenta.js`, `docs/memory-debt.json`).
It has no `game.json` (`docs/manifest-debt.json`).
