# 🔤 Bulls and Cows: Word (Toque y Fama: Palabra)

A game built in `public/cup/games/word/` (SUELTOS in `games.js`, id `'letras'`): played in a day of
La Copa and on its own at `/word/`, the Cup's practice screen with a link that says no "cup" (D-212).
Every game is one more game (C-21, D-542); the Cup-wide behaviour (seed, trial, clock, result) is in
[cup.md](cup.md).

## Summary

Crack a five-letter secret word with bulls and cows, in 8 guesses.

## Rules

- Any 5 different letters are a valid guess, no dictionary, as Bulls and Cows accepts any number with different digits.
- Each letter turns green in its place (bull) and yellow elsewhere (cow); the card uses 🟩 and 🟨 (#196).
- Score: 10 per letter found in its place (once per place) plus, if solved, 50 − 5 × (guesses − 1) (D-108). Tiebreak: the faster player.

The texts the player reads (how to play, scoring, tiebreak) are its entry in `JUEGOS_COPA` of
`public/cup/rules.js`, in the three languages (C-3).

## Modes

Solo only: a day of La Copa, or its own page `/word/` (no cup; with a player, it goes to the rankings,
D-212). It can come out in Uno al día. Before each Cup day, a trial with other content that does not count (D-103).

## Flow

Waiting room (how to play, the trial) → the 3-2-1 countdown (D-105) → the board → the result with its
score breakdown (`public/cup/desglose.js`, D-106) and the share card. The secret word comes from the cup's language list (`palabras.js`: 120 in Spanish, 185 in English, 143 in Portuguese). The keyboard is QWERTY with Ñ in Spanish and a gap in its place in the others (`alfabeto(lang)`, D-170).

## Message protocol

None of its own: the day's content comes from the seed `code:day:salt` (D-97), the same on every phone
with nothing on the network. The Cup saves the result (see [cup.md](cup.md), "Protocolo de mensajes").

## Files

- `public/cup/games/word/engine.js` — the day's word, the verdict of a guess and the keyboard per language
- `public/cup/games/word/ui.js` — the board and the keyboard
- `public/cup/games/word/palabras.js` — the word lists
- `public/word/index.html` — its page, generated by `node tools/release/og.mjs cards`
- Engine tests: `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs` (with the other Cup games)

## Exceptions to the canons

Debt in `docs/games-debt.json` (the ratchet of `public/assets/js/games.test.mjs`): no `engine.test.mjs`
in its folder (its engine is tested in `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs`) and no `window.__…` hook in
its `ui.js` (the end-to-end scripts drive it through La Copa's `window.__copa`). It does not save with
`createSessionStore` (C-6): the Cup keeps its attempt (`public/cup/cuenta.js`, `docs/memory-debt.json`).
It has no `game.json` (`docs/manifest-debt.json`).
