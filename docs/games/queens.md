# 👑 Queens (Reinas)

A game built in `public/cup/games/queens/` (SUELTOS in `games.js`, id `'reinas'`): played in a day of
La Copa and on its own at `/queens/`, the Cup's practice screen with a link that says no "cup" (D-212).
Every game is one more game (C-21, D-542); the Cup-wide behaviour (seed, trial, clock, result) is in
[cup.md](cup.md).

## Summary

One queen per row, column and color zone, none touching, not even diagonally.

## Rules

- Tap a square for a queen, again for an X, again to clear it.
- Undo, Clear all (two taps, D-169) and Give up (D-110) under the board; Undo takes back the last gesture without asking (D-283).
- Score by time: 100 up to 30 s, down to 10 at 5 min (D-107); mistakes cost nothing; giving up is 0.

The texts the player reads (how to play, scoring, tiebreak) are its entry in `JUEGOS_COPA` of
`public/cup/rules.js`, in the three languages (C-3).

## Modes

Solo only: a day of La Copa, or its own page `/queens/` (no cup; with a player, it goes to the rankings,
D-212). It can come out in Uno al día. Before each Cup day, a trial with other content that does not count (D-103).

## Flow

Waiting room (how to play, the trial) → the 3-2-1 countdown (D-105) → the board → the result with its
score breakdown (`public/cup/desglose.js`, D-106) and the share card. The zones are adjusted one square at a time until the solver finds a single solution. Regions carry a texture over their color for colour-blind players (D-481, `assets/js/region-patterns.js`). The elapsed time shows in the play head (`clock`, D-384).

## Message protocol

None of its own: the day's content comes from the seed `code:day:salt` (D-97), the same on every phone
with nothing on the network. The Cup saves the result (see [cup.md](cup.md), "Protocolo de mensajes").

## Files

- `public/cup/games/queens/engine.js` — the board, its zones and the single-solution solver
- `public/cup/games/queens/ui.js` — the board, its buttons and `camino` (tested in `juegos.test.mjs`)
- `public/cup/games/deshacer.js` — the Undo button and its steps
- `public/queens/index.html` — its page, generated by `node tools/release/og.mjs cards`
- Engine tests: `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs` (with the other Cup games)

## Exceptions to the canons

Debt in `docs/games-debt.json` (the ratchet of `public/assets/js/games.test.mjs`): no `engine.test.mjs`
in its folder (its engine is tested in `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs`) and no `window.__…` hook in
its `ui.js` (the end-to-end scripts drive it through La Copa's `window.__copa`). It does not save with
`createSessionStore` (C-6): the Cup keeps its attempt (`public/cup/cuenta.js`, `docs/memory-debt.json`).
It has no `game.json` (`docs/manifest-debt.json`).
