# 📅 What Year? (¿En qué año?)

A game built in `public/cup/games/year/` (SUELTOS in `games.js`, id `'anio'`): played in a day of
La Copa and on its own at `/year/`, the Cup's practice screen with a link that says no "cup" (D-212).
Every game is one more game (C-21, D-542); the Cup-wide behaviour (seed, trial, clock, result) is in
[cup.md](cup.md).

## Summary

Type the year of 6 milestones; the closer, the more points.

## Rules

- Type each milestone's year in four boxes and OK; a BC switch shows only when the theme has a card before Christ (today Historia, D-326).
- The margin grows with the event's age: `max(12, (2026 − year) / 3)` years (D-325).
- Score per milestone: `100 / (1 + (2·distance / margin)²)`; the day is the average of the 6. Tiebreak: the faster player.

The texts the player reads (how to play, scoring, tiebreak) are its entry in `JUEGOS_COPA` of
`public/cup/rules.js`, in the three languages (C-3).

## Modes

Solo only: a day of La Copa, or its own page `/year/` (no cup; with a player, it goes to the rankings,
D-212). It can come out in Uno al día. Before each Cup day, a trial with other content that does not count (D-103).

## Flow

Waiting room (how to play, the trial) → the 3-2-1 countdown (D-105) → the board → the result with its
score breakdown (`public/cup/desglose.js`, D-106) and the share card. Its milestones come from the Timeline decks (`cup/games/mazos.js`); in a cup it uses a different theme from the Timeline day (`temasDeLaCopa`). The reveal says "Your answer: …" (`yearAnswer`).

## Message protocol

None of its own: the day's content comes from the seed `code:day:salt` (D-97), the same on every phone
with nothing on the network. The Cup saves the result (see [cup.md](cup.md), "Protocolo de mensajes").

## Files

- `public/cup/games/year/engine.js` — the day's milestones and the score
- `public/cup/games/year/ui.js` — the boxes, the BC switch and the reveal
- `public/cup/games/mazos.js` — the decks shared with Timeline
- `public/year/index.html` — its page, generated by `node tools/release/og.mjs cards`
- Engine tests: `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs` (with the other Cup games)

## Exceptions to the canons

Debt in `docs/games-debt.json` (the ratchet of `public/assets/js/games.test.mjs`): no `engine.test.mjs`
in its folder (its engine is tested in `public/cup/games/juegos.test.mjs`) and no `window.__…` hook in
its `ui.js` (the end-to-end scripts drive it through La Copa's `window.__copa`). It does not save with
`createSessionStore` (C-6): the Cup keeps its attempt (`public/cup/cuenta.js`, `docs/memory-debt.json`).
It has no `game.json` (`docs/manifest-debt.json`).
