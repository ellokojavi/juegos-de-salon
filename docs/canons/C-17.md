## C-17 · The home page

The home page (`public/index.html`) is the game list and the ways to pick one. Its rules
came out of several decisions that were corrected along the way; what is in force is here, and each point says
where it comes from. Whatever changes the home page is changed here, in the same PR.

- **Everything on the home page can be searched, in every language (D-403, D-404).** The 🔎 search
  (`public/assets/js/search.js`) finds each card by its name, tagline and type in any language, and by
  its `keywords`, kept in its `games.js` entry (the games manifest, #855): what it is, what it is played with and what it is like, in es, en, pt and de, at
  least three per language. A new game, or anything new with a card, brings its keywords in the same
  PR; something new inside an existing card (a new kind of La Copa, a new mode) adds its words to that
  card's keywords. `search.test.mjs` fails without them. A new language of the app adds its keywords
  for every card when it comes in.
- **The list is drawn at once from the last visit's copy (D-435).** The page saves the list's HTML
  (`juegos-de-salon:portada-foto`, keyed by version, language, day and today's One a Day played or not) and a script under `#games`
  puts it back before the modules arrive; the module clears `#games` and draws the real cards in the
  same task. So anything drawn into the list must look the same in the copy as drawn (no state that
  only the module knows, nothing random), and the space above the list (`#lang-slot`, `.tools`,
  `#azar-slot`, `.filtros`) is reserved at the size the module draws it, or the list jumps.
  `tools/e2e/home/instant.mjs` checks that the copy equals the real list.
- **What is offered and in what order.** The cards come from `PORTADA` in `games.js`: the games
  in `GAMES` and La Copa's standalone games (`SUELTOS`, apart only because of how they are built, D-198)
  go mixed, from most played to least, by the `POPULARIDAD` list (D-255): most games
  finished in the last four weeks first, recounted every week by the processes round with
  `tools/agents/popularity.mjs update` (D-361), with no list
  of its own in the page (D-142, D-149). La Copa goes first, in a half row next to Uno al día. A
  game that is not available shows, with "Próximamente" next to its name, but does not open, not even
  with the keyboard (`available` in `games.js`, C-2; D-142), and goes at the end of the list, with and without
  a filter (v0.128.2). The card says players and duration,
  and if the game is not in the chosen language it says so before opening it ("🇪🇸 En español",
  `idiomas` in `games.js`; today none declares it, D-170). One of `SUELTOS` with `labs: true` does not
  show on the home page, even if its link is already being shared.
- **A new game says "New" for a week** (D-333, D-507): next to its name, like "Próximamente", from its
  `nuevoDesde` date in `games.js` for `DIAS_NUEVO` days (`esNuevo`). Every game that reaches the
  home page brings that date; `games.test.mjs` stops one that doesn't.
  Unlike the plain yellow "Próximamente" pill, it is a sticker that shines softly, so it sits on the
  card rather than over it (D-373): tilted −6°, a see-through dark fill with a thin faint edge in its
  own color and a light shadow, the title's rainbow flowing through its letters (`shimmer`, one
  gradient across the word, like the home page title), a low breathing glow and a little wobble around
  its tilt now and then; with reduced motion it stays tilted and still (owner's requests; the
  owner picked this look among ten on a comparison page).
  While it says "New" it also goes **on top of the list, right after La Copa** (`ordenPortada`,
  D-335): with no plays yet, by popularity it would be last. When the label goes, it goes back to
  its place in `POPULARIDAD`.
- **🔥 "Popular"** (D-361, for everyone since D-369): the first three of `POPULARIDAD` that are not
  La Copa (`populares`), a game still "New" included: it then wears both labels (D-548). They wear the "New" sticker in fire colors, with the 🔥 outside the
  gradient, beside the name when it fits (on its own line only when the name fills it).
- **✨ Nuevos and 🔥 Populares filters** (D-362, D-369): a row of two chips above the types, each shown
  only when it has games, kept in the link as `?type=new` / `?type=popular`. With a favorite marked,
  ⭐ Favoritos goes first in that row: with its name, tighter, or the ⭐ alone, centered (D-364).
  These shortcuts (and the 🔎 pill, D-390) are outlined, with no fill; the types are filled. The chosen
  pill is the same cyan in both rows, because both are one single choice (D-407).
- **The app's name on the floating menu** (D-364, D-369): while the filters float, "🎲 Juegos de
  Salón" tops them in the title's font, in the 36 px the floating filters leave above them, so nothing
  moves when it appears; a tap goes back to the top. The frosted background reaches
  4 px below the last row, so the space under the filters matches the space above the title.
- **Home page experiments** (D-361): what is tried on the home page before players see it is a URL
  parameter the page reads, off by default, listed in `public/labs/home/experiments.js`. `/labs/home/`
  opens the real home page with `?lab`, which draws the bar (`public/labs/experiments.css`) with one
  switch for each; never in a frame, where an iPhone would not let anything stick (D-363).
  **Everything in development sits behind the lab** (D-395): an experiment, or anything else not yet
  validated, turns on only for a lab key holder (`labAccess()` in `public/assets/js/lab-access.js`);
  without one its parameter shows the site as it is. It reaches everyone only once validated in the lab.
  A feature on a page players visit asks its runtime flag, `flag('<id>')` in `public/assets/js/flags.js`, named in `flag-list.js` (D-515), with a row in `tools/e2e/flags-held.json` (`off` or `lab`) so the e2e tests know its state (`flags-snapshot.test.mjs` fails without it)
  (D-498): Firebase `flags/<id>` is *lab*, *all* or *off*, and the owner publishes it with a toggle
  (on its `/labs/` card, the one place), not a PR. With none
  being tried, its 🏠 Experimentos de la portada card waits in La Bodega, folded (D-372).
- **In the lab: the games as carousels** (flag `home-carousels`, `/labs/` 🎠; the owner's design, 2026-11-18, changed
  by the owner from the lab since; ready for everyone, the owner, 2026-11-21). With the flag on, the top of the page keeps the bar, the title, the tagline,
  La Copa and Uno al día; 🎲 Juego al azar, the filters, the 🔎 search and the list's cards give way, under a dividing
  line, to rows of three games, as many lines as each needs, nothing sliding (`home-carousels.js`, `shelves()`): ⭐ Favoritos (only with one marked), 🔥 Populares, ✨ Nuevos, 👥 Con
  amigos (more than one can play), 🤹 Destreza (3 minutes or fewer, or the skill type) and one per type, except skill (in Destreza) and words (`NO_ROW` in `home-carousels.js`, the owner, 2026-11-20: Agents moved to the logic type; the other word games sit in their other rows). The hero
  gives the dice's room back, so the first row shows on the first screen. **A game shows once on the page**: it stays in the first row that
  holds it, in that order, and leaves the later ones; a row left with no game is not drawn, and every game of the
  list is in one (`home-carousels.test.mjs`). **A tile is the icon and the name, nothing else**:
  no label, no duration, no well around the icon; the tile's edge is its only box.
  A tap opens the game's cover, as the list's cards do (for everyone with the view, the owner, 2026-11-21). **Lab sub-flag `home-carousels-sheet`** (replacing `home-carousels-covers`, which meant the opposite): a tap opens instead the game's bottom sheet (`.hoja`), **always as tall as ten rows of its ranking need** (up to what the
  screen gives) and the same in its two tabs, which share one cell: the sheet does not move when the tab changes,
  and the ranking is read as the sheet opens. **Detalles:** on top, the game's cover animation playing by itself, to
  show how it is played (the demo of its waiting room, `portada`, or without one its "how to play" drawing,
  `ejemplo`; only La Copa's games bring one), in a frame of `cup/cover/?game=<id>` so the home page loads no
  game's code or styles (D-378), never tapped; its page grows the drawing to fill the frame (up to three times,
  never wider) or shrinks it on a short screen (down to half), and leaves out a credit line with a link. A game
  with no animation shows its icon, big, in that place. Under it, centred on it, the game's line and who plays, how
  long and its types. **🏅 Ranking:** its solo table when it has one (Generala, Toque y Fama, Ahorcado, as its own cover puts it first, D-274), else its wins when it is played in a room,
  **the first ten and no more** (the rows around the player are left out here), in rows of 36 px with no card.
  The ⭐ and ▶ Jugar are always in sight (U-39). **The sheet is shared as the game's cover**: its share button sends
  the game's name, the invitation line of a cover's share and `/?game=<folder>` (the game's folder, in English,
  C-18; under the language's door), which opens the home page with that game's sheet over it; on a phone without
  the carousels the same link goes to the game's own page, so it never leads nowhere. The rows are drawn at once and do not wait for the sub-flag's read (a tap opens the cover until it says the sheet). The 👥 row sorts by how many play, which D-149 left out of the
  filters: it is tried here, and a launch decides it. The phone remembers the flag
  (`juegos-de-salon:home-carousels`) so the next visit hides the filters before the modules arrive. The tests hold
  the flag off (`tools/e2e/flags-held.json`); `tools/e2e/home/carousels.mjs` turns it on.
- **A promotional splash, off by default** (D-351): when the owner turns on a campaign
  (`ACTIVE` in `splash.js`, with `node tools/agents/splash.mjs on <id> | off`), a window over the
  home page tells what is new, with the page darkened behind; each phone sees it once, and a new
  campaign shows again. ✕, "Ahora no" and its links mark it as seen at once; a tap outside the window or Escape is forgiven once: the first such close leaves it for a later visit, the second marks it (D-462). A campaign marked `audience: 'regulars'` waits, unseen, for a phone with 5 or more
  visit days in the last four weeks and 10 or more finished games that has not paid (D-458): never a first visit. Each game in it shows a clip of real play (`marketing/splash/`) and
  opens with ▶ Play; the way out is a quiet "Ahora no". It fits a 320×640 screen with three games. `?splash=<id>` previews one (one that is not on, only with a lab key, D-395);
  `?test` and the scripts never see it. Tried in the lab from the home page's experiments bar (D-389).
  **One window per visit** (D-423, D-424): when the recap of an ended cup of La Copa, or of an ended
  season of Uno al día, opens (in the lab for now, except the cups the owner opens to all their players, D-426), the splash waits for the next visit (`startRecap` in `assets/js/recap.js` runs first). One gate (`assets/js/home-windows.js`, D-431) extends it to every window the home page opens on its own: recap, then splash (one campaign per visit), then the install bubble. A visit whose address carries `?support=` (☕ the support card or Stripe's thank-you, D-449) is already that visit's window: the gate opens nothing else. Behind the lab flag `news-once` (D-546): a visit from a notification (`?notif=`) opens no window either, and a campaign whose items are all games this phone already finished (`juegos-de-salon:jugados`, kept by `trackFinish`) is skipped.
- **Seasonal touches** (D-457): on a date's days (Halloween first, then Thanksgiving) every page, the games included (D-622; moving
  touches stay home-only), dress up a little from a template in `public/assets/season/templates.js`: the tab's favicon,
  the hero's emoji cycle, background particles, a corner drawing, a flyby, a rare peek. None of it is
  text, takes a tap or moves under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and `?test` keeps it off; a template in
  the lab (`public: false`) shows only to lab key holders. How to add one: `docs/SEASONS.md`.
- **Above the list, the shortcuts that choose for you.** 🎲 Juego al azar, under the tagline, picks
  from the games that can be opened, never La Copa (D-188), and opens the game with the
  dice on top (D-237). It always picks among every game, whatever filter is on, and never one that
  needs a group, three or more players at least (`alAzar` in `azar.js`, D-374; Cuarto Rey was the one, and since D-420 it is in the lab's La Bodega, off the home page). Uno al día is a half-row card next to La Copa: both weigh
  more than a game (D-230, D-239; the detail, in [UNO-AL-DIA.md](../UNO-AL-DIA.md)).
- **The two half-row cards** (`.fila-alta`, `.game-card.alta`) carry the 40 px emoji
  to the left of the title, the tagline and the pills full width, and the title in color: La
  Copa in gold, Uno al día in light blue; games go in white. So that players and duration
  fit on one line, the pill says "👥 2–10" and "jugadores" stays only for the screen
  reader (`.unidad`); a game's wide card does show it (D-239). The pills go
  at the bottom left, apart from the tagline and at the same height on both cards (D-305).
- **Filtering is by type or by favorites, and nothing else.** One type at a time (`TIPOS`), which
  toggles on and off with a tap. There are only chips for types that have some game that
  can be opened, so the list is never empty. How many play is not filtered: each game
  asks it inside (D-149). With a filter on, the line below says "Se ven n de m" and
  offers "Ver todos" (D-149; #71, resolved in [USABILIDAD.md](../USABILIDAD.md)).
- **⭐ Favoritos is one more filter**, exclusive with the types, and appears only with some favorite
  marked. Favorites belong to that browser (`localStorage`, by game id), with no account
  (`favoritos.js`, D-238). La Copa cannot be marked, nor a game that is not available. Unmarking
  one while in ⭐ Favoritos does not remove it from view (it can be marked again), unless none
  is left: then all are shown.
- **The filter goes in the URL, in English** (`?type=logic`, `?type=favorites`): coming back from a game
  leaves it as it was and a filtered link can be shared. An old link (`?tipo=`) is rewritten
  and one that does not apply (`?jugadores=`, or `?type=favorites` without favorites) shows all without warning
  (D-149, D-214, D-238).
- **The filters stick to the top** when scrolling down the list, and tapping one while stuck
  goes to the first filtered card (D-196). Stuck, their background rises to cover the notch
  strip: cards are not seen passing behind the clock (U-12). They keep floating after that tap even when the
  result is too short to scroll that far: an empty space under the list (`.filtros-relleno`) holds
  them, and shrinks as the player scrolls up, so they leave at the first pixel up; the jump is
  rounded up to a whole pixel (D-371).
- **The types go in a single row**, at any width from 320 px and in the three languages:
  with the row under 560 px the emoji moves above the name (five types did not fit at 900 px in es and pt), and under a 300 px box the chips tighten so as
  not to stick to the edge (D-144, D-149, U-11). What is added to the row gets in only if it fits,
  and that is measured on the page, not assumed: ⭐ Favoritos goes first, with its name or with the ⭐
  alone and less room between chips, and if it still does not fit (at 320 px, almost always) it goes on its own
  line above the types (D-238, D-241).
- **Nothing overflows the screen at 320 px**, not even where the browser cannot break a long
  word ("Galgenmännchen" in Chrome on Linux): the card column does not grow wider than the
  screen (`minmax(0, 1fr)`, D-238).
- **Nothing inside a card is tappable**: the card is one whole link. What is tapped
  without opening the game, like the ⭐, goes beside the link, on top of the card (`.game-slot`), with
  a 44 px touch area and without covering text (C-8, D-238).
- **The top bar** comes in two blocks: the language (C-3) on the left and, on the right, the
  actions: sound (C-4), 🏅 rankings (D-212, open to everyone since D-217; a medal and not a cup, so it is not confused with La Copa) and share. With
  6 px between buttons they fit on one line from 375 px; narrower (320 px), the actions
  drop to a second line and stay on the right (v0.128.1). In every language, share opens a sheet (the one from D-223 and D-232) with the vertical
  promo Shorts under two tabs, 🇪🇸 Español and 🇬🇧 English (Spanish opens on its own tab, the other
  languages on English), playing muted on their own (D-410), and two options: **Invitar a
  jugar** (invite to play), which shares the app with its social card in the language being viewed (`compartirApp`,
  D-226, U-33), and **Compartir el video** (share the video), the Short of the tab on show (`SHORTS` in `compartir-portada.js`,
  D-253, D-410). Its icon is the operating systems' share icon, an
  open box with an arrow pointing up, in SVG and not an emoji (D-242), with the box light blue,
  the arrow pink and the bottom shadow of the page's buttons (D-243).
  The bubble inviting to add the app to the home screen shows at the bottom, only on phones where it can be
  added (not on the computer or in the already-installed app), and the ✕ turns it off for good in that
  browser (D-232).
- **The footer's last line links the privacy page** (D-463): the version, "código en GitHub" and
  "Privacidad" (`COMMON.privacy`), which opens `/privacy/`. The same page is linked from the note
  under the email field of every feedback form. **The page describes the code:** a PR that stores
  something new about players, or keeps it for a different time, edits `public/privacy/texts.js`
  in the same PR, in the three languages (`texts.test.mjs` checks their shape). A line about a lab game is an item `{ game, t }`: the page shows it only once that game is out for everyone.
- **The footer's sunset reaches the bottom of the screen** on every page: it covers the room `<body>` keeps for a phone's gesture bar (`--safe-bottom`), which used to show as a dark band under it (the owner's report, 2026-11-18).
- **The footer outside the home page:** game covers (the one-player games' too, on their own page, not in La Copa or Uno al día), La Copa's entrance,
  Rankings and Uno al día end with the same violet-sunset footer (`site-footer.js`, U-38); only Rankings
  and Uno al día carry the funny phrase. A screen's own links (La Copa's board: report, leave the cup) are
  `options` of that footer, shown as its tiles, never loose links under the page (`site-footer.test.mjs`).
  Lab games too, from the day they enter `/labs/`: their own 💬 comment is the footer's option on the cover (`footerInto`).
  Never during a game or on a result screen. **Every game is a game** (the owner, 2026-10-27; C-21): no player text or doc calls one a "minigame"; only La Copa's Gran Final plays shortened versions.
- **It is tested whole in Chrome:** `tools/e2e/favorites.mjs` (filters, favorites and 320 px in the
  three languages), `share-home.mjs`, `install-prompt.mjs`, `daily.mjs` and
  `lang-from-url.mjs`. `window.__portada` reports the filter, the visible cards and the
  favorites. A visible change remakes the screenshot `docs/screenshots/menu.png` (C-13).
