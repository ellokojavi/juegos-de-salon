- **🎲 Dudo, in the lab: shake the phone to roll your dice.** With a lab key, setup offers 🫨 Agitar para tirar: each round, while your dice are still to roll, your hand becomes a green cloth with bigger dice, and you shake the phone to roll them. While it shakes, they roll in 3D, like the home page's 🎲 random-game die, spinning faster with every shake while the phone rattles with them, and they keep rolling as long as you shake; when your hand stops they land on what you rolled, and the cloth turns back into your hand with the dice lined up as always. An iPhone asks for the motion permission with one tap; a computer, or a phone without a sensor, gets a 🎲 Tirar los dados button.
- **⏱️ 30 seconds per turn, also in the lab:** whoever doesn't bid, doubt or call it exactly in time loses a die, without the table being revealed, and opens the next round. The clock shows on your own turn and pauses under a reveal, a pass screen or a hidden page (U-20). Both switches are off by default, and in a room the creator's choice applies to everyone.
