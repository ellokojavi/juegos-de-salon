- **🎲 Dudo, in the lab: shake the phone to roll your dice.** With a lab key, setup offers 🫨 Agitar para tirar: each round your dice are rolled in a box of their own, a green cloth over the table with bigger dice, and you shake the phone to roll them. While it shakes, they roll in 3D, like the home page's 🎲 random-game die, spinning faster with every shake while the phone rattles with them; they land on what you rolled and then fly down into your hand, lined up as always. An iPhone asks for the motion permission with one tap; a computer, or a phone without a sensor, gets a 🎲 Tirar los dados button.
- **⏱️ 30 seconds per turn, also in the lab:** whoever doesn't bid, doubt or call it exactly in time loses a die, without the table being revealed, and opens the next round. The clock shows on your own turn and pauses under a reveal, a pass screen or a hidden page (U-20). Both switches are off by default, and in a room the creator's choice applies to everyone.
