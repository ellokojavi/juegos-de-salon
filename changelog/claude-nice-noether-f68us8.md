- 🧪 **Babel with music** (lab, flag `babel-music`): an experiment with background music in Babel, from the
  countdown until 5 s after the game is lost, when it fades out over 2 s. It plays one royalty-free retro track
  (`public/assets/music/babel/track.mp3`, "Retro Game" by The Mountain, 64 kbps mono), looped and low so the game's sounds stay on
  top; the mute button silences it too (`public/assets/js/music.js`).
