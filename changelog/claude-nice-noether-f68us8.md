- 🧪 **Babel with music** (lab, flag `babel-music`): an experiment with background music in Babel, from the
  countdown until the player leaves the result screen. Each game picks one of five royalty-free tracks
  at random (`public/assets/music/babel/`, 64 kbps mono), looped and low so the game's sounds stay on
  top; the mute button silences it too (`public/assets/js/music.js`).
