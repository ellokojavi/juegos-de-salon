- **Artefactos (lab) wears the look of the other games**: the screens follow Tren en Vilo's (the dots and the clock on one line, «Etapa 2 de 5» in yellow under them, Probar above Deshacer · Pista · Rendirse, a white title and Jugar before the rules), and the board is flat neon: yellow beams with pink joints, a green cup, a night sky. The spikes are Aletazo's spinning stars, and whatever the ball touches lights up as hard as it hits: a faint line where it rolls, a flash and a ring on a knock, a few sparks on the hardest blows.
