- **⚙️ Artilugio, a new machine puzzle in the lab's Juegos en prueba** (`/labs/contraption/`), in the genre of The Incredible Machine (Sierra, 1993), credited on the cover; the board, the parts, the levels and the look are our own. Each level drops a ball that must land in the basket 🧺: pick a ramp, plank, trampoline or fan from the tray, tap the board to place it, tap it again to turn, move or remove it, then ▶ Probar to watch the ball run. Six levels that unlock one after another; the fewer parts, the more stars, kept on the phone. In Spanish, English and Portuguese.
