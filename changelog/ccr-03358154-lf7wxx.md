- **⚙️ Artilugio plays as Tren en Vilo does: five stages of growing difficulty against a 6-minute clock** (`/labs/contraption/`). Each stage comes from a harder tier of the 72 generated levels (one ramp, one part among decoys, two parts, three, three among decoys); the first game of the day is the same five for everyone. The clock stops while a stage's summary is open; each stage passed is worth 10, plus up to 5 for using few parts and up to 10 for speed, and the result explains the sum stage by stage. The endless run and 🎲 Otro are gone.
