- **Home page search, in the lab** (D-387). With `?search` (a switch on the lab's home page bar), a box above the filters narrows the list while typing: by a game's name in any language, its tagline, its type or its keywords ("lógica", "dados", "toque", "mapa"). Accents and capitals do not count, it combines with the type filters, and "Ver todos" clears it. Players do not see it yet.
