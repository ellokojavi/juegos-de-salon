- **Labs: Derrape gets new controls** (feel round 1, after the owner's first drive): the car no longer accelerates by itself. The left thumb slides sideways to steer and the right thumb is the pedal (held, it accelerates; slid down, it brakes), both analogue, so a bend can be braked into while turning. A gauge just above each thumb, where the thumb does not cover it, shows the wheel and the pedal, and the car turns its front wheels and lights its brake lights. The 🔧 panel also offers one finger as a stick, and the thumb's travel.
