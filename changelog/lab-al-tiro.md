- **⚡ ¡Al tiro!, microgames in the lab's Juegos en prueba** (`/labs/al-tiro/`): in the style of WarioWare, one quick order and 3 to 5 seconds to do it. Each run is 10 different games drawn from 30 (count, add, pop, sort, cut, follow the rat, balance the blocks…), one after another and a little faster every three. Before starting, it shows the three ways to play (tap, drag, swipe), each with a finger doing it, and every game's intro shows its own. Spanish only for now.
