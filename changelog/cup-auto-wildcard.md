- **La Copa: the ×2 wildcard is no longer lost.** Whoever reaches the Grand Final without having used it gets it on its own on the last day they played before the final (day 6, or an earlier one if they skipped it), marked with a gold **×2 auto**. On day 6 the card and the game's cover warn: "No has usado tu comodín ×2. Se aplicará hoy automáticamente." It also counts in the cups already running; not in the 3-day cup nor in one ended before its final (D-444).
